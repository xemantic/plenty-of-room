## Formulate

### The question

Two claims now carry a headline that sits inside **0.03 nm**:

- `C-0069`'s `Q5` arm places **34 of 34** with **0.0256 nm** of plan budget to spare;
- `C-0066`'s bound 4 clears a **2.69 nm** tie duplex between two consecutive arms by **0.0256 nm**,
  and **fails** at the 2.73 nm square-lattice interhelical distance.

Both are computed on `C-0041`'s and `C-0053`'s hard-body plan model at **nominal** positions.
Nothing in this programme has ever modelled scatter.

### Numeric target and acceptance predicate

**Acceptance.** A tolerance model over the quantities that bind, propagated onto both knife edges
and onto `C-0070`'s seat sensitivity, delivering for each channel **the relative scatter at which
the verdict is lost** — the project's *"the value the unknown would need for the answer to change"*
form — **with its correlation structure named, not a single σ**;
**or** the statement that no defensible tolerance model can be built, with what would supply one.

**Falsifiable predicates.**

| | predicate |
|---|---|
| **P1** | `C-0069`'s arm margin and `C-0066`'s tie clearance are the **same** quantity, `pitch − width − length`, and both reproduce to `< 1e−6` nm |
| **P2** | every channel of the tolerance model reports a **threshold** in relative amplitude, and no channel reports a fabricated distribution |
| **P3** | the propagation is reported for at least **three** correlation structures of the same amplitude, and their coefficients differ by a factor this task measures |
| **P4** | at least one quantity is shown **not** to propagate onto the margin, with its coefficient asserted exactly zero |
| **P5** | a **design with margin** is delivered, or the statement that none exists on this lattice |
| **P6** | the literature is searched for a measured origami fabrication-tolerance distribution, and the query strings are recorded whether or not one is found |

### Units and conventions, locked

- Lengths **nm**, forces **pN**, stiffness **pN/nm**, rotational stiffness **pN·nm/rad**;
  `k_BT = 4.141947 pN·nm` at **300 K**, aqueous **2 mM MgCl₂**.
- `x` runs **along** the host sheet's helices, `y` **across** them, `z` normal and positive upward.
- **The plan margin** is `M = p − d − L`: root pitch minus one duplex width minus the element's
  own plan length. `M > 0` means the element fits under `C-0053`'s footprint convention.
- **A relative scatter** `ε` on a quantity `q` is `δq/q`; a **threshold** `ε*` is the amplitude at
  which the channel's own contribution equals the whole margin.
- **A built length is a whole number of base pairs.** A *solved* length is a real number of nm and
  is what an equation returns; the two are different objects and are carried separately.
- **A sign convention on the correlation**: `COMMON` means the host and the element see the same
  relative rise perturbation; `OPPOSED` means equal and opposite; `INDEPENDENT` means uncorrelated
  and is quoted as an RMS.

### Verification type

**Logical** (exact linear propagation on a margin that is a difference of two integer base-pair
counts, plus four closed-form floors that need no distribution) **+ in-silico** (`C-0039`'s exact
elastica, `C-0053`/`C-0069`'s packer and `C-0009`'s grillage re-run as libraries at a reduced path
count; `C-0070`'s pinned register re-run over a seat sweep).

## Plan

### The cheap bounds, which run before any distribution

Four floors, each of which needs no measured scatter at all:

1. **The design quantum.** DNA quantises every length at the 0.34 nm rise. If the margin is below
   one rise, no design can be *drawn* to hold it.
2. **The disagreement between two measured values of the same lattice constant.** 2.73 − 2.69 nm.
3. **The thermal axial fluctuation of the two segments the margin is a difference of**,
   `√(k_BT ℓ/S)` on the *measured* stretch modulus.
4. **The arm tip's own transverse fluctuation**, whose floor is the arm's own cantilever bending
   `√(k_BT L³/3EI)` and which therefore survives a perfectly rigid root.

**If any of these exceeds the margin, the question is settled without a distribution** and the
expensive part becomes the *threshold* and the *design*, not the *fit*.

### Method justification against cost

The alternative — fitting a distribution — is refused twice over. First, no measured distribution
is known to exist (that is what the literature search is for, and a negative there is a result).
Second, and decisively, if a cheap floor already exceeds the margin then a fitted distribution
could only move the answer if it were *narrower* than that floor, which it cannot be: the floors
are physical, not statistical. So the cost is spent on **propagation coefficients**, which are
exact arithmetic, and on **the design that has margin**, which is one elastica scan and one
grillage solve.

### What would falsify this approach

| # | falsifier |
|---|---|
| **F1** | a channel whose own floor lands **inside** 0.0256 nm — which would make the margin quotable and turn this task into a distribution fit |
| **F2** | the two knife edges turning out to be **different** quantities, so that one scatter model cannot settle both |
| **F3** | the helical twist entering the margin with a non-zero coefficient — the margin is asserted to be twist-free because a crossover pitch is an integer base-pair count |
| **F4** | no path count leaving a margin above the design quantum, so that the branch has no design with margin at all |
| **F5** | a measured origami lattice-scatter distribution existing and being **narrower** than every floor here |

### Deliverables

1. The identity `M = p − d − L` and both claims' 0.0256 nm reproduced through it.
2. The four floors, each as a ratio to the margin.
3. The propagation table: coefficient and threshold per channel and per correlation structure.
4. What does **not** propagate: the twist, and the per-path stiffness scatter of `T-45`.
5. `C-0070`'s seat threshold, by sweep.
6. The design with margin, priced in path count, per-path force and flatness.
7. The literature, with its query strings.
