## Plan

statement by statement, keeping superseded readings struck rather than deleted.

**Justification against cost.** The deliverable is the product. A reader of §4(g) currently learns that the
four-layer verdict is conditional on an unlocated threshold, and would not learn that the threshold is located,
that a better cross-section exists, or that a coupled tile is now flat — which are the three things that most

checkers, as `C-0115`'s was. It is not: this range is mostly favourable movement, and saying so is part of the
result — five consecutive passes of corrections is not the base rate.
