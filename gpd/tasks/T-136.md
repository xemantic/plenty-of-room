# T-136 — Is there a FLAT 30-root placement?

**Leaf** `A8.2` (*"identify the dominant compliance term … and budget stiffness at the joints"*),
with `A1.2` for the anchoring array the roots belong to.

**Raised by** [`C-0072`](../claims/C-0072-plan-tolerance-model.md)'s Deliverable 6, which found that
dropping four of `C-0063`'s 34 upward arm roots takes the plan margin from **0.0256 nm to 1.3495 nm
(53×)** and, in the same table, takes the tile's dishing from **0.0706 to 0.2603** of the free-tile
stroke — outside `T-5b`'s 0.10.
`C-0072` states plainly that its own reduction is a **plan rule** (drop the interior root of every
row of three) and **not** a flatness optimisation, so 0.2603 is an **upper** bound.

**Read together with [`T-138`](T-138.md).**
`C-0017`'s mandate is a stiffness on a **sum**, so a path count sizes the element *and* counts the
instances. Dropping to 30 roots is dropping to 30 paths: the array recounts and the element
re-sizes at the same time. The two tasks are one question and are answered in that coupled form —
`T-138` supplies the self-consistent bookkeeping, this task supplies the placement.

---

## Formulate

### The question, as a predicate

> **P1 (flatness).** Does some placement of **30** upward arm roots on the Gen-1 sheet's own
> upward lattice, carrying `C-0017`'s **33.3333 pN/nm** as a sum in **equal** springs, dish less
> than **0.10** of the free-tile stroke under `C-0022`'s solved edge load —
> **at the design state**, and **over the whole range** the placed 2 mM / 10 nm / 0.192 V device
> traverses (`C-0068`'s range: gaps 10 → 7 nm at its own bias)?

> **P2 (margin).** Does that placement keep the plan margin `C-0072` bought — i.e. does the
> **re-sized 30-path arm** still place 30 times, and what is `ceiling − arm` on **that** placement
> rather than on `C-0072`'s plan-rule reduction?

> **P3 (the trade).** Are P1 and P2 satisfiable **together**, or is the branch forced to choose
> between a quotable margin and a flat tile?

### Declared falsifier

**No two-per-row placement clears 0.10**, at either objective and at any lattice phase.
Then `C-0072`'s trade is real and irreducible, and the branch must choose. This is to be **said
plainly** if it is the answer; a negative result here is as valuable as a positive one, because it
converts *"the margin is unquotable"* into *"the margin and the flatness are one budget"*.

### Locked units, geometry and sign conventions — restated, not inherited

- Lengths **nm**, forces **pN**, stiffness **pN/nm**, pressure **pN/nm²** (= 1 MPa exactly);
  `k_BT = 4.141947 pN·nm` at **300 K** in aqueous **2 mM MgCl₂**.
- `x` runs **along** the helices, `y` **across** them, `z` **normal and positive upward** — away
  from the grafted layer, which lies below the tile. `w` is positive **downward**. Origin at the
  tile centre.
- **Dishing** is the peak absolute departure from the area-weighted best-fit plane on the same
  **81 × 81** grid as `C-0026`, `C-0047`, `C-0058`, `C-0063` and `C-0064`, quoted over `C-0006`'s
  free-tile stroke.
- **A root** is the crossover that ties one arm to its host duplex, on the unoccupied **`EAST`**
  (upward) azimuth; the coupling enters the sheet there.
- **A rooted element occupies `[root, root ± L]` and the next along the same row may start at
  `high + d`**, `d = 2.69 nm` — `C-0053`'s footprint convention, carried unchanged through
  `armDirections`. It is the convention that turns the 10.88 nm root pitch into a length ceiling.
- **A state** is a `(concentration, gap, bias)` triple of `C-0022`'s solved edge profiles; an
  **operating range** is the set of states **one device** traverses (`CH-0077`, `C-0068`).

## Plan

### The cheap bounds, which run before any solve

1. **The count vector at 30 is forced and carries no freedom at all.**
   `C-0063`'s bound 1 is `3a + 2(15 − a) = 34` → four rows of three. At **30** roots on 15 rows with
   at most two per row the same arithmetic gives `2 × 15 = 30` **exactly**: every row carries
   exactly two, and the "two-per-row constraint" is an **identity**, not a constraint. The design
   space is therefore a product of per-row 2-subsets and nothing else. One line of arithmetic, and
   it fixes the shape of the whole search before it is run.

2. **The largest plan ceiling any placement at a given count admits, in closed form.**
   A placement's ceiling is a **min over rows**, and rows are independent, so
   `max over placements of min over rows` is exactly `sup{L : Σ_r min(m, maxRootedElementsInRow(r, L)) ≥ n}`
   — a bisection on a monotone capacity, using `C-0069`'s own `maximumRootedElementsInRow`.
   This is a **proof**, not a search: it says what any 30-root placement can possibly keep, and it
   is what `C-0072`'s 9.12 nm must be checked against.

3. **The reachable dishing floor over EVERY candidate root of a phase.**
   `MultiStateSurrogate.reachableDishingFloor` is a rigorous lower bound on the peak dishing of any
   force vector; evaluated on the **full** site set of a phase it is a lower bound on **every**
   30-root subset of it, with any distribution. A floor above 0.10 would fire the falsifier before
   a single placement is enumerated.

### The method, and why this one

- **The enumeration is small, and that is the whole cost argument.** With the count vector forced,
  each row offers at most `C(4, 2) = 6` feasible 2-subsets, so the centro-symmetric family
  (`C-0063`'s congruence admits exactly **two** phases, 8 and 24) is `6^4 · 3^3 = 34 992` at phase
  24 and `3^4 · 6^3 × 2 = 34 992` at phase 8 — an order of magnitude **smaller** than `C-0063`'s
  361 584, because forbidding a row of three removes most of the family. It is **exhaustive**.
- **The non-symmetric family is `~3.7e9` and is not enumerable**, so it is searched by
  `C-0063`'s own `descendPlacement` with `minimumPerRow = maximumPerRow = 2`, from a greedy start
  and a deterministic ensemble of pseudo-random starts, **at every one of the 32 phases**. A
  descent reports what it found; the floor of bound 3 is what says how much room may be left.
- **Superposition makes it affordable.** `MultiStateRootBank` factorises the host **once** per
  phase and slices a `MultiStateSurrogate` per candidate, so a 30-root placement costs a 30 × 30
  Cholesky rather than an 855-DOF solve (`C-0058`, `C-0063`, `C-0068`). The slice is exact, and the
  gate that says so is a comparison against an assembled `OrigamiGrillage` solve at the same
  stations.
- **Both objectives, in one enumeration**: the single design state (comparable with `C-0063`'s
  0.0706 and `C-0072`'s 0.2603) and the worst over the design device's traversed range (comparable
  with `C-0068`'s 0.0789). `C-0068` established that a flatness verdict at one state does not
  travel, so a single-state answer here would not be an answer.
- **The plan margin is carried beside the flatness for every candidate**, so the output is a
  **Pareto front** and P3 is answered by inspection rather than by argument.

### What would falsify this approach

- **The sliced surrogate disagreeing with an assembled solve** at the same stations. Then the whole
  sweep is on the wrong object and no number in it means anything. Asserted as a gate-3 test at
  departure `0.0`.
- **A uniform load on a uniform Winkler foundation dishing anything but zero** on the free tile.
  `CLAUDE.md`'s standing falsifier; asserted as a test.
- **`C-0063`'s 0.0706, `C-0068`'s 0.0789 and `C-0072`'s 0.2603 failing to reproduce** on the same
  pipeline. Then the comparison this task exists to make is not a comparison.
- **The capacity bisection of bound 2 disagreeing with an exhaustive maximum** over the enumerated
  family. Then the closed form is not a bound and the search has no proof beside it.
- **The descent beating the exhaustive enumeration at a centro-symmetric phase.** That would mean
  the exhaustive family is not what it claims to be — it is checked, and the reverse (the descent
  losing) is expected and is a measurement of how much the symmetry restriction costs.

### Cost

One `MultiStateRootBank` per phase (≈ 52 unit-load solves on a factorised host), ≈ 70 000
exhaustive candidates at the two symmetric phases and ≈ 10⁴ descent evaluations per phase over 32
phases. Minutes, not hours — against a re-run of `C-0063`'s full 1.1 million-placement sweep,
which is not needed because the forced count vector shrinks the family.

## Verification gates

| gate | what will be checked |
|---|---|
| **1 — dimensional** | dishing over a stroke is dimensionless and invariant under scaling every length; the worst dishing is exactly **linear** in the applied pressure; a plan ceiling is a length and scales with the tile; unphysical arguments throw |
| **2 — limiting cases** | one root per row has ceiling `20 + max\|x\|` and 15 roots place at any length below it; a row of three caps at exactly `pitch − d`; at 30 roots the two-per-row family is the **whole** family; an equal-spring coupling at zero total stiffness reproduces the free tile |
| **3 — symmetry and conservation** | a **uniform** load on a free tile dishes **exactly zero**; the sliced surrogate equals an assembled `OrigamiGrillage` solve at departure `0.0`; the enumerated family is closed under `(x, y) → (−x, −y)`; the capacity bisection equals the exhaustive maximum |
| **4 — numerical convergence** | nested subdivisions 1 / 2 / 4; the 81 × 81 dishing grid against 41 and 161; the ceiling bisection over resolutions `1e−6 … 1e−12`; the descent's own reproducibility on repeat |
| **5 — literature and upstream** | `C-0063`'s 0.0706, `C-0068`'s 0.0789, `C-0072`'s 0.26028 and 1.3495 nm, `C-0069`'s 8.19 nm and `C-0055`'s 10.88 nm pitch, all re-derived on this pipeline |

## Acceptance

**PASS** if P1, P2 and P3 are answered with a number and a named falsifier verdict — in either
direction. A negative on P1 is an acceptance, provided it is stated plainly and the family it was
searched in is named (`CLAUDE.md`: *"a saturation measured inside one family is not a floor of the
object"*).
