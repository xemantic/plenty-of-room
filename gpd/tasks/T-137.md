# T-137 — the plan model against the MEASURED weave

**Leaf** `A8.2` (*"identify the dominant compliance term … and budget stiffness at the joints"*),
with `A1.2` for the anchoring array the plan model belongs to.
**Raised by** [`C-0072`](../claims/C-0072-plan-tolerance-model.md), *Still open* item 7.
**Reserved IDs**: claim `C-0076`, challenges `CH-0088`, `CH-0089`.

## Formulate

### The finding that raised it

`C-0072`'s literature survey found that the interhelical distance in a lattice origami is not a
constant but a **deterministic sawtooth** — 18.5 Å at a crossover to 36 Å midway
(Bai et al., *PNAS* **109**:20012, 2012; confirmed by all-atom MD and by oxDNA **on a 2D tile**).
So the **2.69 nm** that `C-0041`, `C-0053`, `C-0065`, `C-0066` and `C-0069` all use as a *steric
exclusion width* is a **Bragg lattice constant** — a mean — and not a local separation.

`C-0072` read that sawtooth through its own plan-margin identity `M = p − d − L` and reported

| `d` | `M` | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 1.85 nm — the weave minimum | **+0.866 nm** | comfortable |
| 2.69 nm — the lattice constant | +0.0256 nm | the knife edge |
| 3.60 nm — the weave maximum | **−0.884 nm** | does not place |

and left the re-run to this task.
It also left an unpriced contradiction: the weave **minimum, 1.85 nm, is inside the 2.0 nm steric
diameter** `CLAUDE.md` records as the duplex's own surface.

### The numeric targets

1. **The plan margin at each of `C-0063`'s 34 stations under a position-dependent exclusion width**,
   against the standing `M = 0.02560917 nm` at the single 2.69 nm width.
2. **The placed count** of `C-0069`'s 34-arm array and `C-0053`'s 45-arm array under the same
   width, against their standing 34 and 43.
3. **An adjudication of 1.85 nm against 2.0 nm**, resting on a primary source rather than on the
   inherited round number.

### The acceptance predicate (falsifiable)

`C-0041`/`C-0053`'s hard-body packer re-run with a **position-dependent** exclusion width, with the
placed count and the plan margin reported at every one of `C-0063`'s 34 stations —
**or** the statement that a single width is defensible, with the quantity it is a width *of* named
and the weave's coefficient on the margin computed rather than asserted.

### Verification type

**Logical** (the weave's phase is an integer lattice coordinate, so the value at a station is a
closed form and the coefficient is exact arithmetic) **+ in-silico** (`C-0053`'s
`placeHingeArms`/`maximumArmsInRow`, `C-0063`'s `armDirections`/`upwardHingeSites` and
`C-0069`'s `rootedLengthCeiling`/`placeRootedOutputElement` re-run as libraries at a
position-dependent width) **+ literature** (the weave's shape, phase, amplitude and mean read
directly from the primary sources, each flagged).

### Locked units, geometry and sign conventions

- Lengths **nm**, forces **pN**, stiffness **pN/nm**; `k_BT = 4.141947 pN·nm` at **300 K** in
  aqueous **2 mM MgCl₂**. The oxDNA and cryo-EM sources are at their own ionic strengths and that
  is carried as a validity note, not silently transferred.
- `x` runs **along** the host sheet's helices, `y` **across** them in the sheet plane, `z` normal
  and positive **upward** (away from the grafted layer). Origin at the tile centre.
- **A plane index `k`** is the 8 bp crossover-plane index of `C-0055`'s lattice,
  `x = phase + k · 8 · rise`. Duplex `b`'s azimuth at plane `k` is entry `(k − 2b) mod 4` of
  `NORTH, WEST, SOUTH, EAST` — `C-0055`'s rule, not restated here but consumed.
- **Interface `b`** is the interface between duplex `b` and duplex `b+1`.
- **The weave** `D_b(k)` is the distance between the **helix axes** (the base-pair midpoints) of
  duplexes `b` and `b+1` at plane `k` — the definition both primary sources use verbatim.
- **A duplex's steric diameter** is twice its phosphate radius, and the phosphate backbone **is**
  the surface (`CLAUDE.md`). Three readings are carried: the round 2.0 nm this project asserts,
  the fibre 1.78–1.88 nm bracket, and `T-71`'s **measured** 13 084-linkage value.
- **The plan margin** is `M = p − d − L`, `p` the root pitch **along x**, `L` the element's plan
  length **along x**, `d` the clearance charged **along x** between one element's tip and the next
  element's root. This is `C-0072`'s identity verbatim.

## Plan

### The cheap bound runs first, and it is a lattice congruence

Three cheap bounds, all closed form, all before any packer:

1. **Which axis is which.** The weave is a separation measured **across** the helices between two
   duplexes that are **covalently linked** by the crossover at the weave's own minimum. The `d` in
   `M = p − d − L` is a clearance charged **along** the helices between two **unbonded** bodies.
   Whether the weave enters `M` at all is therefore a question about the *slot*, not about the
   *number*, and it is answered by writing the three roles the symbol `d` plays in the plan model
   down beside each other. Cost: nothing.
2. **The weave's phase at a station.** `C-0065` established that every one of `C-0063`'s 34
   stations is the same helical phase of its own host duplex. The weave's extrema sit at the
   crossover planes; the stations sit at the **upward** planes. If those two sets are disjoint in a
   fixed way, the weave value at a station is quantised and the count is again **0 or 34**. This is
   one modular-arithmetic statement and it is checkable over all 32 phases × 15 rows in
   milliseconds.
3. **The steric floor.** `T-71` measured the B-DNA phosphate radius on 13 084 crystallographic
   linkages and the constant is already in this repository. Doubling it decides the
   1.85-versus-2.0 contradiction without a new source. Cost: one multiplication.

**Justification against cost.** The expensive object here would be a re-derivation of the weave
itself — an oxDNA or all-atom simulation of a 15-duplex single-layer sheet — which is days of
compute to reproduce a published measurement that already exists for *this exact object* (Snodin
et al.'s 2D tile). It is refused on `CLAUDE.md`'s *"prefer published measurement on the actual
material over simulating it"*. What is cheap and has never been done is to ask **which of the plan
model's three uses of one symbol the measurement is a measurement of**, and to evaluate the weave
at the lattice coordinates the design already occupies. If bounds 1 and 2 both return zero
coefficients the packer re-run is a confirmation and is run as one, at ~seconds, rather than as the
instrument that decides.

### The packer re-run, if the cheap bound leaves anything

A position-dependent width enters the existing pipelines in exactly two places — `armDirections`'
frontier (`high + width`) and `placeRootedOutputElement`'s row-independence test — so it is added
as a **new function in a new file** taking a `widthAt(x)` function, required to reproduce
`armDirections` bit-for-bit when the function is constant (a gate-2 test, departure `0.0`). No
shared main source is edited; two other agents are in this checkout.

### What would falsify this approach

**F1 (declared falsifier).** *The weave's coefficient on the plan margin `M` is non-zero* — i.e.
the along-x clearance and the across-helix lattice separation turn out to be the same quantity
after all, so `C-0072`'s +0.866 / −0.884 bracket stands and the placement verdict really is
undecided between comfortable and impossible. Then this task must deliver a distribution over the
placed count rather than a coefficient, and the recommendation of `C-0071` loses its plan margin
outright.

**F2.** *The stations do not sit at a fixed weave phase* — if the upward planes and the crossover
planes are not disjoint in a `b`-independent way, the weave value is a distribution over the 34
stations and deliverable 2's *"0 or 34"* shape does not exist.

**F3.** *The measured phosphate radius does not admit 18.5 Å* — if `T-71`'s measured diameter
exceeds 1.85 nm, then the weave minimum genuinely interpenetrates the duplex surface and the
contradiction is real, which would be a statement about what a crossover is and would have to be
carried as an open item rather than adjudicated.

**F4.** *A position-dependent width changes a placed count.* Then a single width is not defensible
and every one of the five inheriting claims must be re-read at the position-dependent one.

**F5.** *The weave's mean is not the lattice constant.* The plan model would then be wrong about
its own central value as well as about its variation, and the correction would be a shift rather
than a spread.
