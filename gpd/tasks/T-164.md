## Formulate

### The question

`C-0095` settled that a crossover **exists** at the last base pair of a duplex:
it is the raster turn, caDNAno automates it, and Rothemund's 24-helix rectangle is
288 bp = 18 column pitches exactly, both edges on the crossover lattice, 90 % well-formed.
So `C-0090`'s two *"conventions"* are not a choice between a right and a wrong model.
`CH-0111` reframes them as the two ends of a **stiffness bracket** on **14 of the 56** crossovers
of the recommended tile — admitted with an interior crossover's `k_θ` and vertical link at one end,
absent entirely at the other:

| the two end columns | best 34-root dishing / stroke, 38.08 nm, phase 8 | inside `T-5b`'s 0.10? |
|---|---|---|
| full interior `k_θ` and vertical link (**admitted**) | **0.0621469105** | **yes** |
| a soft or distorted row-end crossover | **not computed** | **not known** |
| no crossover at all (**refused**) | **0.168371808** | **no** |

`T-5b`'s 0.10 sits `(0.10 − 0.0621469105)/(0.168371808 − 0.0621469105) = 0.356`
of the way across that bracket, **so the acceptance verdict changes inside it**,
and **monotonicity is unshown**.

And Rothemund states in print that at an edge on the crossover lattice
*"a crossover involving staple strands is in tension with an adjacent crossover involving the scaffold strand"*,
that *"such a configuration of crossovers in tension has never before been used in DNA nanostructures"*,
and that *"how the strain is actually relieved is unknown, the final base pairs of each helix may be distorted"*.

### Numeric target and acceptance predicate

> The best 34-root dishing at 38.08 nm / phase 8
> with the two row-end columns' `k_θ` swept from 0 to an interior crossover's value —
> or the statement that the response is not monotone in it.

**Acceptance** (falsifiable, each a predicate in the result file):

| # | predicate |
|---|---|
| **P1** | a **cheap bound** on the row-end crossover's stiffness is stated **before** the sweep, from this project's own constants, and it says what the one-sided material costs |
| **P2** | the sweep is run on `C-0090`'s own pipeline at 38.08 nm / phase 8 over the **exhaustive** centro-symmetric 34-root family at every stiffness value, and **monotonicity is measured, not assumed** |
| **P3** | if the sweep is monotone, the **crossing** — the fraction of an interior crossover's stiffness the 38.08 nm tile needs to clear `T-5b`'s 0.10 — is located and compared against the cheap bound |
| **P4** | the consequence for `C-0090`'s and `C-0095`'s verdicts and for the recommendation to carry the admitted reading is stated; a moved verdict is filed as a challenge, not an overwrite |
| **P5** | where no accessible source gives the stiffness, that is said plainly and the **ceiling plus threshold** is delivered instead (`P-6`), with the measurement that would falsify either half named |

### Locked units, geometry and sign conventions

- Lengths **nm**, forces **pN**, stiffness **pN/nm**, rotational stiffness **pN·nm/rad**,
  pressure **pN/nm²** (= 1 MPa exactly), `k_BT = 4.141947 pN·nm` at **T = 300 K**, aqueous **2 mM MgCl₂**.
- `x` runs **along** the helices, `y` **across** them, `z` normal and positive **upward**; `w` positive **downward**.
- **A row** is one duplex; **a column** is the sheet's own 16 bp column lattice;
  **an interface** is the boundary between adjacent duplexes, `b = 0 … D−2`;
  **the row end** is `x = ±edgeX/2`, the last base pair.
- **A crossover is TWO elements** (`C-0009`, `CLAUDE.md`): a dihedral spring `k_θ` resisting the
  relative roll of the two duplexes, and a **vertical link** tying their surfaces together,
  which is a *constraint* carried by a penalty stiffness and contributes no rigidity of its own.
- **`s`** is the dimensionless **row-end softening**, `k_θ,row-end / k_θ,interior`, on the crossovers
  of the two end columns only. **Channel A** scales the dihedral spring alone and retains the link;
  **channel B** scales both, and is the channel whose `s → 0` limit approaches the refused reading.
- Row length **112 bp = 38.08 nm** (`C-0086`), 24-rise **8.16 nm** arm (`C-0090`), **34** roots
  (`C-0055`), phase **8**, 15 duplexes at the SAXS 2.69 nm, rise 0.34 nm, `C-0017`'s 33.3333 pN/nm
  mandate shared equally, `C-0022`'s solved collar at 2 mM / 10 nm / 0.192 V **as `C-0090` carried it**.

### Verification type

**logical** (a covalent count and a constraint/elasticity distinction, both closed form, which
bound the reachable set before any solve)
**+ in-silico** (`C-0090`'s exhaustive centro-symmetric placement pipeline re-run at 38.08 nm /
phase 8 at every value of `s`, through `C-0058`'s Woodbury bank).
**Nothing here is measured**; TRL 1–3 throughout.

## Plan

### The cheap bound runs first, and it is a count and a distinction — not a solve

1. **A crossover is TWO elements and only one of them is elastic.**
   `C-0090`'s *refused* reading deletes the dihedral spring, the vertical link **and** the node.
   But `C-0095` has settled that the crossover **exists** — the backbone is covalently continuous
   through it — so the vertical link, which is a *constraint* and not a stiffness, is present at
   full value whatever the strain relief does to the torsional register.
   Rothemund's own remedy (*"one or two scaffold bases could be left unpaired and allowed to form a
   hairpin"*) adds slack to the **torsion**, not to the connectivity.
   **Prediction to be tested: the physically reachable set is channel A, and the refused reading
   is outside it** — i.e. `CH-0111`'s bracket has an unreachable lower end.
2. **A ceiling on `s` that is a count, not a model.**
   `Gen1Tile.crossoverHingeStiffness(α) = 2 α B/(100 a)` is **two** softened phosphate bonds in
   parallel (Chen et al. 2014). `C-0029`'s counting theorem: *a duplex END has exactly two strand
   termini*, and no force field can add a third — so a row-end crossover cannot carry **more** bonds
   than an interior one, and `s ≤ 1` exactly. The only other integer state of the same ladder is a
   single-strand turn, `s = 1/2`.
3. **An unrelieved strain is a PRESTRAIN, not a compliance.**
   Rothemund's passage is about a static configuration, not about a linear elastic constant, so it
   does not by itself move `k_θ`. That is why the honest deliverable is `P-6`'s **ceiling plus
   threshold** and not a value.

**Justification against cost.** Steps 1–3 cost no solve at all and they decide the shape of the
answer: if the crossing lies below the counting floor the verdict is safe and the expensive route
(an oxDNA or all-atom edge crossover, which is the only way to answer Rothemund's own question) is
not worth spending. `CH-0111` itself ranks the sweep first and the simulation third.

### The sweep, and why it is affordable

`C-0058`'s `InfluenceSurrogate` bank slices per placement, so one value of `s` costs **one**
factorisation of the 855-DOF host plus one back-substitution per candidate station; the exhaustive
centro-symmetric family at phase 8 (163 296 placements) is then a 34 × 34 Cholesky each.
That is `C-0090`'s own pipeline with **one changed parameter**, and it is ~1 min per value of `s`.

- **Grid**: `s ∈ {0, 0.125, 0.25, 0.375, 0.5, 0.625, 0.75, 0.875, 1}` on both channels,
  the exhaustive family at each.
- **Monotonicity**: the sign of every consecutive difference, reported, **not assumed** —
  `CLAUDE.md` records that a verdict that is not monotone in a swept variable has no threshold and
  that sweeping it finer finds more alternation (`C-0070`'s lateral seat).
- **Crossing**: bisected only where the grid shows a sign change against `T-5b`'s 0.10, on the
  **exhaustive best** at each bisection step, six steps.
- **The best placement key** is carried at every `s`, because a design that changes with the
  parameter is not one design.

### The five verification gates

| gate | what it will check |
|---|---|
| **1 — dimensional** | a softening factor is dimensionless and in `[0, 1]`; unphysical arguments throw at every entry point; `k_θ` stays in `pN·nm/rad` |
| **2 — limiting cases** | an empty softening map is bit-identical to the unmodified lattice; **`s = 0` on BOTH elements is bit-identical to `consumedCrossovers` at the same sites**, which is an independent code path; the row-end column set is exactly the two end columns and carries exactly 14 crossovers |
| **3 — symmetry and conservation** | **a uniform load on a uniform foundation dishes exactly zero at every `s`**; the row-end crossover set is centro-symmetric; the Woodbury surrogate reproduces the assembled solve |
| **4 — convergence** | nested subdivisions 1 ⊂ 2 ⊂ 4 and the dishing sample grid 41/81/161 at the endpoints and at the crossing |
| **5 — literature and upstream** | at `s = 1` reproduce `C-0090`'s **0.0621469105**; the refused pipeline reproduce **0.168371808**; the counting ceiling reproduce `Gen1Tile.crossoverHingeStiffness` |

### The declared falsifier

> **F1 (the declared falsifier).** **The best 34-root dishing is NOT monotone in `s`** —
> some consecutive pair of the nine grid values moves the wrong way by more than the study's own
> reproducibility floor. Then there is no threshold to quote, `CH-0111`'s bracket cannot be read as
> an interval, and the deliverable becomes the alternation itself.

Beside it, five more, all recorded in the result file:

| # | falsifier | what it would mean |
|---|---|---|
| **F2** | at `s = 1` the pipeline does not reproduce `C-0090`'s 0.0621469105 | the sweep is not on `C-0090`'s object and nothing here transfers |
| **F3** | channel B's `s → 0` limit differs materially from the refused reading 0.168371808 | the two lattices are not the same mechanics, and the difference is a mesh artefact that must be measured rather than assumed away |
| **F4** | channel A's crossing lies **above** the counting floor `s = 1/2` | the verdict is genuinely at risk on the physics, and the expensive simulation becomes worth spending |
| **F5** | the best placement **key** moves across the sweep | the recommended design is a function of an unmeasured parameter, which is a separate and worse exposure than the dishing value |
| **F6** | a uniform load on a uniform foundation dishes more than `1e−6` of the free stroke at some `s` | the softened lattice is wrong, not the physics (`CLAUDE.md`'s standing falsifier) |

### What would falsify the whole approach

That the row-end crossover's mechanics is **not** a `k_θ` at all — that the strain Rothemund
describes is relieved by a *displacement* (a distorted terminal base pair, a frayed end) which the
grillage represents with the vertical link rather than the dihedral spring. Channel B is carried
precisely so that this reading has a number too, and the two channels bracket it.
