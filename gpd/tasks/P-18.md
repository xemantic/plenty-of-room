## The problem, restated

The tree rounds every emitted `Double` at the serialisation boundary to **nine significant digits**
with an absolute floor at `1e-9`. `SelfConsistentFieldLayer.heightAtPressure` closes its bracket at
`HEIGHT_TOLERANCE = 1e-6`, *relative*. So anything downstream of a solved height is **printed three
digits past what it is pinned to**.

The consequence `CH-0043` established is not cosmetic. `gpd/README.md`'s certificate — "a re-run that
changes nothing produces no diff" — then measures whether the **code path** changed, not whether the
**answer** changed. `P-15` paid the bill: a correct Illinois takes a shorter path to the same root,
589 fields of `T-1f` moved by a median `9.0e−7` — *exactly* `HEIGHT_TOLERANCE` — no verdict changed,
and every one of them had to be adjudicated by hand.

It has since fired in new places, and not only through the SCF:
`C-0064`'s optimiser produced files differing in **1 599 lines** between runs, reduced to **28** by
deciding at six significant digits rather than nine; `C-0068` reports the same shape at 28 of 1 423;
`C-0066` had to delete a `runtimeSeconds` field to get byte-identical re-emission.

## Numeric target and acceptance predicate

**Acceptance.** The rounding is carried **down** to the determined precision, **or**
`HEIGHT_TOLERANCE` is carried **up** to the printed one; every result file is re-emitted and diffed;
which files move, by how much, and whether any verdict changes is reported. A moved verdict is a
challenge against the owning claim, not a silent edit.

**Falsifiable predicates.**

1. **P1 — the determined precision is measured, not guessed.** For each emitted quantity family
   downstream of a solved height, a *relative movement* under a legitimate change of the solve path
   is reported, and the digit count is `floor(−log₁₀ movement)` of it. If the movements of two
   families differ by more than one decade, a single tree-wide digit count is **refuted** and a
   per-provenance (or per-key) rule is required.
2. **P2 — the cost of the honest direction is priced.** The wall clock and SCF-solve count of the
   same pipeline at `HEIGHT_TOLERANCE ∈ {1e−4 … 1e−8}` is reported. If tightening to `1e−9` costs
   less than ~2× and the quantities then move by less than `1e−9`, direction (b) is viable and must
   be taken.
3. **P3 — the alternative is checked for reachability, not assumed.** If the residual function
   `P(h)` carries its own irreducible noise `η` (path-dependent warm start, cached solves,
   grid-count `M = round(h/Δz)` stepping), then the root is determined to no better than `η/|d ln P/d ln h|`
   **whatever tolerance is asked for**, and asking for less is `CLAUDE.md`'s already-recorded trap —
   *"an unreachable convergence tolerance is silent"*.
4. **P4 — the argmin rule holds.** Rounding at the serialisation boundary does not make a file
   reproducible if it contains an argmin: an index is not a rounded double. Any decision taken
   inside a study whose emission precision changes must be taken at the **same** precision.
5. **P5 — every result file is accounted for.** Either re-emitted and diffed, or shown by
   construction to be unable to move (its emitter's rounding is untouched), with the construction
   argument falsified by a re-emitted sample.

## Conventions, fixed before deriving

- **Determined precision** of a quantity `Q` is the number of significant digits invariant under any
  change that respects the solver's own declared tolerances. It is `floor(−log₁₀ m)` where `m` is
  the largest relative movement of `Q` observed under such a change, clamped to `[1, 9]`.
- **Provenance** of an emitted number is the loosest solver tolerance on any path from a model input
  to it. Nine digits is defensible only where that tolerance is at or below `1e−9`.
- A **relative movement** is `|Q − Q_ref| / |Q_ref|`, and where `Q_ref` is meant to be zero it is
  compared **absolutely** — this project's standing rule.

## Plan

### The cheap bound, which runs first

Before re-running anything, two static facts are established by reading the tree, and either of them
alone decides the shape of the answer:

- **How many independent rounding constants exist.** If the "tree-wide nine digits" is in fact several
  package-local copies, then a per-provenance rule needs no new mechanism — the mechanism is already
  there and merely uncalibrated — and a change to one of them silently leaves the others.
- **Which studies can move at all.** `roundedForResult` is called at exactly one place per study. A
  study whose call is untouched **cannot** emit a different byte, whatever the model does. That
  converts "re-emit 65 files" into "re-emit the files whose emitter changed, plus a sample of the
  others as a falsifier".

**Stated falsifier for the cheap bound:** re-emit a sample of studies whose emitter was *not* touched.
If any of them moves, the construction argument is wrong and the full re-emission is owed.

### The measurement

One representative Gen-1 design point (`C-0001`'s 10 nm, `σ = 0.0240 nm⁻²`, des Cloizeaux
interaction, the production grid `Δz = 0.2 nm`), carried through the **same** pipeline `T-1f` uses —
`chainLengthAtRestingHeight` → `equilibriumHeight` → pressure, stiffness, `heightAtPressure` at §3's
100 pN, stroke, secant stiffness — at a ladder of height tolerances, plus two probes of the residual
function itself:

- **the path-noise probe**: the same quantity from a *fresh* layer and from one whose cache and warm
  field carry a different history. This is the movement `P-15` measured, isolated;
- **the grid-count probe**: `M = round(h/Δz)` is a **step** function of `h`, so `pressureAt` is
  piecewise in `h`. The jump across an `M` boundary is a floor on the root's determinacy that no
  tolerance passes.

**Why this method and not the obvious one.** The obvious method is to re-run `T-1d` and `T-1f` at a
tighter tolerance and diff — 33 minutes plus the whole re-adjudication `CH-0043` exists to describe.
One design point costs seconds and answers P1, P2 and P3 together. `CLAUDE.md` additionally warns
that *the cheapest place to evaluate an SCF layer is never its own floor*, so every probe stays at
`0.7–1.0 L₀` and none is taken near the saturation height.

**What would falsify this approach.** If the path-noise probe returns **zero** movement at every
quantity, then the emitted numbers are functions of the code path alone, the notion of "determined
precision" has no measurable content here, and the task reduces to the cost question P2 by itself.
If the ladder shows the quantities still moving by `~1e−6` at `HEIGHT_TOLERANCE = 1e−8`, the height
tolerance is **not** what limits them and `CH-0043`'s diagnosis — not just its recommendation — is
wrong.

### The change

Whichever direction the measurement selects, the rounding gains a *declared* precision rather than a
constant one, because P1's refutation condition is expected to fire: a stiffness read as a second
difference of free energies cannot be determined to the same digit as the height underneath it.

### Verification gates

| gate | how |
|---|---|
| **1. Dimensional** | a digit count and a relative movement are both dimensionless; rounding is scale-free — `round(10ˣ·v)` at `d` digits equals `10ˣ·round(v)` for integer `x` |
| **2. Limiting cases** | `d = 17` is the identity on a binary64; `d = 1` keeps one digit; integers, booleans and strings pass through; a zero rounds to zero at every `d` |
| **3. Symmetry / conservation** | rounding is odd in the sign of its argument at every digit count; a per-key override applies to the whole subtree under that key and to nothing else |
| **4. Numerical convergence** | the tolerance ladder itself — the movement of each quantity against the tightest tolerance, exhibited rather than asserted |
| **5. Literature cross-check** | none applies: this is a statement about this repository's own solvers, and it is checked against their **declared** tolerances, which is the only external reference there is |
