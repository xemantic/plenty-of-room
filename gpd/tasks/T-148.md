## Formulate

### The question

`CH-0084` translates Strauss et al.'s measured staple incorporation into a **43.6 %** relative
per-path stiffness scatter and grades it against `C-0060`'s **34.6 %** flatness threshold, a
**1.26×** fail — and then says, in its own *"honest qualifications"*, that the grade is
**indicative**:

> *"This is a translation, not an equivalence. A Bernoulli dropout and `C-0060`'s alternating
> scatter pattern have the same relative standard deviation and **different spatial structure**,
> and `C-0060` measures the pattern at **2.21×**."*

`CLAUDE.md` records this class of error in as many words — *"which way a tolerance is correlated
matters more than how big it is"* — and `C-0026`'s own alternating-scatter rule restores
**exactly zero** crossover force along the helices and 0.088 pN across them, at the same
amplitude. A dropout has no such symmetry, and the standard deviation is therefore not the
statement.

**So: re-run the flatness pipeline with staple incorporation as a Bernoulli dropout whose
probability is position-dependent as Strauss measures it, and grade the result against `T-5b`'s
0.10 — or state that the model cannot be made position-dependent from what is published.**

### The numeric target

The **peak dishing over the free-tile stroke**, as a **distribution** over dropout realisations,
against `T-5b`'s **0.10** convention, on each of the placements that carry a standing flatness
verdict.

### The acceptance predicate, falsifiable

1. **`P1`** — the mean-dropout arithmetic on `C-0017`'s mandate is reproduced **without any
   spatial model**, and the *station-weighted* mean under each position-dependent convention is
   reported beside `CH-0084`'s position-independent 0.84.
2. **`P2`** — a position-dependent Bernoulli field is **fitted** to Strauss's own three measured
   numbers (48 % edge, 95 % centre, 84 % mean) rather than assumed, on a stated reference
   geometry, and the transfer onto the Gen-1 tile is quoted **as a convention** with its cost.
3. **`P3`** — the dishing is reported as **percentiles over realisations**, not as a mean, with a
   sampling-convergence statement and a binomial standard error on every exceedance probability.
4. **`P4`** — the verdict is delivered on **`C-0058`/`C-0060`'s two-level 3 × 15 design**, on
   **`C-0063`/`C-0068`'s 34 equal springs**, and on **`C-0074`'s 30-root phase-8 design**, so that
   the equal-spring and non-uniform families are compared under the *same* dropout.
5. **`P5`** — every standing dishing figure is reproduced at **zero dropout**, at a departure
   reported and not asserted.

### Units, geometry and sign conventions — restated, not inherited

- Lengths **nm**, forces **pN**, stiffness **pN/nm**, pressure **pN/nm² = 1 MPa exactly**;
  `k_BT = 4.141947 pN·nm` at **300 K**, aqueous buffer with **2 mM MgCl₂** unless a row says
  otherwise.
- `x` runs **along** the helices, `y` **across** them, `z` normal and positive **upward**, away
  from the grafted layer. `w` is positive **downward**.
- **Dishing** is the peak absolute departure from the area-weighted least-squares best-fit
  **plane**, on the same **81 × 81** grid as `C-0026`, `C-0047`, `C-0058`, `C-0060`, `C-0063`,
  `C-0064`, `C-0068` and `C-0074`. **Flat** means below **10 %** of the free-tile stroke —
  `T-5b`'s **convention**, not a physical threshold.
- **An operating state is a `(concentration, gap, bias)` triple of `C-0022`'s solved profiles**,
  and an **operating range** is the set of states **one device** traverses (`CH-0077`). The design
  state is **2 mM, 10 nm, 0.192 V**; the range adds **2 mM, 7 nm, 0.192 V** (`C-0068`).
- **A dropout is a removal, not a perturbation.** A path whose staple is absent has stiffness
  **exactly zero** and is solved as an absent station, never as a small stiffness.
- **Incorporation is a probability per station and the realisations are independent.** Strauss
  reports no correlation length; independence is stated as the convention it is.

## Plan

### The cheap bound, which runs before any solve

**`C-0017`'s mandate is an equality on a SUM** (`C-0058`, `C-0060`), so the expected realised total
is `E[K] = Σ k_i p_i` and its variance is `Var(K) = Σ k_i² p_i (1 − p_i)` — closed form, no
pipeline, no spatial structure beyond the `p_i` themselves. That settles the **placement** half of
the question outright and prices what a builder would have to over-design by. It also gives the
**effective path count** `E[n]` against `C-0049`'s per-path allowable.

A second cheap bound is already in the corpus and is a **rigorous** one:
`MultiStateSurrogate.reachableDishingFloor` is the least-squares-optimal dishing over **all** force
vectors at a station set, and removing stations can only raise it. Evaluated on the **full** set it
is therefore a lower bound valid for **every** realisation — so if it exceeds 0.10 the Monte Carlo
cannot be needed, and if it does not, the Monte Carlo is necessary. Four linear-algebra operations.

### The method, and why it is the affordable one

The dishing pipeline is already a **Woodbury surrogate**: one factorisation of the unsupported
lattice plus one unit-point-load solve per station prices *any* set of per-path stiffnesses at the
cost of an `n × n` Cholesky. A dropout realisation is a **subset** of stations, and slicing the
influence bank onto a subset is **exact** superposition, not an approximation. So a Monte Carlo of
`10⁴` realisations costs `n + S` lattice solves once per placement and then microseconds each.

The alternative — an assembled `OrigamiGrillage` solve per realisation — is an 855-DOF Cholesky
each and is three to four orders of magnitude more expensive for an identical answer. The
surrogate route is the one `C-0058`, `C-0063`, `C-0064`, `C-0068` and `C-0074` all use, and its
agreement with the assembled solve is asserted upstream and re-asserted here.

**Randomness is seeded and reproducible.** A SplitMix64 stream, the seed and the sample count in
the result file, decisions taken at six significant digits and emitted at nine
(`CLAUDE.md`: *a decision must be rounded coarser than the number it is taken on*). No argmin over
realisations is reported — percentiles are order statistics of a rounded sample, and the rank is
reported with the value.

### The mapping — how "edge" and "centre" become a field on this tile

Strauss measures on a **Rothemund rectangle** and the Gen-1 tile is a different object. Three
conventions are carried, each **fitted to Strauss's own three numbers on a stated reference
geometry** and then evaluated on the Gen-1 tile, so that the tile's own mean incorporation is an
**output** and not an input:

| convention | field | free parameter | fitted to |
|---|---|---|---|
| `FLAT_BAND` | `p = p_edge` within a band of width `w` of any tile edge, `p_centre` inside | `w` | the reference rectangle's **area** mean = 0.84 |
| `EXPONENTIAL` | `p = p_centre − (p_centre − p_edge) e^{−d/λ}`, `d` the distance to the nearest edge | `λ` | the same |
| `LATTICE_RING` | one lattice cell: `p` falls by half the range per missing neighbour direction, across-helix cell 2.69 nm, along-helix cell 16 bp | **none** | nothing — its predicted mean is a **check** |
| `UNIFORM` | `p ≡ 0.84` everywhere | none | `CH-0084`'s own reading, the baseline |

`p_edge = 0.48` and `p_centre = 0.95` are the measured extremes throughout.

> **Amended during Execute, and the amendment is an improvement rather than a retreat.** The Plan
> assumed the reference rectangle's dimensions would have to be *derived* from the p7249 scaffold
> and bracketed by helix count. They are **read directly** — Rothemund 2006's Suppl. Note S3 gives
> 24 helices × 288 bp and Strauss's own Suppl. Tables 6/7 and Fig. 4c give a stapled core of
> 16 staple columns of 16 bp — so the only unmeasured number left is the **interhelical distance**,
> and that is what the bracket now sweeps (2.69 / 2.73 / 3.00 nm).
>
> **And a fifth convention was added, because the map itself turned out to be readable.**
> Supplementary Fig. 14 prints all 168 per-staple values; transcribed and validated three ways,
> they support a `MEASURED_DEPTH` field with **no fit at all** — the mildest position-dependent
> reading, and therefore the one a negative verdict is strongest on.

### What would falsify this approach

- **`F1` — the three fitted conventions disagree about the `T-5b` verdict** at the reported
  percentile on the standing flat placement. Then the position dependence is *not resolvable* from
  what is published, and the deliverable is the statement `T-148` allows for — *"the model cannot
  be made position-dependent"* — rather than a number.
- **`F2` — the zero-dropout limit fails to reproduce a standing figure.** Every placement's
  published dishing must come back at departure `0.0`; anything else means the pipeline being
  re-run is not the pipeline that was published.
- **`F3` — the uncoupled tile dishes non-zero under a uniform load.** A free plate on a uniform
  Winkler foundation translates exactly (`CLAUDE.md`), so this must be zero to solver precision at
  every dropout realisation, since a realisation cannot change a load case. Wired as a test.
- **`F4` — the reachable floor over the full station set already exceeds 0.10.** Then no
  distribution and no realisation can be flat and the Monte Carlo is unnecessary; the cheap bound
  would have decided it.

### The five verification gates

1. **dimensional** — a probability is dimensionless and stays in `[0, 1]`; the fitted band and
   decay length are lengths in nm; the Bernoulli relative standard deviation `√(f/(1−f))` is
   dimensionless and reproduces `CH-0084`'s 43.6 % / 104.1 % / 22.9 % exactly; every unphysical
   argument throws.
2. **limiting cases** — `p ≡ 1` reproduces the standing dishing of every placement at departure
   `0.0`; `p ≡ 0.84` uniform reproduces `CH-0084`'s own mean arithmetic; a band of zero width and
   an infinite decay length both give the constant field; a realisation with **no** surviving path
   returns the free-tile dishing exactly; the area-mean quadrature of the exponential field agrees
   with its closed form.
3. **symmetry and conservation** — the expected total `Σ k_i p_i` and its variance
   `Σ k_i² p_i(1−p_i)` are reproduced by the sample to within the sampling error; the free tile
   under a **uniform** load dishes exactly zero (`F3`); a point-reflected placement under a
   point-reflected incorporation field gives the identical distribution, because the lattice is
   centro-symmetric (`C-0015`); the SplitMix64 stream is bit-reproducible from its seed.
4. **numerical convergence** — sample count 2 500 / 5 000 / 10 000 / 20 000 on the reported
   percentiles and exceedance probabilities, with the binomial standard error quoted beside every
   probability; the 81 × 81 dishing grid refined 41 / 81 / 161 at one realisation; the reference
   geometry bracketed.
5. **literature cross-check** — Strauss et al. (2018) **read directly**, with the arithmetic of the
   `+7` percentage-point detection-to-incorporation offset reproduced (41 → 48, 88 → 95, 77 → 84);
   Rothemund's own 94 % pixel yield carried beside it; `C-0060`'s 34.6 % and 31.6 % thresholds,
   `C-0026`'s 17 % break-even, `C-0058`'s 0.2182 and 0.0753, `C-0063`'s 0.0706, `C-0068`'s 0.0789
   and `C-0074`'s 0.1726 / 0.0648 all reproduced from their own result files or pipelines.

### What this task does NOT do

- It does not measure anything. Strauss's numbers are **measured**, which is unusual for this
  repository and is flagged as such — but they are measured on a **plain Rothemund rectangle at one
  folding protocol**, and a Gen-1 coupling path is a designed element nobody has measured.
- It does not touch `C-0060`'s stiffness answer, which `CH-0084` explicitly leaves standing.
- It does not search for a design that survives the dropout. Where a verdict moves, that is
  `CH-0102`, and the search is a new task.
