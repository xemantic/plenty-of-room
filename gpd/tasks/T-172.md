## Formulate

`C-0099` settled the **linear** question completely — the row-end crossover's dihedral spring is worth 2.85 % of `CH-0111`'s interval and the flatness verdict never crosses `T-5b`'s 0.10 — and said in its own validity range that it had **not** settled this one:

> *"THE DECOMPOSITION IS LINEAR AND CARRIES NO PRESTRAIN. Rothemund's `a crossover involving staple strands is in tension with an adjacent crossover involving the scaffold strand` describes an **initial stress**, and `C-0009`'s lattice has no term for one — no initial strain, no geometric stiffness, no residual moment."*

### Units and conventions, restated rather than inherited

- Angles **rad** (degrees only where a source quotes them), rotational stiffness **pN·nm/rad**, couples **pN·nm**, lengths **nm**; `k_BT = 4.141947 pN·nm` at 300 K, aqueous 2 mM MgCl₂.
- `x` runs **along** the helices, `y` **across** them, `z` normal and positive **upward**; `w` positive **downward**.
- A **crossover is two elements** (`C-0009`): a dihedral spring `k_θ` resisting the *relative roll* of the two duplexes, and a vertical link, which is a constraint.
- A **prestrain** `θ₀` is the relative roll the crossover is **built at**: its hinge stores `½ k_θ (Δφ − θ₀)²`. Positive `θ₀` means the upper duplex is rolled positively relative to the lower one at zero load.
- **Dishing** is `C-0063`'s: the peak of the deflection field with its best-fit plane removed, over the free plate's mean descent under the same uniform load.

### Acceptance predicate

- **`P1`** an initial-stress term exists in `C-0009`'s grillage, added **additively**, with the zero-prestrain case asserted identical to current behaviour;
- **`P2`** the best 34-root dishing at 38.08 nm / phase 8 is re-read under it;
- **`P3`** either a source bounds the prestrain, **or** the flatness is insensitive to any prestrain inside a **stated** bound.

## Plan

### The cheap bound, and why it is cheap

Expand the prestrained hinge energy:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;`½ k_θ (Δφ − θ₀)² = ½ k_θ Δφ² − k_θ θ₀ Δφ + ½ k_θ θ₀²`.

**The quadratic term is untouched**, so a prestrain moves no entry of the stiffness matrix: it is a fixed couple `± k_θ θ₀` on two roll degrees of freedom, i.e. a **load vector**. Three consequences, all before any solve:

1. the deflection field is **linear** in `θ₀`, so one unit-prestrain solve fixes the whole axis;
2. peak dishing is an absolute value, so the triangle inequality gives a rigorous ceiling `D(θ₀) ≤ D_load + |θ₀|·D_unit` and therefore a **crossing angle** `θ*` at which `T-5b`'s 0.10 could first be reached;
3. `C-0058`'s Woodbury bank stays exact, because the host's factorisation is unchanged.

**Justification against cost.** The alternative — an oxDNA or all-atom edge crossover — is what `CH-0111` ranked third and `C-0099` recommended against; and it would have to be run at a prestrain nobody can specify. One extra solve gives `θ*`, and `θ*` is compared against a ladder of candidate prestrains **derived** from the lattice (`CLAUDE.md`'s *register departure is linear in the base-pair offset*) plus two assumption-free geometric ceilings, a quarter turn and a half turn. If `θ*` is past the ladder, the branch closes on arithmetic.

### What would falsify this approach

| # | falsifier |
|---|---|
| **F1** | zero prestrain does not reproduce `C-0090`'s **0.0621469105** |
| **F2** | the coupled response is not linear in `θ₀`, i.e. the term is not a load |
| **F3** | a **uniform** prestrain over every crossover dishes zero, i.e. an eigenstrain behaves like a uniform load — the falsifier the task was **proposed** with, and the one this task expects to refute |
| **F4** | the exhaustive optimum placement moves under a prestrain |
| **F5** | a uniform load on a uniform foundation dishes more than `1e-6` of the free stroke (the standing one) |
| **F6** | `T-5b`'s 0.10 is crossed at a prestrain inside the derived ladder |

**On `F3`.** `CLAUDE.md`'s standing falsifier is that *a uniform load on a uniform Winkler foundation produces no dishing at all*, because a free plate translates. **That does not transfer to an eigenstrain**: the state that relaxes every hinge and every vertical link at once under a uniform `θ₀` is a **cylinder of curvature `θ₀/d`**, not a translation. A tile under a uniform prestrain curls, and this task measures the sagitta rather than asserting a zero.
