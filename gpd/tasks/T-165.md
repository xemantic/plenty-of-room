## Formulate

### The question

`C-0093` established that a shared-body coupling is the largest single improvement anywhere in
this branch — **0.24028028** of the free-tile stroke at the 90th percentile under `C-0087`'s
measured staple dropout, against `C-0089`'s best-of-22 **0.284537599** and `T-5b`'s **0.10** —
and that it reaches that number with **uniform** ties on **`C-0015`'s abstract equal-tributary
grid**.

Two axes were therefore left unspent, and `C-0093` names both:

> 1. **A distribution search on the shared-body topology.** The ties here are uniform.
> 2. **A placement search on the UPWARD lattice under this topology.** The 90-tie winner sits on
>    `C-0015`'s abstract equal-tributary grid; the 53 real upward sites are irregular.

**This task spends them both.** Together they would have to be worth **2.40×**.

### The numeric target and the acceptance predicate

> **`P1`** — the **90th-percentile** peak dishing, as a ratio of the free-tile stroke, of a
> shared-body coupling whose **stations are the real upward crossover sites** of `C-0055`'s
> `EAST` lattice and whose **tie stiffnesses are searched**, under `C-0087`'s measured Bernoulli
> dropout at 10 000 seeded realisations, is emitted against `T-5b`'s **0.10**.

Further predicates:

- **`P2`** — the **cheap bounds run first** and are reported whatever they say: the upward-site
  census over all 32 phases, the redundancy division at the inventory the lattice actually
  offers, and the **stiff-tie kinematic limit**.
- **`P3`** — the distribution search is run at `C-0017`'s mandate **placed on the body's ground**,
  not shared between the ties as a sum, and the tie is capped by a **force**.
- **`P4`** — the placement search is run on stations the lattice **supplies**; no abstract grid
  carries a headline.
- **`P5`** — the array is graded on the identical stations, so the topology's own worth is read
  at matched placement.
- **`P6`** — every standing figure reproduces: `C-0063`'s 0.0706145537, `C-0093`'s 0.0344013403
  and 0.24028028, `C-0066`'s 53-site inventory at phase 24, `C-0026`'s 4.90731102 nm.
- **`P7`** — the falsifiers are executable tests.

**A negative is a real answer.** If the last axis is spent, the deliverable says so plainly and
says what that does to `C-0089`, `C-0093` and `ANSWERS.md` §1.

### Locked units, geometry and sign conventions

- Lengths **nm**, forces **pN**, stiffness **pN/nm**, plate rigidity **pN·nm**, pressure
  **pN/nm² = 1 MPa exactly**; `k_BT = 4.141947 pN·nm` at **300 K** in aqueous **2 mM MgCl₂**.
- `x` runs **along** the helices, `y` **across** them, `z` normal and positive **upward** — away
  from the grafted layer, which is **below** the tile. `w` is positive **downward**; the origin is
  the tile centre.
- **Dishing** is the peak absolute departure from the area-weighted least-squares best-fit
  **plane**, on the same **81 × 81** grid as every flatness claim upstream. **Flat** means below
  **0.10** of the free-tile stroke — `T-5b`'s **convention**, not a physical threshold.
- A **tie** runs from a tile station to the shared **body**; the body runs to **ground** through
  its own element. A tie force is positive **upward on the tile**.
- **A dropout is a REMOVAL, not a perturbation** (`C-0087`), and it removes the tie; the body
  stays (`C-0093`).
- **The operating state is named**: `C-0022`'s **solved 2 mM / 10 nm / 0.192 V** profile —
  `C-0063`'s, `C-0087`'s, `C-0089`'s and `C-0093`'s design state. This is the **tenth** time this
  programme has had to say which state a flatness verdict is read at.
- **The verdict statistic is the 90th percentile**, a nearest-rank order statistic, and it is
  `C-0087`'s, `C-0089`'s and `C-0093`'s, so all four claims are comparable cell for cell.
- **Every optimised percentile is read OUT OF SAMPLE.** A descent sees a 200-realisation training
  ensemble at seed **20260819**; every quoted number is read on the independent
  10 000-realisation grading ensemble at `C-0087`'s seed **20260817**.

### What the mandate means under this topology, and it is not what it means under an array

`C-0017`'s **33.3333333 pN/nm** is an equality on the coupling's **heave secant**. Under an array
that secant is `Σ tᵢ`, so the mandate is a **per-station budget** and a distribution is a
redistribution of a scarce quantity. Under a shared body the same secant is `series(Σ tᵢ, g)`:
`C-0093` measures **99.90 %** of the coupling's compliance in the body's **ground** at the
stiffest ties, and the ground is a rigid-body mode of the tile, which dishing projects out.

**So the ties are not budgeted at all.** What caps a tie is the per-path **force**:

- **conservatively**, `C-0049`'s `a/s` read as a stiffness — `10 pN / 3 nm = 3.33333333 pN/nm`,
  which assumes the tie stretches by the whole stroke;
- **honestly**, the **solved** peak tie force under dropout, against `C-0006`'s 10 pN unzip and
  the 48 pN shear allowable. `C-0093`'s winner reads 9.01 pN at ties of 1000 pN/nm.

Both readings are swept and both are emitted. A search run at `Σ tᵢ = 33.3333333` would be a
search on the wrong constraint.

## Plan

### The cheap bounds, in the order they run, and why they can end the task

**`B1` — the upward-site census, a lattice count with no solve.** `C-0066` counts **53** `EAST`
sites at phase 24, which is `C-0063`'s phase. The census over **all 32 phases** costs one pass
over `anchoring.upwardHingeSites` and says what the lattice's own ceiling is. A count is what
`C-0089` and `C-0093` both fail on, so the ceiling is the first thing to establish.

**`B2` — the redundancy division.** `C-0093`'s measured slope is **−0.784357442** in
`d ln p90 / d ln n` and its fit crosses `T-5b`'s 0.10 at **252.126899** ties. Evaluated at the
inventory `B1` returns, the fit says what factor the placement and distribution axes must buy
**together**. Against that: `C-0089` measured the distribution axis at **1.30–1.61×** on the array
and `C-0074`'s irregular-beats-regular at **1.13×**. If the demanded factor exceeds the product of
the two measured ones, the expected answer is a negative and the search is confirmatory — which is
worth running anyway, because a *measured* negative on the last axis is the deliverable.

**`B3` — the stiff-tie kinematic limit, and it is the bound that prices the DISTRIBUTION axis
before it is searched.** For a rigid shared body, scaling every tie by `s → ∞` gives

> `K_c = s [T₀ − T₀Φ(ΦᵀT₀Φ)⁻¹ΦᵀT₀] + O(1)`

whose leading term diverges in every **non-affine** direction. The tile's stations are therefore
driven onto a **plane** — a *kinematic* constraint whose three remaining freedoms are held by the
ground — and that limit **does not depend on the tie distribution at all**. So the whole tie
axis, distribution included, is bounded by the gap between a finite tie ladder and its own
kinematic limit, and `C-0093`'s own table already shows that gap closing: 0.2496 at 100 pN/nm
against 0.2403 at 1000. **Measure the limit and the spread; if they coincide, the distribution
axis is worth a percent and not a factor.**

**`B4` — the lattice's own column ceiling against `C-0089`'s run-length demand.** An upward line
has the bare 32 bp = 10.88 nm pitch, so a 40 nm row carries at most **4** columns. `C-0089`'s
run-length arithmetic demands **13**. That division needs no topology and no solve.

**`B5` — the ORACLE floor at the real stations under dropout** (`InfluenceSurrogate
.reachableDishingFloorAt`). It optimises over **all** force vectors at the surviving stations, so
it bounds *every* topology and not only an array — `CH-0104`'s discipline applies verbatim: it can
**exclude** and can never **admit**, and it must be quoted with the gap to what a fixed design
reaches.

### The expensive parts, and their justification against cost

The instruments already exist and each is an exact reduction rather than an approximation:

1. **`C-0058`'s Woodbury bank.** A station enters `OrigamiGrillage` as a *load*, so one
   factorisation of the unsupported host plus `n + 1` load cases prices every coupling at those
   stations. A candidate then costs an `n × n` Cholesky.
2. **`C-0093`'s condensation.** The shared body is one added term on the same system, and
   `InfluenceSurrogate.solveWithSharedBody` carries the array as its `body = null` corner.
3. **`C-0089`'s ranking instrument.** The worst single removal ranks designs against the 90th
   percentile at **ρ = 0.9729**, `n + 1` solves against 10 000, which is what makes a *placement*
   search over subsets affordable.

The three searches:

- **Placement at the ceiling count** — one bank per phase over **all** that phase's upward sites,
  32 phases. This is the placement search at the count the lattice permits, and at full inventory
  there is no subset choice, so it is exhaustive in the only variable there is.
- **Placement at fixed count** — subsets of size 34 and 45 from the best phase's inventory, on the
  cheap objective, so that the **count** and the **placement** are separated (which is `T-163`'s
  question asked of this topology).
- **Distribution** — a cyclic coordinate descent on `log tᵢ` at fixed placement, under a per-tie
  ceiling, with the ground re-placed on the mandate at every candidate. Trained out of sample.

**What would falsify this approach.** If the stiff-tie limit `B3` does *not* collapse the
distribution family — if two very different distributions at 1000 pN/nm give materially different
dishing — then the coupling is not near its kinematic limit, the descent is doing real work, and
the cheap bound must be withdrawn rather than quoted. That is falsifier `F2` and it is wired as a
test.

### Verification gates

1. **dimensional** — a site census is a count; a pitch over a bending length is dimensionless; a
   tie ceiling is a force over a stroke; a descent refuses a non-positive ceiling, an empty start
   and a start outside its own bounds.
2. **limiting cases** — the full-inventory placement at phase 24 contains `C-0063`'s 34 roots as a
   subset; a one-tie and a two-tie free rigid body add exactly zero; a body grounded at `10⁹×` the
   mandate reproduces the array; a descent whose objective is constant returns its start.
3. **symmetry and conservation** — `F3`, on the uncoupled tile **and** under a free shared body;
   the reflection congruence of the upward lattice (`C-0063`'s centro-symmetric phases) is
   asserted on the census rather than inherited; the grading ensemble draws `C-0087`'s own stream.
4. **numerical convergence and statistical power** — realisations at 1250/2500/5000/10 000; the
   dishing grid at 41/81/161 on the **mean** over 200 realisations (`C-0087`'s cure for the
   degenerate nested-grid percentile); the descent's training-ensemble size at 100/200/400; a
   binomial standard error beside every exceedance. **Departures at two significant digits, no
   wall clock and no step counter** (`C-0093`, `P-18`).
5. **literature and upstream cross-check** — the reproductions of `P6`, plus `C-0093`'s slope and
   its 252.126899 crossing recomputed rather than transcribed.

### Reproducible randomness

Grading **seed 20260817** (`C-0087`'s own) at **10 000** realisations; training **seed 20260819**
(`C-0089`'s own) at **200**. Both are emitted in the result file's parameter block, with the
sample counts. Decisions at **6 significant digits** with the earlier candidate winning ties;
emission at **9**.
