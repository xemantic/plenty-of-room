## Formulate

### The question, in one line

`C-0053`'s footprint convention charges **one duplex girth, 2.69 nm**, between two consecutive collinear elements on the same host row.
`C-0079`/`CH-0093` show that slot is **coaxial**, contains **no body**, and that what it must prevent is a blunt-end **stacking bond** rather than a clash.
So: **what clearance should it carry, and what does the design get back?**

### Locked units and constants

- Lengths **nm**, forces **pN**, energies **pN·nm** and `k_BT`; `k_BT = 4.141947 pN·nm` at **300 K**; `1 kcal/mol = 6.94769 pN·nm`; `1 zJ = 1 pN·nm` exactly.
- Medium **aqueous 2 mM MgCl₂**, `I = 3c = 6 mM`. Every stacking number in the literature is measured at a **higher** Mg²⁺ than that and this is carried as a validity note, never transferred silently.
- `x` runs **along** the host sheet's helices, `y` **across** them, `z` **normal and positive upward**. Origin at the tile centre.
- **The rise is 0.34 nm and it is the design quantum along `x`.** Every axial length on a duplex is an integer count of it.
- Root pitch `p` = **32 bp = 10.88 nm** (`C-0055`'s upward lattice); arm `L` = **8.16439083 nm** (`C-0039`/`C-0055`, the exact elastica root at the per-path secant); the standing collinear allowance `d` = **2.69 nm** (SAXS, Fischer et al.).
- **A collinear clearance is an AXIAL gap between two duplex END FACES.** It is *not* a girth, *not* an interhelical distance, and *not* a transverse exclusion.
- **Sign convention on the bond**: a stacking free energy is **negative** (favourable); the elastic work done to close a gap is **positive**. The bond is suppressed when their sum is positive.

### Acceptance predicate

A **collinear clearance quantised at the 0.34 nm rise**, with

1. `C-0069`'s `Q5` margin `M = p − d − L`,
2. `C-0069`'s **`c ≤ 2.3416`** end-condition razor and both of its bisected joint ceilings,
3. `C-0053`'s plan budget and placed count, `C-0074`'s 30-root ceiling,

**all re-read at it** — or the plain statement that the allowance is a fabrication number this programme cannot set.

The clearance must be stated as an **energy against `k_BT`**, not as a distance alone,
and the deliverable must say **what the design has to control**, not only how far apart to put things.

## Plan

### The cheap bounds, which run before any code

**Bound 1 — the design space is a handful of integers, and so is the margin.**
A clearance between two duplex end faces on a common host row is an axial length, and DNA quantises
every axial length at the rise. The stacking range is already measured (0.51108–1.3 nm, `C-0079`),
so the candidate set is `{1, 2, …, 8} × 0.34 nm` and the plausible one is three or four integers wide.
**And the same quantisation applies to every other term of the margin identity**: `p` is 32 bp by
construction and `L` is a base-pair count because an arm is built from base pairs. So
`M = (32 − N_d − N_L) × 0.34 nm` — a *multiple* of the quantum, never 0.0256 nm.
If that holds it settles the whole knife edge before any solve, and it is a **proof**, not a search.

**Bound 2 — every candidate clearance already clears the published margin by more than an order of magnitude.**
`M(N) = 10.88 − 0.34N − 8.16439`. At `N = 8` (the nearest integer to the standing 2.69 nm) it is
`−0.0044`; at `N = 4` it is `1.3556`. So the question is never *whether* `CH-0093` widens the budget
but *by how many base pairs*, and the expensive part is only the joint window, which is one cube.

**Bound 3 — the criterion is a two-state balance and it needs no simulation.**
The two faces are covalently tethered to the same sheet at a fixed pitch, so a bond can only form if
the material strains to close the gap. Writing `W(g) = ½ k_closure (g − g_contact)²` against
`|ΔG_stack| = 4.4114 k_BT` gives the threshold in closed form, and the only uncertain input
(`k_closure`) is bracketed by a four-decade sweep `Gen1Tile` already carries.

### Method, justified against cost

Everything above is arithmetic. The **only** thing worth spending a solve on is `C-0069`'s
two-restraint window, because it is a bisection on `C-0039`'s exact elastica and its answer is what
decides whether a wider budget buys the design real margin on its two joints. Four bisections at
each of ~8 clearances is a few seconds. **A packing re-run is likewise cheap** because
`maximumRootedElementsInRow` enumerates subsets of a row of at most four.

**What would falsify this approach**: if the arm length were *not* expressible as a base-pair count
inside the mandate — then the margin is not an integer identity and bound 1 collapses, and the
question becomes a tolerance problem rather than a lattice one.

### Declared falsifiers

| # | falsifier | what it would mean |
|---|---|---|
| **F1** | the suppression criterion comes out **above** the standing 2.69 nm | `CH-0093` buys nothing; the convention was right by accident and the budget does not move |
| **F2** | the recommended clearance leaves `M ≤ 0` | the recommendation refuses the element it exists to serve |
| **F3** | the margin identity is **not** an integer multiple of the rise — some term cannot be built at a base-pair count | bound 1 collapses; the knife edge is a tolerance problem, not a lattice one |
| **F4** | at the recommended budget one of `Q5`'s two joints is still inside its ceiling by **< 10 %** | the widening does not reach the razor and `C-0071`'s two `NONE` bands stand |
| **F5** | the 34-instance array stops placing at one level, or `C-0053`'s 45-arm count or `C-0074`'s 30-root ceiling moves **adversely** | the clearance buys the joint window at the price of the placement |

### Five verification gates

1. **Dimensional** — a clearance, a gap and a margin are lengths and scale with every length; an energy is `pN·nm`; the end factor is dimensionless; the whole quantisation is invariant under a common rescaling of `(length, rise)`.
2. **Limiting cases** — at zero clearance the budget is the bare pitch; at `N = 8` the identity returns `C-0069`'s own arithmetic; a rigid closure path (`k → ∞`) makes the required gap the contact separation; an occupancy of 1 makes the criterion the bare `|ΔG|` balance.
3. **Symmetry and conservation** — `bendingLengthForStiffness` and `bendingFactorForLength` remain exact inverses at every new budget; the margin computed on the lattice equals the margin computed in nm; the placed count is invariant under a rigid translation and under the placement's own centro-symmetry.
4. **Numerical convergence** — the restraint bisections are resolution-independent; the elastica arm is RK4-step independent; the capacity bisection is resolution-independent.
5. **Literature and upstream** — `C-0069`'s 8.19 / 2.34166 / 79.678 / 13.930 / 0.0256, `C-0072`'s four floors, `C-0074`'s 9.5350, `C-0053`'s 43 of 45 and `C-0079`'s 4.4114 `k_BT` and 15.1103× all reproduced from the libraries that own them, with the departure quoted.
