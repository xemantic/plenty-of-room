# T-169 — a study's emitted prose cites a ceiling `C-0049` withdrew

| | |
|---|---|
| **Leaf** | `A8.2` |
| **Raised by** | [`C-0101`](../claims/C-0101-re-emitting-what-the-repair-moved.md) §4 |
| **Reserved IDs** | claim `C-0105` |
| **Verification type** | **logical** (an audit of which clause a declared number belongs to) **+ in-silico** (the study re-run and every emitted row re-read under both readings) |

## Formulate

Re-emitting the elastica study on `C-0096`'s repaired solver replaced a spurious *"the arm folds"* note with

> *"places, but past the **40 pN/nm** ceiling at the desired stroke"*

in 26 of its 34 placement rows.
**`C-0049` withdrew that reading.**
40 pN/nm is `1.2 × (100 pN / 3 nm)` — a *declared* linearity tolerance on the placement discharge, carrying §3's **acceptable** clause's stroke inside it.
The same construction at §3's **desired** clause is **12 pN/nm**,
so reading 40 at a 10 nm stroke is not the conservative choice: it is the wrong clause's number and 3.33× too generous.
`CLAUDE.md` records the general form — *a ceiling written as a multiple of a mandate inherits the mandate's stroke*.

### Units and conventions, restated rather than inherited

- Stiffness **pN/nm**, force **pN**, stroke **nm**; `k_BT = 4.141947 pN·nm` at 300 K.
- A **secant** is `F(s)/s` and a **tangent** is `dF/ds`; `C-0017`'s placement is an *equality on the secant* and its stability an *floor on the tangent*.
- A **declared** ceiling is a design tolerance nobody measured; a **derived** ceiling is one that follows from a cited allowable.

### Acceptance predicate

- **`P1`** no emitted verdict quotes the withdrawn ceiling at a stroke it is not owed at;
- **`P2`** every row's verdict is unchanged under both readings — the row fails on the reach either way;
- **`P3`** the miss does not rest on a *declared* number at all.

Or the statement that the ceiling should be **restored**, which is the acceptance's other branch and needs an argument against `C-0049`, i.e. a challenge.

## Plan

**The cheap bound is one division and it runs before anything else**: `40 / (100/3) = 1.2`, so the ceiling is a *multiple of the mandate* and moving the clause moves it to 12. Both readings then have to be applied to the same 34 emitted rows; if the softest placing row's tangent is far above **12**, no verdict can move and the re-wording is free.

**Method.** Read the clause-correct ceiling out of `synthesis/CouplingCeiling.kt`, which `C-0049` already owns and which takes the stroke as an argument, rather than writing a second copy of the arithmetic. Re-word the emitted string off it, re-run the study, and re-read the emitted file row by row under both readings **and** under `C-0006`'s per-path secant ceiling `n·allowable/s` — the only ceiling in `C-0017`'s stack that is not declared.

**What would falsify this approach.**

| # | falsifier |
|---|---|
| **F1** | re-wording the note moves a verdict |
| **F2** | the withdrawn phrase survives anywhere in the re-emitted file |
| **F3** | the clause-correct reading is *looser* than the withdrawn one, so the correction is a relaxation |

**Cost.** One string, one library call, one study run, one small reader study. Justified because the alternative — leaving it — is `C-0078`'s failure (*a verdict that survives can survive on a different reason*) in a committed result file, which is the defect `C-0101` had just corrected one level up.
