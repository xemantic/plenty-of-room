## Formulate

### The question

`C-0087` and `C-0089` together establish that under Strauss et al.'s measured per-staple
incorporation the flat Gen-1 tile stops being flat, and that **what refuses it is a COUNT**:

- the best 90th-percentile dishing over 22 `placement × distribution` cells is **0.284537599**,
  still **2.85×** `T-5b`'s 0.10;
- density is monotone but buys **1.60×** over six times the paths where the verdict needs 5.3×;
- **a dropout IS an increase in the attachment pitch**, so with `ℓ = 12.8290845 nm` and a p90
  longest absence run of 3 the density demanded is **13 columns, 195 paths** against the **34**
  the plan admits — **5.7× short, in a division that needs no solve**;
- and the negative belongs to **fixed** distributions: the *oracle* floor over each realisation's
  survivors reaches **0.00111–0.01988**, **255×** better (`CH-0104`).

Every coupling this programme has priced is an **array of independent load paths**: path `i` runs
from station `i` on the tile to **ground**, and `C-0017`'s mandate is the **sum** of their
stiffnesses. A missing staple therefore *removes a support*.

`C-0089` names one structural escape and prices none of it:

> **One stiff body tied at many points makes a missing tie a stiffness perturbation rather than a
> removed load path.**

**Does it? And can such a body be built and placed on this tile?**

### The numeric target

The **90th percentile** of the peak dishing over the free-tile stroke, over `C-0087`'s seeded
Bernoulli dropout realisations at its `MEASURED_DEPTH` incorporation, against `T-5b`'s **0.10**
convention — the identical statistic, ensemble, seed and tolerance `C-0089` reports, so that the
two answers are comparable cell for cell.

### The operating state — named, because a flatness verdict is owed one

**`C-0022`'s solved 2 mM / 10 nm / 0.192 V** profile, the design state of `C-0063`, `C-0068`,
`C-0087` and `C-0089`, with the held end of `C-0068`'s range (2 mM / 7 nm / 0.192 V) carried
beside it. This is the ninth time the programme has had to say which state a flatness verdict is
read at.

### Geometry, sign conventions and units

- Lengths **nm**, forces **pN**, stiffness **pN/nm**, pressure **pN/nm² = 1 MPa exactly**;
  `k_BT = 4.141947 pN·nm` at **300 K** in aqueous **2 mM MgCl₂**.
- `x` **along** the helices, `y` **across** them, `z` normal and positive **upward** — away from
  the grafted layer, which is **below** the tile. `w` is positive **downward**; the origin is the
  tile centre.
- **Dishing** is the peak absolute departure from the area-weighted least-squares best-fit
  **plane**, on the same **81 × 81** grid as every flatness claim upstream. **Flat** means below
  **0.10** of the free-tile stroke — `T-5b`'s **convention**, not a physical threshold.
- A **tie** runs from a tile station to the **shared body**; the body runs to **ground** through
  its own element(s). A tie force `f_i` is positive **upward on the tile** and therefore
  downward on the body.

### The topology, stated as arithmetic before anything is modelled

Write `M` for the free tile's compliance at the stations, `T = diag(t)` for the tie stiffnesses,
`F_b` for the **shared body's own compliance at the same stations, ground included**. Then

```
array          f = T (w_free − M f)                 ⟹  (T⁻¹ + M) f = w_free
shared body    f = T (w_free − M f − F_b f)         ⟹  (T⁻¹ + M + F_b) f = w_free
```

**The shared body is an additive compliance in series with the ties, and nothing else.** The
array is its `F_b = 0` corner, so the two topologies live in one family and one code path, and
the coupling matrix the tile sees is `K_c = (T⁻¹ + F_b)⁻¹` — **not diagonal**, which is the whole
of the difference.

### The acceptance predicate, falsifiable

1. **`P1`** — the closed-form rigid limit is derived and **verified numerically before any
   dropout run**: for a free rigid body `K_c = T − T A (AᵀTA)⁻¹ AᵀT` with `A = [1, x, y]`, of rank
   `max(n − 3, 0)`, hence **exactly zero at `n = 1, 2, 3`** — `CLAUDE.md`'s own sequence.
2. **`P2`** — the **mandate arithmetic** is reported: what `C-0017`'s 33.3333 pN/nm buys as a
   per-station support stiffness under each topology, with no solve.
3. **`P3`** — the body's own rigidity is a **swept design variable**, from a single-layer Gen-1
   sheet to the rigid limit, and the buildable end is named with the motif that supplies it.
4. **`P4`** — buildability and placement are checked against `C-0035`'s height budget, `C-0061`'s
   arm slab, `C-0066`'s clear column and `C-0041`'s severance, **and reported even if negative**.
5. **`P5`** — the 90th-percentile dishing under `C-0087`'s unchanged dropout, against 0.10, with
   the single-removal amplification beside it.
6. **`P6`** — every standing figure that the pipeline must reproduce does:
   `C-0063`'s **0.0706145537** at zero dropout and zero body compliance, `C-0058`'s 0.0753,
   `C-0087`'s 0.5010 and 0.6391, `C-0089`'s 0.284537599 arithmetic inputs.
7. **`P7`** — five verification gates as executable tests, including the free falsifiers: a
   **uniform load on a uniform foundation dishes exactly zero**, and the rigid-body limit
   reproduces the closed form of `P1`.

## Plan

### The cheap bound, and why it runs first

Three things are settled by arithmetic before a single Monte Carlo realisation, and any one of
them can end the task:

1. **The rigid limit.** A perfectly rigid shared body is the *best case* of the family in the only
   sense that matters here — it is the body that adds no compliance of its own. If the rigid
   shared body cannot reach `T-5b`'s 0.10 **at zero dropout**, no elastic body can and the branch
   is dead for the price of `n` linear solves. That is the **declared falsifier**.
2. **The rank statement.** `K_c` for a *free* rigid body has rank `n − 3`: the body can carry no
   net force and no net moment, so it supports only the **non-affine** part of the station
   displacement — which is exactly what dishing measures. At `n ≤ 3` it adds **exactly zero**,
   reproducing `CLAUDE.md`'s *"a body attached at ONE point adds exactly zero"*, *"two points
   determine a line"* and *"the arm's own `EI` is engaged only at three ties"* as one formula.
3. **The mandate arithmetic.** `C-0017`'s 33.3333 pN/nm is a **sum over the paths** of an array,
   so at 34 paths every station is supported at `0.98 pN/nm`. Under a shared body the mandate is
   supplied by the **body's ground element**, in series with the *parallel* sum of the ties — so
   the ties are freed and the per-station support stiffness is capped by the **per-path force
   allowable** instead. `Σᵢ series(tᵢ, gᵢ)` against `series(Σtᵢ, g)`: the same total, a different
   place to put the compliance, and the difference is a division.

The expensive calculation is a Monte Carlo over 10 000 seeded realisations per cell. It costs
nothing extra in machinery — the additive-compliance form above means the existing exact Woodbury
surrogate needs **one** new method — but it costs an hour of wall clock over a sweep, which is why
the three bounds above run first and are reported whatever they say.

### Method

- **The tile, the load, the lattice, the ensemble and the dishing pipeline are `C-0089`'s
  unchanged**, read from the same result files at run time: `C-0009`'s grillage at
  `C-0063`'s phase 24, `C-0022`'s solved collar, `C-0087`'s `MEASURED_DEPTH` field, seed
  **20260817** at 10 000 realisations.
- **The shared body** is an orthotropic Ritz plate on the same Legendre product basis
  `PlateOnFoundation` uses, condensed to the tie stations: `F_b = Φ (S + G)⁻¹ Φᵀ`, with `S` the
  body's own bending energy from `OrigamiSheet`'s `D_∥`, `D_⊥` and `D_k`, and `G` its ground.
  The **rigid** body is the same expression restricted to the three rigid modes. Basis order is a
  convergence axis, because Rayleigh-Ritz **overestimates** a body's stiffness and that error
  runs in the shared body's favour.
- **The mandate is placed, not assumed**: the body's ground stiffness is scaled by a bracketed
  root so that the whole coupling's heave secant is exactly `C-0017`'s 33.3333 pN/nm at full
  presence, for every tie stiffness in the sweep. Without that the comparison is between two
  different couplings.
- **The per-path force is read from the solve**, not from a stiffness times a stroke: the
  surrogate already reports each tie's reaction under the solved collar.

### What would falsify this approach

- **`F1` — the declared falsifier.** *A perfectly rigid shared body, tied at `C-0063`'s 34
  stations with unbounded tie stiffness and the mandate placed on its ground, does not reach
  `T-5b`'s 0.10 at **zero** dropout.* If that fires, the topology cannot help at all, the count
  argument survives it, `C-0089` stands unqualified and no elastic body and no Monte Carlo is
  worth running.
- **`F2`** — the shared body's single-removal amplification is **not** materially below the
  array's 4.05–7.97. That is `C-0089`'s own mechanism, and it is the one this task claims the
  topology changes.
- **`F3`** — the uncoupled tile under a **uniform** load dishes non-zero. A wiring falsifier, kept
  from `C-0089`.
- **`F4`** — at `F_b = 0` the pipeline fails to reproduce `C-0063`'s 0.0706145537. Then the new
  code path is not the old one generalised and nothing it says can be compared upstream.
- **`F5`** — the shared body cannot be placed, and the count question is then priced anyway,
  because the acceptance admits *"or the statement that the shared body cannot be built"*.

### Cost

One new main source and one study. The banks are `C-0089`'s and are rebuilt, not re-read: eight
Woodbury banks at 81 × 81, a tie-stiffness sweep, a body-rigidity sweep and a dropout ensemble at
10 000 realisations per graded cell. Budget ~20 minutes; smoke-run at toy counts first.
