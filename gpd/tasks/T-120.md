## The question, stated so it can be answered wrongly

`C-0054` establishes that a connected 15-duplex sheet can spend at most **42 of its 56** crossovers as
flexure hinges, because each of its 14 interfaces needs one retained crossover.
`C-0053` reaches the same sheet from the plan view and lands lower, at **25** arms.
Both claims compute everything on the **lattice**, so nothing either of them reports is wrong.

What neither asks is whether the **continuum** statements the rest of the programme rests on —
`C-0006`'s load distribution, `C-0010`'s positional variance, `C-0047`'s flatness — still describe that
body at all. `C-0009` already found the reduction **marginal by its own criterion on the intact sheet**
(`ℓ_∥/p = 0.83 < 1`, *"an anchor's influence patch contains 3.9 crossovers … four elements is not a
continuum"*). At the ceiling the sheet has **one crossover per interface**, four times sparser.

**So the question is not whether the plate degrades. It is at what count it stops being the right model,
and what is the right model on the other side.**

## Conventions, restated rather than inherited

- Lengths **nm**, forces **pN**, stiffness **pN/nm**, rigidities **pN·nm**, foundation stiffness
  **pN/nm³**, pressure **pN/nm²** (= 1 MPa exactly); `k_BT = 4.141947 pN·nm` at **300 K** in aqueous
  **2 mM MgCl₂**.
- **Plan view.** `x` **along** the helices, `y` **across** them, origin at the tile centre; `w` positive
  **downward**, compressing the polymer layer.
- **An interface** is an adjacent duplex pair `(b, b+1)`; a 15-duplex sheet has **14**. The nominal
  eight-column layout gives each interface **four** crossovers at the 32 bp = **10.88 nm** per-interface
  pitch, so the inventory is **56** (`C-0015`, `C-0040`).
- **The retained count `N_ret`** is the single parameter of every criterion below. Consumption is
  `C-0054`'s `SPREAD` pattern — round robin over the interfaces, which is the connectivity-optimal
  design and the only one that reaches the ceiling in one piece.
- **The depleted per-interface pitch** is `p_eff = p · N/N_ret`, exactly `p` at `N_ret = N`. This is the
  areal-density reading of the pitch and it is what makes every criterion below reduce to `C-0009`'s
  published number at zero consumption, **exactly rather than to a tolerance**.
- **The depleted across-helix rigidity** is the continuum one, `D_⊥ = k_θ d/p_eff`, i.e. `C-0009`'s own
  `k_θ d/p` scaled by `N_ret/N`. `C-0054`'s two conventions (Voigt `k_θ d² N_ret/A`, Reuss
  `L_y k_θ/(L_x Σ 1/n_i)`) are carried beside it and their bracket is reported.
- **The connectivity ceiling** is `N_ret = D − 1 = 14`, i.e. 42 spent — `C-0054`'s pigeonhole.

## The four criteria, and which of them is a convention

| | criterion | direction pairing | what it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | `ℓ_⊥/p` | **mismatched** — across-helix length, along-helix pitch | `C-0006`'s, which `C-0009` **corrected**; carried only so the correction is visible at the ceiling too |
| **B** | `ℓ_∥/p_eff` | matched, along the helices | `C-0009`'s first matched criterion |
| **C** | `ℓ_⊥/d` | matched, across the helices | `C-0009`'s second matched criterion |
| **D** | **crossovers in an anchor's influence patch**, `π ℓ_∥ ℓ_⊥/(d p_eff)` | — | `CLAUDE.md`: *"quote the number of crossovers inside an anchor's influence patch, which needs no convention at all"* |

`ℓ_∥ = (D_∥/k_f)^(1/4)` and `ℓ_⊥ = (D_⊥/k_f)^(1/4)` are the Winkler bending lengths, and `D_∥ = EI/d` is
**untouched by consumption** — a crossover is not a load path along the helices. So all four criteria are
exact powers of `N_ret`:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;`ℓ_⊥ ∝ N_ret^(1/4)`, `ℓ_⊥/d ∝ N_ret^(1/4)`, `ℓ_∥/p_eff ∝ N_ret`,
**`patch ∝ N_ret^(5/4)`**

and each **inverts in closed form**. `CLAUDE.md`: *"invert a length-dependent allowable, do not just
evaluate it"* — the question is not what the criterion reads at 14, it is what count it crosses 1 at.

## Plan

**Cheap bound first, and it is the whole answer to the predicate.** Four criteria and three inversions,
in closed form, at `C-0009`'s five foundation multipliers ×[0.25, 4]. No mesh, no matrix, no free
parameter. **Declared falsifier: if the patch count at the ceiling is still ≥ 1**, the continuum survives
the connectivity ceiling and the task reduces to a paragraph saying so.

**Then, and only then, three models side by side** under `C-0022`'s solved load, because a criterion says
a reduction is invalid and does not say what to use instead:

1. **PLATE** — `C-0006`'s orthotropic Kirchhoff plate with `D_⊥` smeared to `k_θ d/p_eff`.
2. **LATTICE** — `C-0009`'s grillage with the consumed crossovers removed (`C-0054`'s
   `OrigamiGrillage.consumedCrossovers`). The reference: it has neither reduction baked into it.
3. **BEAMS** — the same grillage with **every** crossover consumed: fifteen Euler-Bernoulli duplexes on
   one shared Winkler foundation, with no across-helix load path at all.

The verdict is the consumption at which **BEAMS** becomes the nearer model to **LATTICE** than **PLATE**
is. That is a model-selection statement, not a criterion, and it is what "what replaces it" means.

**Method justification against cost.** Every ingredient is a standing library — `OrigamiGrillage`,
`PlateOnFoundation`, `ConsumedCrossoverSheet`, `edgeCollarPressure` — so the whole task is a study main
plus one small model file. The alternative (a fresh homogenisation theory for a sparse lattice) would
cost an iteration and could not be checked against anything.

**What would falsify this approach:** if the lattice at the ceiling sat *nearer the plate* than the
uncoupled beam array on every load case, then depletion would be a change of parameter after all and
`C-0054`'s harmonic/smeared discrepancy would be a modelling artefact. That is a real possible outcome
and it is checked, not assumed.

## The five gates

1. **Dimensional.** Every criterion is a pure ratio; the patch count is an area over an area. Scaling
   every length by `λ` and `k_f` by `λ^(-4)` leaves all four invariant. Unphysical arguments throw.
2. **Limiting cases.** **Zero consumption reproduces `C-0009`'s published table exactly** — 0.83, 1.50,
   0.37, 3.9 at nominal and 1.18/2.12/7.9 and 0.59/1.06/2.0 at the ends of its foundation sweep. **A
   uniform load dishes a free tile exactly zero** at every consumption level in all three models — the
   strongest falsifier available (`CLAUDE.md`). Full consumption leaves the BEAMS model identically.
3. **Symmetry and conservation.** Global force balance at every level and in all three models. The
   inversions round-trip: evaluating a criterion at its own inverted count returns the target. **And a
   symmetry that is not a restatement: `ℓ_⊥/d` depends only on `D_⊥/k_f`, so consuming to `N_ret/N = 1/4`
   is EXACTLY a 4× stiffer foundation for criterion C and is NOT for criterion B** — the two levers are
   degenerate in one direction and split by 2.8× in the other.
4. **Numerical convergence.** Nested mesh 1 ⊂ 2 ⊂ 4 (`CLAUDE.md`: never 1/2/3/4); the crossover link
   penalty over `1e3 … 1e6`; the plate's Ritz basis degree 8/10/12; the patch census over the sample
   grid. The result file re-run through `tools/study.sh` and diffed.
5. **Literature and upstream.** `C-0009`'s four criteria, its `D_⊥`, its anisotropy and its
   `1.015467` lattice/continuum ratio; `C-0054`'s ceiling, its `(15/14)²` identity and its dishing at 0
   and 42 spent; `C-0047`'s 0.218 and 0.695 under the same solved profile; `C-0022`'s 0.308 free tile;
   `C-0010`'s 2.5 % `D_⊥` insensitivity. Lattice constants cited to Rothemund (2006), Fischer et al.
   (2016) and Chen et al. (2014) as in `C-0009`.

## Acceptance

**PASS** if all four criteria are re-derived at `N_ret = 14`, each inverted to the count it crosses 1 at,
the intact sheet reproduces `C-0009` exactly, and the model-selection crossing is located under
`C-0022`'s solved load with the consequences for `C-0006`, `C-0010` and `C-0047` stated at that count.
