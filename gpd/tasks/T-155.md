## Formulate

### The question

`C-0087` settled `CH-0084`'s flatness half **negatively and unanimously**: under Strauss et al.'s
measured per-staple incorporation map, not one of sixty `placement × convention × mandate` cells
is inside `T-5b`'s 0.10 even at the median, and the lowest exceedance anywhere is 89.6 % of
realisations.

It also said, in one line, why:

> **An exhaustively optimised placement is a cancellation, and a cancellation has no tolerance to
> a missing term.**

Removing **exactly one** of `C-0063`'s 34 paths takes the dishing 0.0706 → **0.5010** of the
stroke; one of `C-0058`'s 45 takes 0.0753 → **0.3060**. And the dropout **reverses the ranking**
of the two standing flat designs, because the denser array loses less per absent path.

`C-0087` **grades** the four standing designs; it does not **search**. So:

**Is there a coupling placement and distribution whose 90th-percentile dishing under `C-0087`'s
measured dropout is inside `T-5b`'s 0.10 — or is no Gen-1 coupling flat at the state of the art in
folding?**

### The numeric target

The **90th percentile** of the peak dishing over the free-tile stroke, over seeded Bernoulli
dropout realisations, against `T-5b`'s **0.10** convention.

**Why the 90th percentile.** `C-0087` reports p90 and names the choice as open (its item 4:
*"nothing upstream says what fraction of built tiles a design may lose"*). This task **optimises
the same statistic** so that its answer is comparable with `C-0087`'s grades cell for cell, and
reports p50, p95, the worst realisation and the exceedance probability beside it so that another
choice of yield can be read off the same file. A **mean** would be the wrong objective for the
reason `C-0087` found: the distribution is right-skewed and the design question is about the tail.

### The operating state — named, because a flatness verdict is owed one

This project has recorded seven times that a quantity is not well posed without the state it is
read at. The state here is **`C-0022`'s solved 2 mM / 10 nm / 0.192 V** profile — `C-0063`'s and
`C-0087`'s design state — with the **held end of `C-0068`'s range**, 2 mM / 7 nm / 0.192 V,
carried beside it so that the verdict is reported both at the design state and over the range the
placed 2 mM device traverses. Every headline is the **design state** unless a row says otherwise.

### The acceptance predicate, falsifiable

1. **`P1`** — the cheap bounds run and are reported **before** any sampler, and each says what it
   settles: the worst single-path removal (`n` solves), the **oracle floor under dropout** (a
   rigorous per-realisation lower bound over *every* distribution), and the run-length pitch
   arithmetic (no solve at all).
2. **`P2`** — the objective optimised is a **percentile**, stated, with the whole distribution
   reported and not a point; and the percentile-optimised design is compared against the
   zero-defect-optimised one on the **same** grading ensemble.
3. **`P3`** — the three axes `C-0087` names as the direction are priced **separately**: *denser*
   (a path-count sweep), *more regular* (a grid against a lattice placement at matched count),
   and **redundant** (what a path count above the mandate's need buys, and whether the tile can
   carry it — against `C-0069`'s 8.19 nm plan budget, `C-0074`'s lattice ceiling and `C-0053`'s
   packer).
4. **`P4`** — a **recommendation**, or the statement that no Gen-1 coupling is flat under the
   measured dropout together with what would have to change for it to be.
5. **`P5`** — every standing figure reproduces at **zero dropout** (`C-0063`'s 0.0706, `C-0058`'s
   0.0753, `C-0074`'s 0.0682, `C-0017`'s 0.2182) and `C-0087`'s own Monte Carlo reproduces at the
   same seed and sample count, at a departure reported and not asserted.
6. **`P6`** — the effect on `C-0063`, `C-0068`, `C-0058`, `C-0060`, `C-0071` and `ANSWERS.md`
   §4(g) is stated, as a **challenge** where a verdict moves.

### Units, geometry and sign conventions — restated, not inherited

- Lengths **nm**, forces **pN**, stiffness **pN/nm**, pressure **pN/nm² = 1 MPa exactly**;
  `k_BT = 4.141947 pN·nm` at **300 K**, aqueous **2 mM MgCl₂**.
- `x` runs **along** the helices, `y` **across** them, `z` normal and positive **upward**; `w` is
  positive **downward**; the origin is the tile centre.
- **Dishing** is the peak absolute departure from the area-weighted least-squares best-fit
  **plane**, on the same **81 × 81** grid as every flatness claim upstream. **Flat** means below
  **0.10** of the free-tile stroke — `T-5b`'s **convention**, not a physical threshold.
- **A dropout is a REMOVAL**: an absent path has no stiffness and is solved as an **absent
  station**, which is exact superposition (`C-0087`, `InfluenceSurrogate.solveWithDropout`).
- Realisations are **independent** across stations — `C-0087`'s convention, carried, and its
  limitation carried with it.
- `C-0017`'s **33.3333 pN/nm** is an equality on a **SUM** at §3's **acceptable 3 nm** stroke.
- The random stream is `C-0087`'s **SplitMix64 at seed 20260817** for grading, so that the
  reproduction of its published percentiles is bit-exact; an **independent** seed is used for the
  training ensemble an optimiser sees, so that every quoted percentile is **out of sample**.

## Plan

### The cheap bounds, and why each runs before what it precedes

**`B1` — the worst single-path removal, `n` solves, no sampling.** `C-0087`'s own bound, extended
from two designs to every candidate here, and used as a **cheap objective** in its own right: it
is `n + 1` surrogate solves against a sampler's thousands, so if it ranks candidates the way the
percentile does, a placement search can be run on it. Whether it does is measured (a rank
correlation against p90 over the whole candidate set) rather than assumed.

**`B2` — the oracle floor under dropout, and it can settle the question outright.**
`InfluenceSurrogate.reachableDishingFloor` is the least-squares-optimal RMS dishing over *all*
force vectors at a station set. Evaluated over the **surviving** stations of a realisation it is a
lower bound on that realisation's peak dishing for **every** stiffness distribution — indeed for
an oracle that is allowed to choose a different distribution per realisation. Percentiles are
monotone under a pointwise bound, so

> if the **p90 of the oracle floor** exceeds `T-5b`'s 0.10, then **no distribution whatever** on
> that station set is flat at the 90th percentile, and no search is needed.

It costs `O(n³)` per realisation from one precomputed Gram matrix, so the whole bound is cheaper
than a single Monte Carlo cell. `C-0087`'s `F4` is the same bound at full presence (0.00548); this
is the version that can actually fire.

**`B3` — the run-length pitch arithmetic, with no solve at all.** `CLAUDE.md`: *"an attachment
coupling can be a NET DISHING SOURCE, and the sign flips at an attachment pitch of one Winkler
bending length"*, `ℓ_∥ = (D_∥/k_f)^(1/4)`. A dropout **is** an increase in the attachment pitch, so
a design robust to `j` consecutive absences in a row needs `columns ≥ (j + 1)·edgeX/ℓ_∥`, and the
`j` a design must survive at the 90th percentile is the 90th percentile of the **longest run of
absences** over the array — a combinatorial statistic of the ensemble, computed with no lattice at
all. This predicts the *density* the dropout demands before any solve, and is the arithmetic that
turns the redundancy axis into a number.

**`B4` — the plan ceiling, read from the claims that own it.** `C-0075`'s self-consistent count
table (`gpd/results/T-138-…json`): the arm is placed, `L ∝ n^(1/3)`, and a row of three roots caps
a rooted element at `pitch − d = 8.19 nm`. If `B3` asks for a density the tile cannot build, the
answer is settled from the plan side and the sweep above the ceiling is a statement about a
*different* tile.

### The expensive part, and its cost justified

Only after the bounds: a Monte Carlo on `C-0087`'s pipeline, with the objective moved to a
percentile. The costs are one bank per `(station set, state)` — a Cholesky of the unsupported host
plus one back-substitution and one 81 × 81 sampling per station — and then `realisations × n ×
6561` fused multiply-adds per candidate distribution. At 10 000 realisations and 34 paths that is
2.2 G FMA per cell, which is seconds; an `n`-parameter coordinate descent over the percentile is
therefore run on a **400-realisation training ensemble** at an independent seed and validated at
10 000 on `C-0087`'s own seed, which is both cheaper and honest (an in-sample percentile optimum is
not a result).

One-parameter families (`C-0058`'s rim × R, the `1/p` compensation, load-matched) are swept
exhaustively rather than descended, because they are the **buildable** ones (`C-0060`: two levels
are buildable, 45 free stiffnesses are three to four staple lengths) and because a sweep of twenty
points is cheaper than a descent.

### What would falsify this approach

- **`F1` (the declared falsifier)** — **the density axis is not monotone**: if the 90th percentile
  does *not* fall as the path count rises, then *denser* is not the direction `C-0087` reads out of
  its own ranking reversal, the mechanism named in that claim is wrong, and this task's whole
  premise — that redundancy is what buys robustness — has to be withdrawn and replaced by a
  statement about which placements happen to be lucky.
- **`F2`** — the percentile objective buys nothing: if the distribution optimised on p90 is no
  better than the one optimised at zero defects, the objective was not what `C-0087`'s open item 1
  says it is, and the deliverable is a smaller statement.
- **`F3`** — the uncoupled tile dishes non-zero under a **uniform** load. A free plate on a uniform
  foundation translates exactly; a non-zero here is a solver defect, not physics.
- **`F4`** — a standing zero-dropout figure fails to reproduce, or `C-0087`'s published p90 fails
  to reproduce at its own seed and sample count.
- **`F5`** — the oracle floor `B2` exceeds 0.10 at every station set, settling the question with no
  search at all. This is the *good* kind of firing: it would make the negative rigorous over all
  distributions rather than over a searched family.

### What this task will NOT do

- It will not re-open the incorporation field: `C-0087`'s five conventions are consumed as they
  stand, with **`MEASURED_DEPTH`** (the least pessimistic position-dependent one) as the headline
  and **`UNIFORM`** as `CH-0084`'s baseline.
- It will not model correlation between staples. `C-0087`'s independence convention is carried and
  its limitation is carried with it.
- It will not change `T-5b`'s 0.10, which is a convention.

## Result

Filed as [`C-0089`](../claims/C-0089-dropout-robust-placement.md).
**The answer is NO**, and it is a **count** that refuses it:
over 22 graded `placement × distribution` cells the lowest 90th-percentile dishing is
**0.284537599** of the stroke against `T-5b`'s 0.10, at 100 % exceedance.

**The declared falsifier `F1` did NOT fire** — the density axis is monotone at every one of five
steps, 0.8522 → 0.5327 over 15 → 90 paths — and neither did `F2`, `F3`, `F4` or `F5`.
`F5`'s not firing is the load-bearing one: the oracle floor reaches only 0.00111–0.01988, so the
negative is a statement about **fixed** distributions and not about the geometry, which is
[`CH-0104`](../challenges/CH-0104-a-reachable-floor-is-an-oracle-and-cannot-license-a-design.md).

Also raises
[`CH-0103`](../challenges/CH-0103-the-path-count-recommendation-runs-against-fabrication.md), and
queues **`T-162`** (a coupling that is not an array) and **`T-163`** (the fixed-geometry
path-count sweep) — both renumbered up from `T-158`/`T-159`, which two sibling agents took in the
same iteration.
