## Formulate

### The question

Three claims — [`C-0072`](../claims/C-0072-plan-tolerance-model.md),
[`C-0074`](../claims/C-0074-two-per-row-placement.md),
[`C-0075`](../claims/C-0075-path-count-consistency.md) — recommend reducing the Gen-1 output
coupling from **34** arms to **30**, and all three price the move on **plan margin**:
`C-0069`'s knife edge of **0.0256098233 nm** becomes **1.34951193 nm** under `C-0072`'s own
reduction rule and **1.76451193 nm** at `C-0074`'s recommended phase-8 placement, 68.9× and
5.19 base-pair rises.

`CH-0103` observes that **the path count is the axis fabrication charges on**. Under `C-0087`'s
measured staple dropout `C-0089` finds the 90th-percentile dishing monotone decreasing in the
count — 0.85219673 at 15 paths to 0.532748246 at 90 — so a reduction that buys plan margin
**spends** dropout robustness, and no claim in the corpus contains both terms of that trade.

**But `C-0089`'s count sweep is on the abstract `m × 15` grid.** On the upward lattice the count
and the placement are confounded, and `C-0089`'s own numbers show it: `C-0074`'s 30 roots read
**0.583664426** against `C-0063`'s 34 at **0.639129638**, i.e. the *smaller* count reads *better*
— because it is a different phase whose zero-defect equal-spring design (0.242359741) is 3.4×
worse to begin with. And [`C-0098`](../claims/C-0098-shared-body-placement-and-distribution.md)
has since measured that the transfer from the abstract grid to the real lattice is not benign:
the redundancy slope is **−0.376769756** there against **−0.784357442** on the grids, **2.08×
shallower**, and `C-0089`'s cheap ranking instrument falls from ρ = 0.9729 across *designs* to
**0.468487481** across *phases*.

> **So the question is: at FIXED station geometry, on one crossover phase of the lattice the arms
> actually root on, what is the path count worth under the measured dropout — and does the second
> term of `CH-0103`'s trade survive being measured?**

### The numeric target and the acceptance predicates

> **`P1`** — the **90th-percentile** peak dishing, as a ratio of the free-tile stroke, of an
> equal-spring array at `C-0017`'s unchanged total, under `C-0087`'s measured Bernoulli dropout
> at 10 000 seeded realisations, is emitted at path counts **22, 25, 28, 30, 34 and 45** on a
> **nested** family of station sets at **one** crossover phase of the upward `EAST` lattice.

- **`P2`** — the **cheap bound runs first and is reported whatever it says**: does `C-0089`'s
  single-removal bound (`n` solves, no sampling) rank designs the way the percentile does
  **across counts**, as distinct from across designs at one count? `C-0098` found it does not
  transfer across *phases*; whether it transfers across *counts* is open, cheap, and worth as
  much as the sweep.
- **`P3`** — the count and the placement are **separated**: the nested chain holds the geometry
  and moves only the count; a subset search at each fixed count moves only the placement. Both
  are graded on the same ensemble.
- **`P4`** — the two axes are priced **in one table**: what 34 → 30 buys in plan margin (read at
  run time from `C-0075`'s own count table) against what it costs at the 90th percentile.
- **`P5`** — a verdict on `CH-0103`: upheld, or the reduction stands. Either is a real answer,
  and the ground of whichever it is must be stated.
- **`P6`** — every standing figure reproduces rather than being transcribed: `C-0063`'s
  **0.0706145537**, `C-0074`'s **0.0682**, `C-0072`'s 30-root **0.260281397**, `C-0087`'s
  **0.639129638** and **0.501011167**, `C-0089`'s **0.583664426**, `C-0026`'s **4.90731102 nm**,
  `C-0047`'s **12.8290845 nm**.
- **`P7`** — the falsifiers are executable tests.

### Locked units, geometry and sign conventions

- Lengths **nm**, forces **pN**, stiffness **pN/nm**, pressure **pN/nm² = 1 MPa exactly**;
  `k_BT = 4.141947 pN·nm` at **300 K** in aqueous **2 mM MgCl₂**.
- `x` runs **along** the helices, `y` **across** them, `z` normal and positive **upward** — away
  from the grafted layer, which is **below** the tile. `w` is positive **downward**; the origin
  is the tile centre.
- **Dishing** is the peak absolute departure from the area-weighted least-squares best-fit
  **plane**, on the same **81 × 81** grid as every flatness claim upstream. **Flat** means below
  **0.10** of the free-tile stroke — `T-5b`'s **convention**, not a physical threshold.
- **A dropout is a REMOVAL, not a perturbation** (`C-0087`): an absent path is solved as an
  absent station, which is exact superposition.
- **The operating state is named**: `C-0022`'s **solved 2 mM / 10 nm / 0.192 V** profile — the
  design state of `C-0063`, `C-0087`, `C-0089` and `C-0098`. The **eleventh** time this programme
  has had to say which state a flatness verdict is read at.
- **The verdict statistic is the 90th percentile**, a nearest-rank order statistic, and it is
  `C-0087`'s, `C-0089`'s and `C-0098`'s, so every cell is comparable with theirs.
- **The distribution is EQUAL springs at `C-0017`'s unchanged 33.3333333 pN/nm SUM.** That is
  `C-0089`'s Deliverable 1 convention and it is the one that isolates the count: a distribution
  is a redistribution of the same budget and `C-0098` has already priced that axis separately.
- **The host is ONE phase — 24**, `C-0063`'s own: centro-symmetric, an eight-column sheet, and
  the phase the recommendation moves *from*. Phase 8 is carried only for the two reference cells
  that reproduce `C-0074`'s recommended design.

### The geometry convention that makes this task different from `C-0089`

`C-0089` draws a **separate** Bernoulli ensemble per station set. Two counts then differ both in
their design and in their fabrication outcomes, and the difference between two nearby counts is
partly a sampling difference. Here the ensemble is drawn **once over the whole 53-site inventory
at phase 24** and **restricted** to each subset, so two nested designs see literally the same
staple present or absent at every station they share — **common random numbers**, which is what
makes a 34 → 30 difference of a few per cent readable at all. The published per-set convention is
carried beside it at the 34-root cell so the two are calibrated against each other.

## Plan

### The cheap bounds, in the order they run

1. **The rank transfer of `C-0089`'s single-removal bound, across counts.** `n` surrogate solves
   per design and no sampling. `C-0089` measures ρ = 0.9729 across 22 *designs at mixed counts*;
   `C-0098` measures 0.468487481 across *phases*. Here it is measured three ways — across the
   whole graded set, **within one chain across counts**, and **within one count across
   placements** — which is the decomposition neither upstream claim has. Cost: ~600 surrogate
   solves against 210 000 for the sampler.
2. **The redundancy division, read off `C-0089`'s own published curve before anything is run.**
   A log-log fit of its six abstract-grid points predicts what 34 → 30 must cost. If the
   predicted cost is a few per cent against a 68.9× gain in plan margin, the trade is settled by
   arithmetic and the sweep is confirmatory — which is what a cheap bound is for.
3. **The pitch and run-length arithmetic on the real lattice**, `C-0089`'s Bound 3 read at the
   counts this task sweeps. The upward line's pitch is the bare 32 bp, so a row carries at most
   four sites and at these counts one to three; `C-0089`'s demand is 13 columns.

### The method, and why it is this one

The whole sweep is **slices of one influence bank**. `C-0058`'s `InfluenceSurrogate` is an exact
Woodbury reduction and the stations enter an `OrigamiGrillage` solve as *loads*, so the host is
factorised **once** over all 53 candidate sites at phase 24 and every candidate placement costs
an `n × n` Cholesky rather than an 855-degree-of-freedom solve (`CLAUDE.md`: *"a placement sweep
is a BANK problem, not a solve problem"*). Superposition is exact, so the sliced surrogate must
reproduce an independently assembled one at departure ~0 — asserted, or the sweep is on the wrong
object.

The nested chain is **`C-0072`'s own reduction rule**, `rowsWithoutInteriorRoots`, extended
upward by its exact inverse. So the 34-root member **is** `C-0063`'s published placement and the
30-root member **is** `C-0072`'s published reduction of it; the chain is not a construction of
this task's but the programme's own recommendation embedded in a count sweep. A second,
**centro-symmetric** chain runs beside it so the count effect can be separated from the asymmetry
the interior rule introduces below 30.

The arm footprint is **reported, not imposed**. An arm's length is a function of the count
(`C-0075`: `L` grows as `n^(1/3)` at a fixed mandate), so buildability is a *plan* axis that
`C-0069`/`C-0075` already own; conflating it with the station set is what made `C-0089`'s 45-path
row unreadable. Each cell carries `armLength`, `planMargin` and `placesWithItsOwnArm`, read at
run time from `gpd/results/T-138-path-count-consistency.json`.

### Justification against cost

The expensive object is the sampler: 21 graded cells × 10 000 realisations. The bank makes each
realisation an `n × n` Cholesky plus one pass over the 81 × 81 dishing field, so a cell is
seconds rather than minutes. The two cheap bounds run first and both can settle the question on
their own: bound 1 licenses (or refuses) the cheap objective for the subset search, and bound 2
predicts the answer to the actual trade before any realisation is drawn.

### What result would falsify this approach

| # | falsifier | what it would mean |
|---|---|---|
| **`F1`** | **the declared one** — at fixed station geometry on the upward lattice the 90th percentile is **not monotone decreasing** in the path count over 22 → 45 | `C-0089`'s density axis does not transfer to the lattice the arms root on, and `CH-0103`'s premise — that a count reduction spends robustness — is withdrawn rather than priced |
| **`F2`** | `C-0089`'s single-removal bound does not transfer **across counts**: Spearman ρ below **0.8** on the fixed-geometry chain | the cheap objective cannot be used to search a count axis, and `C-0098`'s ρ = 0.468 across phases is the general case rather than the exception. Reported either way |
| **`F3`** | the uncoupled tile under a **uniform** load dishes non-zero | the solver is wrong, not the physics (`CLAUDE.md`) |
| **`F4`** | a standing figure fails to reproduce — `C-0063`'s 0.0706145537, `C-0074`'s 0.0682, `C-0072`'s 0.260281397, `C-0087`'s 0.639129638 | the pipeline is not the one the claims were written on and nothing here is comparable |
| **`F5`** | the cost of 34 → 30 at fixed geometry is **below the ensemble's own convergence departure** | the second term of `CH-0103`'s trade is not measurable at 10 000 realisations: the challenge's cost column is a zero and the recommendation stands untouched on its original ground |

**A negative is a real answer, and so is a null.** If the count is worth a few per cent where the
plan margin is worth 68.9×, this task says the recommendation **survives** and says exactly what
the missing column contains — which is what `CH-0103` asked for and is not the same as agreeing
with it.
