## 1. Formulate

### What makes this pass different from the fourteen before it

Four iterations and ten claims stand between this pass and `C-0210`, and **the answer to §3 row (g)
moved twice more**:

1. **`C-0212`** (`T-316`) showed that every coupled census in the corpus grades on two rules
   *transferred onto* the lattice; searched, the same 32 cells read `22 of 32` flat.
2. **`C-0215`** (`T-322`) showed that those readings are also on a tile the programme does not
   recommend, and on route B's own tile a searched coupling is flat at `27 of 48` and
   **flat AND inside `C-0023`'s per-path allowable at `7`** — the first non-empty conjunction this
   corpus has had.

Two more move the *other* cross-section and the numbers three documents quote for it:
**`C-0218`** (`T-294`) grades `15 × 4` coupled on the tied lattice, discharging a leave `C-0186`
took deliberately; and **`C-0219`** / **`CH-0282`** (`T-330`) correct three published `15 × 4`
free-tile readings, because a dishing decomposition that removes its rigid plane by three
*independent* projections is a least-squares fit only at **even** raster-row counts.

### The hypotheses this pass exists to falsify

- That the two deliverables already carry iterations 50–51 (they carry **49** and nothing after it).
- That a *"22 of 32 flat"* or *"27 of 48 flat"* sentence can be quoted without its three
  companions — the per-path conjunction, the uncoupled comparison, and what the coupling is *for*.
- That the challenge-and-claim census, stale in seven of eight passes, is right this time.
- That the checker census's predicate is now correct, `C-0210` having replaced it once.
- That the *"what we cannot answer"* list is current against the queue.
- That the eight — now nine — decisions are priced on numbers that still stand.

### The standing failure modes this pass must assume, from `CLAUDE.md`

- **A census is dated by its premise set AND by its predicate** (`CH-0182`, `CH-0266`), so every
  self-describing count is derived at a named state and the derivation travels with it.
- **A correcting sentence has to NAME the withdrawn premise** (`CH-0230`), so `tools/T-234-census.py`
  **grows** under a synthesis by construction and must be re-run and settled as the close-out.
- **A discharge is invisible to whoever files the removal**, so the *cannot answer* list is re-read
  against the queue rather than against memory.
- **A document listing what the programme cannot do under-claims**, so its disclaimers are checked
  as hard as its assertions.
- **`strike, never delete`**, so every superseded value stays, struck, with the correction beside it.

## 2. Plan

### Order, and why this order

1. Apply `T-330`'s hand-off — four passages carrying one corrected triple — and **verify the count
   independently** rather than trusting the hand-off, because a hand-off is a claim like any other.
2. Carry iterations 50–51 into §1, §3 row (g) and both decision tables, in the order the claims
   arrived, so that no reader meets a relocation without the withdrawal that followed it.
3. Prosecute all nine decisions for a stale price, and re-read the **status** of every challenge a
   decision prices on.
4. Derive the three self-describing counts. Do not assert them.
5. Re-read the *cannot answer* list against the queue and against the documents' own rows.
6. Run every retained checker, enumerated at the moment of running, before and after.
7. Settle `tools/T-234-census.py` last, as the close-out.

### Method justification against cost

The expensive half — grading, re-emitting, re-deriving — is **already done** by the ten claims this
pass reads. What is left is a reconciliation, and its only leverage is completeness. Two cheap
instruments carry it: a `grep` of each new identifier against both documents (which showed
iteration 49 carried and 50–51 absent, in seconds), and an emitter that **refuses to build** unless
every declared passage's two anchors are asserted against both trees. The second is inherited from
`T-319`, which inherited it from `T-276`, and it fires on the author rather than on the reader.

**What would falsify the approach**: if the anchor discipline passed on an edit that had not been
made — i.e. if a declared passage could be counted without existing. It cannot: the after anchor is
asserted **absent at the ref and present at its declared multiplicity in the tree**.
