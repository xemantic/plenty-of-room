# T-138 — `C-0069`'s path-count sensitivity re-sizes the element but not the array

**Leaf** `A8.2`, with `A1.2`.

**Raised by** [`C-0072`](../claims/C-0072-plan-tolerance-model.md)'s open item 8, which recorded the
defect rather than challenging it: `C-0017`'s mandate is a stiffness on a **sum**, so a path count
`n` sizes the element *and* counts the instances, and
[`C-0069`](../claims/C-0069-output-element-placement.md)'s Deliverable 5 changes the first while
holding the second at 34.

**Read together with [`T-136`](T-136.md)** — the same identity is what hid `T-136`'s escape, because
holding the array at 34 pins the plan ceiling at `C-0069`'s 8.19 nm in every row of that table.

---

## Formulate

### The question, as a predicate

> **P1 (the bookkeeping).** Re-read `C-0069`'s Deliverable 5 with the instance count tied to the
> path count, and put `k_delivered / 33.3333` beside every row. What does the self-consistent table
> look like, and how far from the mandate is the published one?

> **P2 (no headline moves).** Every `C-0069` deliverable is read at the self-consistent `n = 34`.
> **Verify** that rather than assume it: the reference row, the six cheap bounds, the catalogue and
> the two-restraint window must all be unchanged.

> **P3 (the coupled reading).** At 30 roots the self-consistent per-path stiffness is
> `33.3333/30 = 1.1111 pN/nm`, which **re-sizes the arm**. Does the re-sized arm still place 30
> times, and what is the plan margin then? `C-0072`'s 1.3495 nm was computed at *its own*
> plan-rule reduction and at the **34**-arm ceiling convention; check whether it survives the
> re-sizing, and on which placement it is read.

### What "delivered" means, and why it is a third number

There are **three** counts in play and `C-0069`'s table conflates two of them:

| symbol | meaning |
|---|---|
| `n` | the path count the element is **sized** at — it sets the per-path secant `33.3333/n` |
| `N_demanded` | how many instances the array asks the lattice for |
| `N_placed` | how many the plan model actually admits |

The load is carried by what is **built**, so the delivered total is `N_placed · 33.3333/n`, and
`C-0017`'s equality holds only where `N_placed = n`. That makes the ratio `N_placed/n` a column of
the table, not a footnote — and it is a column `C-0069` does not have, in either of its two
readings.

### Declared falsifier

**A `C-0069` headline number that moves under the self-consistent reading.** Then this is a
correction to a claim rather than to a presentation, the verdict on `Q5` is in question, and the
challenge is against the claim's conclusions rather than against its Deliverable 5.

### Locked units and conventions

As `T-136`, and identical to `C-0069`'s: lengths **nm**, forces **pN**, stiffness **pN/nm**;
`k_BT = 4.141947 pN·nm` at **300 K** in aqueous **2 mM MgCl₂**; 40.0 × 40.35 nm single-layer
square-lattice sheet, 15 duplexes at 2.69 nm, 0.34 nm rise, crossover phase 24; `C-0017`'s
**33.3333 pN/nm** as a **sum** at §3's **acceptable 3 nm** stroke; `C-0053`'s footprint convention.

## Plan

### The cheap bound, which is the whole of P1

`k_delivered/33.3333 = N_placed/n` — one division per row, and it needs no model at all. It is run
first and it is what says whether the defect is a presentation error or a physical one.

### The method

- **Re-run `C-0069`'s own pipeline** rather than retype its table: `elasticaArmForStiffness` for the
  arm at each count and each sensitivity axis, `rootedLengthCeiling`/`armDirections` for the plan,
  `rowOfThreeLengthCeiling` for the closed form. Every published cell is reproduced before any new
  cell is added, so a departure is visible.
- **Add the self-consistent reading beside the published one**, not instead of it: two blocks of
  the same eight axes, one at `N_demanded = 34` (as published) and one at `N_demanded = n`.
- **The count vector and the ceiling per count come from `T-136`'s closed form**
  (`maximumPlanCeilingForCount`), which is exact and needs no placement — that is what lets P3 be
  answered without re-running a placement sweep, and it is what shows `C-0069`'s 8.19 nm is a
  property of a 34-instance array and not of the lattice.
- **P2 is a set of equalities**, asserted as tests: the reference row, the six bounds and the
  two-restraint window must reproduce bit-for-bit at `n = 34`, where the two readings coincide by
  construction.

### What would falsify this approach

- **`C-0069`'s published cells failing to reproduce.** Then the re-read is not a re-read.
- **The self-consistent reading changing a verdict at `n = 34`.** The two readings are identical
  there by construction; if they are not, the pipeline is not `C-0069`'s.
- **`maximumPlanCeilingForCount` disagreeing with `rootedLengthCeiling` on a concrete placement**
  (it must be ≥ it, always, and equal on a ceiling-optimal one).

### Cost

Seconds. One elastica root solve per row (each ~0.1 s) and a handful of bisections. This is
bookkeeping with a model attached, and it is ranked **low** for exactly that reason — but it is
what makes `T-136`'s escape legible, so it is done in the same iteration and in the same code.

## Verification gates

| gate | what will be checked |
|---|---|
| **1 — dimensional** | `k_delivered` is a stiffness and `N_placed/n` is dimensionless; the arm is a **cube** root of the per-path stiffness; unphysical counts throw |
| **2 — limiting cases** | at `N_placed = n` the delivered total **is** the mandate, exactly; a count vector with any row of three has ceiling exactly `pitch − d`; one root per row has no row-of-three ceiling at all |
| **3 — symmetry and conservation** | the two readings coincide **identically** at `n = 34`; the closed-form ceiling per count is ≥ every concrete placement's ceiling at that count and equal for the ceiling-optimal one |
| **4 — numerical convergence** | the elastica arm over RK4 steps 200/400/800; the ceiling bisection over resolutions |
| **5 — literature and upstream** | every one of `C-0069`'s eight Deliverable-5 rows and `C-0072`'s nine count rows re-derived |

## Acceptance

**PASS** if the self-consistent table is emitted with `k_delivered/33.3333` on every row, P2 is
**verified** rather than asserted, and P3 returns a number on the placement `T-136` supplies.
