## Formulate

### The question, as a predicate

`C-0071` recommends `C-0069`'s `Q5` — a hinge-rooted arm, **8.16439 nm = 24.0 bp**, root one antiparallel
crossover (13.5294 pN·nm/rad), tip `C-0034`'s `A2` duplex end (78.2353 pN·nm/rad), **34 instances**, placed at
§3's **acceptable** clause (100 pN over 3 nm).
`C-0018` computed §6 task 4's fold for the **affine** mandate and `C-0032` for `C-0030`'s strain-**softening**
flexure; **`Q5` is a third law and its fold has never been computed.**

**Acceptance.** For the device `C-0071` recommends — one buffer, the 10 nm layer at `σ = 0.024 nm⁻²`, one bias,
`L₀ → L₀ − 3 nm` — report

1. the **fold's own stroke** against §3's 3 nm target, per layer model, with the load line named;
2. the **bias margin** `V_pull-in / V_operating`, on the bias axis, with the load line named;
3. both **at 2 mM and at 0.5 mM**, since `DECISIONS-FOR-NDI` decision 1 turns on exactly this margin;
4. a **cheap sign bound** run before the sweep, and whether it agreed with the sweep;
5. the verdict against `CH-0083` and what it does to `C-0071`'s recommendation, to failure route `R7` and to
   §6 task 4;

— **or** the plain statement that §6 task 4 cannot be closed for the recommended device, with what blocks it.

### Units and conventions, restated rather than inherited

- Lengths **nm**, forces **pN**, stiffness **pN/nm**, rotational stiffness **pN·nm/rad**, potential **V**,
  concentration **mM**; `k_BT = 4.141947 pN·nm` at **300 K** in aqueous **MgCl₂** at the stated concentration.
- `z` is normal to the electrode, positive **away** from it; the tile-electrode gap **is** the layer height
  (`C-0012`).
- The **stroke** `s = L₀ − h` is positive **downward**, toward the electrode; `L₀` is a **force-onset** height at
  a defining load of 1.0 pN over the 40 × 40 nm tile (`C-0011`, `CH-0010`).
- A **load line** `R(s)` is positive **upward**, in pN over the **whole array**. Its **secant** `R(s)/s` is what
  §3's placement clause is written on and its **tangent** `dR/ds` is what the stability clause is written on;
  they are the same number only for an affine line through the origin.
  `C-0017`'s 33.3333 pN/nm is a **sum**, so the path count does not enter the load line at all — it enters the
  per-path allowables, which `C-0071` already discharges.
- The equilibrium path is parametrised by the **stroke**: at each `s` there is one bias `V_eq(s)` that puts an
  equilibrium there, and the fold is `max_s V_eq(s)`. Differentiating the balance at `V′(s) = 0` gives
  `k_c(s) + k_eff(s) = 0` **exactly**, so the argmax *is* the tangency point and the two routes to it are
  numerically independent (`C-0018`). **For a nonlinear line `k_c` is the tangent AT THAT STROKE, not a constant.**
- `k_es = −∂F_z/∂h` is **negative** above the force maximum and **positive** below it (`CH-0011`).
- **A bias ceiling belongs to a `(bias, load line)` pair**, never to the bias alone (`CH-0015`), and a fold is
  quoted with **both** its bias and its stroke, because a nonlinear load line moves a fold in its stroke far
  more than in its bias (`C-0032`) and the two are different tests.
- **A device is one buffer, one layer, one bias and `L₀ → L₀ − s*`** (`C-0064`, `C-0068`). States belonging to
  different devices are **not** intersected. `C-0071`'s device is the **10 nm** layer: its 34-root placement is
  phase 24, which `C-0068` shows is selected by that layer and is poor at 5 nm.
- **A requirement on a coupling law is owed over `[0, s*]`, the strokes the device traverses** (`C-0049`), and
  `s* = 3 nm` because `C-0071` is written at the acceptable clause.

### What is being asked of the element, and what is not

`Q5` is an **inextensible** arm of contour 8.16439 nm. It therefore has a **kinematic ceiling** on the stroke
it can be asked about at all, below `C-0018`'s own layer-derived stroke ceiling of ~9.4 nm at the 10 nm layer;
and `C-0039`'s shooting solver enumerates only the **small-rotation branch**, so it refuses before the contour.
That refusal is a **model boundary and must be reported as one** — a branch that ends there has not been shown
to have no fold, only to have none inside the element model's own range.

## Plan

### The cheap bound, which runs first, and its cost justification

`CLAUDE.md` records that **at a fold the composition of two corrections is EXACT, not first order**, because the
baseline coupled tangent vanishes there by construction (`k_c + k_eff = 0` is what located it). The substitution
`affine → Q5` is exactly two corrections applied to `C-0018`'s baseline:

1. a **slope** change, `Δk_c = k_Q5(s*) − 33.3333` at the baseline fold stroke `s*`;
2. a **level** change, `ΔR = R_Q5(s*) − 33.3333 s*`, which moves the bias the path needs at `s*` and therefore
   moves `k_es` there.

The first is **one evaluation of the element's tangent** and needs no field solve at all. Its sign is the
prediction: if `Δk_c > 0` the coupled tangent at the baseline fold is now **positive**, so that stroke is on the
**stable** side and the fold moves **deeper**; if `Δk_c < 0` it moves shallower. The second needs a re-solve and
is what the sweep supplies. **Cost: one elastica solve against ~50 Poisson-Boltzmann solves per path point and
~50 path points per fold search — four orders of magnitude, and it runs before anything is compiled into a
sweep.** This is the same discipline `C-0033` used to get the *sign* of the collar correction for free.

A second cheap bound, offered by `CH-0083` and **declined**: bracketing `Q5`'s fold between `C-0018`'s affine and
`C-0032`'s stiffening reading on the grounds that its tangent minimum lies between theirs. That needs a
monotonicity of the fold in the tangent which `C-0032` observed and did not prove, and — decisively — `Q5`'s
tangent is **not** between theirs at the fold stroke, only at zero stroke. It is reported as a bound that does
not apply, not as one that was not tried.

### The expensive calculation, and why it is affordable

`C-0018`'s `EquilibriumPath` takes its load as an **arbitrary function of the stroke**, so the solver is re-used
unchanged, exactly as `C-0032` re-used it. The only new object is the load line. The sweep is
3 layer heights × 3 buffers × 6 `C-0003` layer models × 2 load lines = **108 fold searches**, half of
`C-0032`'s 216, and the affine half reproduces `C-0018` state by state so the comparison is of one device rather
than of two studies.

**Measure the inner loop before choosing the outer budget.** One `Q5` reaction is a shooting solve on
`C-0039`'s elastica; one path point is a ~40-step bisection on the diffuse drop, each step a 2000-node
Poisson-Boltzmann Newton solve. The elastica is expected to be the *cheap* half, which is the opposite of what
`CH-0083` assumed, so the tabulate-and-interpolate route it suggests is **not** taken — an interpolation would
add an error term (`CH-0083`'s own falsifier for it: 0.2 nm of fold stroke) for no measurable saving. The
measured per-call costs are reported in the result file.

### The five verification gates

1. **Dimensional** — a load line is pN against nm and its tangent pN/nm; a margin is a ratio and is invariant
   under a common rescaling of the two biases; unphysical arguments throw.
2. **Limiting cases** — an affine line reproduces `AffineLoadLine` exactly; the arm's law at vanishing stroke
   tends to its own small-rotation stiffness `c(ρ_n, ρ_f) EI/L³`; a load line placed at the mandate has secant
   exactly 33.3333 at 3 nm; an arm asked for a stroke past its own contour throws.
3. **Symmetry and conservation** — the placement identity: **every** load line placed at 100 pN over 3 nm must
   locate the **same** operating bias at every state, departure 0.0 (`C-0032`'s `Q1`, which is an identity and
   not a convergence); and the tangency identity `|k_c(s_fold) + k_eff(s_fold)|` at every **interior** fold.
4. **Numerical convergence** — the elastica RK4 step count, the Poisson-Boltzmann mesh, the fold coarse-scan
   resolution and the golden-section stroke bracket, each referred to its own finest setting. `CLAUDE.md`:
   *a golden-section maximum is floored by the noise of the search underneath it*, so the bracket axis is
   expected to stop improving, and **a boundary maximum is not a stationary point** — no tangency residual is
   reported at a branch-start or branch-end fold.
5. **Literature and upstream** — `C-0069`'s `Q5` length, end factor, assembled secant, assembled tangent at
   3 nm and tangent minimum; `C-0017`'s six 2 mM floors; `C-0018`'s pull-in band at 10 nm / 2 mM;
   `C-0032`'s fold strokes and bias margins on the affine line. All re-derived, none read from a result file.

### The declared falsifier

**The cheap sign bound disagrees with the solved fold.**
If `Δk_c = k_Q5(s_fold^affine) − 33.3333 > 0` at the baseline fold and the solved `Q5` fold nonetheless sits at
a **shallower** stroke than the affine one at the same state, then the level change dominates the slope change,
the *"exact composition at a fold"* rule does not carry a nonlinear load line, and the cheap bound must be
withdrawn from this class of problem — a result about the method, reportable in its own right.

A second, weaker falsifier: **that the question is not answerable at all** — that `Q5`'s kinematic ceiling
truncates the path before any fold at 10 nm / 2 mM, in which case §6 task 4 stays open for the recommended
device and the honest answer is that the element model, not the physics, is what blocks it.

### What this task does NOT do

- It does **not** re-derive the layer, the field, the mandate or the element. Every one of those is consumed
  from the claim that owns it.
- It does **not** carry `C-0019`'s one-loop softening of `k_brush` or `C-0022`'s finite-tile enhancement of
  `|F_es|`, exactly as `C-0018` and `C-0032` did not; `C-0033` reports the collar's own contribution at the
  fold separately and its composition is exact there, which is a **separate** correction to compose and not a
  term of this one.
- It does **not** answer `T-63`. It supplies a seventh route to it, on the recommended element rather than on a
  withdrawn one, and says plainly whether that route still points the same way.
