## 1. Formulate

### What makes this pass different from the thirteen before it

`C-0191` carried a **reversal**. This pass is owed over a corpus in which the same headline moved
**twice inside one iteration**, in opposite directions, and neither move is a value substitution:

- [`C-0201`](../claims/C-0201-the-tether-is-a-load-not-a-spring.md)'s free-tile alarm
  (**`0.11296458`**, past `T-5b`'s `0.10`) was **relocated** by
  [`C-0204`](../claims/C-0204-the-anchor-azimuth-is-determined.md) — the tether span is
  **determined** at `0.787091706 nm`, not a bracket, and the collapsed bracket is flat at `16 of 16`
  ([`CH-0256`](../challenges/CH-0256-the-azimuth-is-not-a-convention.md)) — and then **withdrawn**
  outright by [`C-0207`](../claims/C-0207-the-uniform-raster-is-flat-with-its-tethers.md) /
  [`CH-0262`](../challenges/CH-0262-the-alarm-did-not-survive-its-relocation.md): route B's uniform
  raster is flat at **`756 of 756`** cells, because the span reaching the worst corner is a **LEVEL**
  and the dishing is a **FIELD**, and three artifacts made the step between them without taking it.
- [`C-0180`](../claims/C-0180-tied-honeycomb-coupled-regrade.md)'s coupled **`2 of 64`** was reduced
  by [`C-0205`](../claims/C-0205-what-link-stiffness-the-recovery-needs.md) to a reading at a
  **numerical penalty**, and **held there** by
  [`C-0208`](../claims/C-0208-a-bond-link-is-two-mechanisms.md) at the resolved per-bond link.
- [`CH-0265`](../challenges/CH-0265-756-of-756-is-a-reading-at-the-penalty.md) is open **against
  `C-0207`'s own `756 of 756`**, and [`CH-0264`](../challenges/CH-0264-a-per-bond-value-against-a-uniform-threshold.md)
  withdraws `CH-0259`'s straddle.

**Agent E already carried `C-0205`, `C-0208` and `CH-0258` into both documents in the same
iteration**, so the first act of this pass is a census of *what is already there* — `C-0191`'s own
lesson that a census is dated by its premise set, met on a **sibling's edit** rather than on a claim.

### The hypotheses this pass exists to falsify

1. *"A withdrawal is a strike."* It is not: `C-0201`'s alarm has **two** successors that disagree
   about whether it survives, and the second withdraws the first's own reading of it. A document
   that strikes the alarm and writes `C-0204`'s number is **wrong by one iteration**.
2. *"A buried question is still asked."* The width finding — *"the tile is not too floppy, it is too
   small"* — sits inside decision 6, which asks a different question. A reader looking for a width
   specification question will not find it there.
3. *"A self-describing count that did not move is not stale."* The checker census's **number** may
   be unchanged while its **predicate** has moved under it, which is `CH-0243` one level out.

### The standing failure modes this pass must assume, from `CLAUDE.md`

- **Strike, never delete**; grep every number out of the claim that owns it.
- **Quote a ratio's construction beside it** — a superseded ratio reads `CITED`.
- Re-read the **`Status` row** of every challenge a number is priced on, in the range since the last pass.
- A **self-describing count** is the one number a tracer cannot own; derive it at a named ref.
- A **question put to the customer can carry a stale price**, and the customer answers the price.
- A document that **under-claims** is as wrong as one that over-claims and far harder to catch.

## 2. Plan

### Order, and why this order

**The cheap bound runs first and it is two greps, neither of which needs a read of either document:**
the retained checkers (enumerated at the moment of running, not inherited), and one `grep` for each
of the ten identifiers iteration 48 produced. That census either finds a carrier or establishes what
the pass is for.

1. Enumerate and run every retained checker on **both** documents; record the reading **before** any edit.
2. `grep` each of `C-0204`–`C-0209` and `CH-0256`–`CH-0266` out of both documents.
3. Derive the three self-describing counts, **and each one's predicate separately**.
4. Re-read every decision against the `Status` row of every challenge it prices a number on.
5. Read the *"what we cannot answer"* list bullet by bullet against `TASKS.md`.
6. Edit: strike in place, one dated restatement per passage; never rewrite history.
7. Re-run every checker; record the after reading.

### Method justification against cost

The whole pass is `grep`, `python3 tools/*.py` and text editing. No study is run, no Kotlin is
touched, no committed result file of any other task is moved. The expensive alternative — re-running
the studies the deliverables quote — is refused on `C-0110`'s ground: iteration 48 has **already**
run them, and this task's job is to carry what they found.

## 3. Falsifiers, declared before the pass runs

- **`F1`** — *the sweep is empty*: if every one of iteration 48's ten identifiers is already carried
  in both documents, this pass has nothing to do and the `T-319` row was opened wrongly.
- **`F2`** — *a retained checker is red after the pass and the red is this pass's*. (A red gate that
  is not this pass's is recorded and named, not repaired.)
- **`F3`** — *the self-describing challenge-and-claim count did NOT move*, i.e. the row that has been
  stale in six of seven passes is correct at this ref. Declared **open**: the honest outcome is
  whichever way it reads.
- **`F4`** — *the checker census moved in its NUMBER*. Declared **open**; the interesting outcome is
  that the number is right and the predicate is not.
- **`F5`** — *the "what we cannot answer" list contains an entry the programme has ANSWERED.*
  Declared **open**. `C-0191` found this class empty; a second empty reading is a result and a
  non-empty one is the drift class with a sign.
- **`F6`** — *a decision carries a STALE PRICE*: a number a decision is priced on whose challenge has
  been adjudicated since iteration 45 without the decision being restated. Declared **open**;
  `C-0191` prosecuted this at all eight decisions and found it empty.
- **`F7`** — *decision 9 should NOT be posed*: the width question is already asked somewhere a reader
  looking for a width specification question would find it. Declared **open**; the judgement is the
  deliverable, not the posing.
- **`F8`** — *a number this pass writes is not in the claim that owns it*, at the precision written.
  This fires on the pass itself and is checked by `grep` per headline number.
