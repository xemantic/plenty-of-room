## Formulate

### The question

[`C-0108`](../claims/C-0108-count-phase-interaction.md) measured the count/phase interaction
on a 32 × 6 grid **at §3's nominal 40.00 nm**,
because that is where its reproduction gates live.

But [`C-0086`](../claims/C-0086-seamless-scaffold-routing.md) shows that a seamless raster
quantises the tile's along-helix width at **odd multiples of 16 bp**,
so 40.00 nm (117.6 bp) is **not a width the design language can draw**
and the nearest buildable one is **112 bp = 38.08 nm**;
and [`C-0090`](../claims/C-0090-buildable-raster-width.md),
[`C-0102`](../claims/C-0102-crossover-phase-selection.md) and everything downstream of them
are read there.

**So the headline count/phase interaction this programme recommends on
is read at a width the design cannot build.**

And 38.08 nm is not a 4.8 % perturbation of 40.00 nm.
It is `7 × 5.44` **exactly**, so:

- a crossover **plane** lands on the row end at `φ ≡ 0 (mod 8)`
  and is a **column** only at `φ = 8` and `φ = 24` (`C-0102`);
- `C-0015`'s **ten** eight-column phases collapse to **two** — or to **none**
  if the row-end crossover is refused;
- `CrossoverLayout.EDGE_MARGIN = 0.05 nm`, a numerical guard that is inert at 40.00 nm,
  becomes a **physical assertion** at a width that is an exact whole number of column pitches.

The strata are therefore **not the same sets** at the two widths,
so a cell-by-cell comparison of two 32 × 6 grids is not automatically a comparison of anything.
What this task compares must be stated, and it is stated in *Plan*.

> **The question is: at the buildable width, is the count/phase interaction the same object
> `C-0108` measured — and does the recommended `(count, phase)` endpoint move?**

### Locked units and conventions

- Lengths **nm**, forces **pN**, stiffness **pN/nm**, pressure **pN/nm²** (= 1 MPa exactly);
  `k_BT = 4.141947 pN·nm` at 300 K, aqueous 2 mM MgCl₂.
- `x` runs **along** the helices and carries the width; `y` **across** them and does **not**
  (`C-0090`: the across-helix span is a **count of duplexes**, 15 × 2.69 = 40.35 nm, at both widths);
  `z` normal and positive **upward**, away from the grafted layer.
- **A row** is one duplex; **a plane** is `C-0055`'s 8 bp crossover plane;
  **a column** is the sheet's own 16 bp column lattice, of which the planes are every other member;
  **a root** is the crossover tying one upward arm to its host duplex.
- **Interior** (*row-end refused*) means the strictly-inside truncation by `EDGE_MARGIN`;
  **row-end admitted** means a plane or column lying *on* the row end is kept, inset by that margin.
  `C-0095` and `C-0099` closed this convention in favour of **admitted**,
  and this task carries **admitted** as primary and **refused** as the declared bracket.
- A **dishing** is `peakDishing / freeStroke` on that width's own free tile,
  so it is dimensionless and comparable across widths (`C-0090`: the level of the load cancels).
- The **statistic** is the 90th percentile, nearest rank, over `C-0087`'s measured dropout.
- `T-5b`'s **0.10** is a **convention**, not a physical threshold.

## Plan

### The cheap bound runs first, and it is arithmetic

Before any solve, three closed-form censuses:

1. **The column and station census** at both widths, both conventions and all 32 phases.
   This is `rasterJunctionPlanes` / `rasterColumnLayout` / `rasterUpwardSites`
   (`C-0090`'s own library, already parametrised by `edgeX`, `admitRowEnd` and `inset`),
   run over a grid — no elasticity, no load, no sampler.
2. **The inset-invariance proof.**
   A lattice **signature** — the column positions and every row's site positions —
   computed at 0.05 nm, half a rise and one rise, and compared.
   Where the signature is identical the guard's *value* is **provably** inert
   and no grading is needed to say so;
   where it is not, the graded spread is measured.
3. **The station-set identity at the 2 × 2's own corners.**
   `C-0090` states that at phases 8 and 24 the 38.08 nm admitted station lattice is
   **bit-identical** to the 40.00 nm one; re-derived here rather than transcribed,
   because it is what makes the two widths' 2 × 2 a **matched** comparison
   (same stations, different host and different load) rather than an unmatched one.

**This is why the task is affordable**: if (2) returns *inert*, an axis the brief expects to
dominate collapses to a proof and the compute goes to the grid instead.

### What is compared, since the strata are not the same sets

- **Matched at the corners.** The 2 × 2 the recommendation moves through — 34 → 30 paths,
  phase 24 → phase 8 — has **identical station sets at both widths**, by (3).
  So its total, its two splits and its interaction are directly comparable,
  and every difference is host geometry and load.
- **Not matched over the grid.** The 32 × 6 grids are **different objects**
  (52/53/60 sites at 40.00 against 45/52/53 refused or 52/53/60 admitted at 38.08).
  The grid comparison is therefore of **summary statistics of the same construction on two lattices** —
  the variance split, the interaction share, the sign census of the count term —
  and is quoted as such, never cell by cell.
- **Per stratum, each width's own strata.** `C-0102`'s strata are recomputed at 38.08 nm
  and the interaction is read inside each; the membership is reported beside it,
  because the sets differ.

### Method, and its cost

The construction is `C-0108`'s, unchanged: the canonical search-free nested chain,
equal springs sharing `C-0017`'s mandate as a **sum**,
`C-0087`'s measured incorporation field evaluated on the **narrower** tile
(the boundary layer transfers in nm, not in fractions — `CLAUDE.md`),
one Bernoulli stream per phase restricted per subset (common random numbers),
`C-0058`'s exact Woodbury surrogate sliced out of one `UpwardRootInfluenceBank` per phase.

**The alternative rejected:** re-searching the placement at 38.08 nm.
`CH-0119` is this programme's own finding that a placement-**searched** family measures
the search and not the count, and `C-0108`'s whole instrument is search-free.
A search would also cost two orders of magnitude more and answer a different question.

**What would falsify this approach:** if the two orderings of the 2 × 2 disagreed on their total
(an arithmetic error, not a result), or if the canonical chain were not nested at some phase
(then a count sweep is not a count sweep), or if the free tile dished under a uniform load
(then the solver is wrong, not the physics).

### Compute budget

192 graded cells at the carried convention plus 192 at the bracket,
each a Woodbury slice and 10 000 dropout realisations,
on 32 influence banks per convention.
Smoke-run at 300 realisations first, per `CLAUDE.md`, for the format strings and the schema only.
