## Formulate

### The question, stated exactly

[`DECISIONS-FOR-NDI.md`](../../DECISIONS-FOR-NDI.md) decision 1, [`ANSWERS.md`](../../ANSWERS.md) question 1 and
`TASKS.md`'s `T-63` row all say the same sentence:

> **Six independent routes recommend it**: `C-0012` on the force clause, `C-0016` on the bias window,
> `C-0017` on the stability floor, `C-0018` on the usable bias, `C-0027` on the corrected margin,
> and `C-0032` on the realised coupling law.

`C-0084` withdrew the sixth: `C-0032`'s escalation of 0.5 mM from a preference to a **requirement** was
measured on `C-0030`'s strain-**softening** coupled-standoff flexure, which `CH-0081`/`C-0069` removed from
the output role, and the element `C-0071` recommends has **no pull-in fold at 2 mM at 0 of 6** layer models.

**Nobody has asked whether the other five survive the same test**, and nobody has asked whether the six are
six. `CLAUDE.md` carries both warnings already —
*a correction can be quoted against a stack that has already left the design*, and
*a verdict that survives can survive on a different reason: re-check the GROUND of every verdict whose premise
is withdrawn, not just the verdict.*

### Acceptance predicates

- **`P1` — the census.** For each of the six routes: the **object** it is read on, whether that object is
  still in the design, whether its 0.5 mM conclusion survives on `C-0069`'s `Q5`, and — the column that
  matters — whether it survives on the **same ground** or a **different** one.
- **`P2` — the corrected count**, with the recommendation restated at whatever strength the surviving routes
  support. Both *"the count moves"* and *"the count stands"* are acceptable outcomes.
- **`P3` — independence.** Any pair of the six that shares a premise is not two routes. Checked **mechanically**,
  by comparing the emitting result files field by field, not by reading prose.
- **`P4` — provenance.** Every figure quoted in the claim is re-derived from its own claim's result JSON.
  A number transcribed from a claim's prose is not evidence that the claim's file says it.
- **`P5` — the shared documents.** `ANSWERS.md` and `DECISIONS-FOR-NDI.md` are corrected **only if the count
  moves**, additively or struck, never rewritten.

### Units, conditions and conventions — restated, not inherited

- Lengths **nm**, forces **pN**, stiffness **pN/nm**, potential **V**, concentration **mM**;
  `k_BT = 4.141947 pN·nm` at **300 K** in aqueous MgCl₂ at the stated concentration.
- The **stroke** `s = L₀ − h` is positive **downward**; `L₀` is a **force-onset** height at a defining load of
  1.0 pN over the 40 × 40 nm tile (`C-0011`, `CH-0010`).
- A **route** is *"a named claim, plus the one quantity of it that is compared between 0.5 mM and 2 mM"*.
  Two routes are **independent** only if neither's compared quantity is the other's, transferred.
- **A margin is quoted with the axis it is read on** and **a quantity with the state it is read at** —
  a stability floor with its held gap, a bias margin with its load line, a force clause with its stroke.
- The **recommended device** is the **10 nm** layer at `σ` = 0.024 nm⁻², placed at 100 pN over §3's
  **acceptable** 3 nm stroke (`C-0071`, `C-0068`). States belonging to other devices are **not** intersected
  with it (`C-0064`).
- The **recommended element** is `C-0069`'s `Q5`: a 34-instance hinge-rooted elastica arm, and its
  **stability-relevant** stiffness is its **tangent** minimum over the traversed `[0, 3 nm]` —
  **30.028762 pN/nm** — not the mandated **secant** 33.3333333 (`C-0049`, `CH-0042`).

## Plan

### The cheap bound, and it runs before any arithmetic

**Two of the six routes can be shown non-independent without evaluating any physics**, by comparing the
emitting result files:

1. `C-0016`'s bias-window clause is read on `T-2`'s `biasClauses[].biasForHundredPiconewtonBlocking`.
   `C-0012`'s force clause is read on `T-3`'s `thresholds[].biasForHundredPiconewtonBlocking`.
   If those agree field for field, `C-0016` is not a second route; it is `C-0012`'s number on a `σ` grid.
2. `C-0027`'s corrected margin is read on `T-25`'s `bufferComparison[]`, whose `stiffnessMarginBaseline*`
   and `biasMargin*` fields are declared transfers from `T-16` and `T-4`. If they agree with the min/max of
   `C-0017`'s `stabilityMargin` and `C-0018`'s coupled `margin`, `C-0027` is `C-0017` + `C-0018`, corrected.

That is a `O(1)` pass over four JSON files and it decides `P3` before `P1` is written.
**Cost justification**: the alternative — re-deriving each route's physics — costs hours of Poisson-Boltzmann
solves and cannot establish non-independence at all, because two independent derivations of the same number
would look identical anyway. The *file* comparison is the only thing that proves a transfer.

### The method

No new physics. Four steps:

1. **Read** the six emitting result files and pull each route's compared quantity at 10 nm, 0.5 mM and 2 mM.
2. **Re-read** the two element-dependent routes on `Q5`:
   - `C-0017`/`C-0027`'s margin is `k_c/|k_eff|` with `k_c` the mandated **secant**; on `Q5` the
     stability-relevant `k_c` is the **tangent** minimum over `[0, 3]`, so the margin scales by
     `30.028762/33.3333333` exactly. The **floor** itself is element-independent and does not move.
   - `C-0018`'s bias margin on `Q5` is `C-0084`'s, read from `T-149`'s `devices` rows.
3. **Read the force clause at the state the device occupies.** `C-0012`'s 4.97× advantage is a
   **zero-stroke** blocking-bias ratio; `C-0017`'s `simultaneousTargetBias` is the same clause at the
   **held** operating point the device actually sits at. Quote both.
4. **Classify** each route `SURVIVES_SAME_GROUND` / `SURVIVES_DIFFERENT_GROUND` / `WITHDRAWN`, and each
   pair `INDEPENDENT` / `TRANSFER`.

### The five verification gates

1. **Dimensional** — a margin is dimensionless and equals a stiffness over a stiffness; a floor is pN/nm;
   a bias ratio is dimensionless. Unphysical entry points throw.
2. **Limiting cases** — a route compared against itself is a `TRANSFER` at departure exactly 0; the
   secant/tangent rescaling is the identity at `k_c = 33.3333333`; an empty census is refused.
3. **Symmetry and conservation** — the census is a **partition**: `withdrawn + transfers + surviving`
   equals the six named routes, exactly once each, asserted as a test.
4. **Numerical convergence** — not applicable to a census, and *said so* rather than faked: the only
   numerics is a ratio of two read numbers. What is asserted instead is the **decision precision** —
   a `TRANSFER` verdict is taken at a relative tolerance derived from the two files' own emission
   precision, never at `==` on a `Double`.
5. **Literature and upstream** — every quoted figure re-derived from the emitting study's own result file,
   with the worst departure reported. This is `P4` and it is the gate this task exists for.

### What would falsify this approach

- **`F1` — the census removing the recommendation.** If any surviving route, re-read on `Q5`, points to
  **2 mM**, then the deliverable's conclusion and not only its count is wrong.
- **`F2` — the six being six.** If no pair of the six shares a number to the emission precision, the cheap
  bound is worthless and the claim reduces to *"one route withdrawn, five stand"*, which `CH-0098` already
  says.
- **`F3` — a route whose object cannot be classified.** If a route is read on an object that is neither
  clearly in nor clearly out of the design, the census cannot be completed and must say so.
