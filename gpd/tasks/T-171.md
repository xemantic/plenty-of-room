## Formulate

### The question

`CLAUDE.md` already records that *a placement's own phase and its HOST's phase are ONE variable*.
`C-0098`'s 32-phase census turns that into a **conflict**: three standing claims each want a
different value of that one integer.

| what wants the phase | the phases it wants at 40.0 nm | owner |
|---|---|---|
| the **richest** upward-tie inventory, 60 sites | 0, 1, 2, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 30, 31 | `C-0098` / `CH-0113` |
| an **eight-column** host | 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 22, 23, 24, 25, 26 | `C-0015` |
| a **centro-symmetric** upward root lattice | 8, 24 | `C-0063` |

The first set is disjoint from the second and the third is inside the second.
`C-0098` measures the *coupling* half of the trade at one load case — the 60-tie phase 17 reads
**0.487309625** of the free-tile stroke at the 90th percentile against phase 24's 53-tie
**0.385192562** — and prices **nothing** on the sheet side.
A seven-column host is a quarter fewer across-helix load paths, which is `C-0009`'s `D_⊥`,
`C-0016`'s per-load-path force, `C-0010`'s variance and `C-0054`'s connectivity budget, and none of
those has ever been re-read at a phase.

### The numeric target and the acceptance predicate

**A priced comparison of the three demands, and a recommendation of one phase — or the statement
that the three are irreconcilable and which one to drop.**

The predicate is met when all five hold:

1. **`P1`** — the census is complete over the phase at **both** widths §3 and `C-0086` admit
   (40.00 nm and the buildable 38.08 nm), under both end-of-row conventions, and the three demand
   sets and every pairwise intersection are emitted rather than asserted.
2. **`P2`** — the seven-column host is priced on the **sheet** side in at least four channels that
   are not the coupling's dishing, each traced to the claim that owns it.
3. **`P3`** — one phase is recommended, or the irreconcilability is stated with the demand to drop
   named and the cost of dropping it quoted.
4. **`P4`** — what the recommendation does to `C-0063`, `C-0090`, `C-0098` and `C-0071`'s
   recommended element is stated, and any moved verdict is raised as a **challenge**, not written
   over.
5. **`P5`** — the pipeline reproduces `C-0063`'s **0.0706145537** at 40.00 nm and `C-0090`'s
   **0.0621469105** at 38.08 nm.

### Units, geometry and sign conventions, restated rather than inherited

- Lengths **nm**, forces **pN**, stiffness **pN/nm**, plate rigidity **pN·nm**, pressure
  **pN/nm² = 1 MPa exactly**; `k_BT = 4.141947 pN·nm` at **300 K** in aqueous **2 mM MgCl₂**.
- `x` runs **along** the helices, `y` **across** them, `z` normal and positive **upward** — away
  from the grafted layer, which lies below the tile; `w` is positive **downward**; the origin is
  the tile centre.
- **The phase `φ` is one integer in `[0, 32)`**, quantised to base pairs, period **32 and not 16**
  (`C-0015`). Crossover planes at `x = 0.34 φ + 2.72 k` nm; the sheet's own **columns** are the
  planes with `k` **even**; row `r`'s **upward** (`EAST`) sites are the planes with
  `k ≡ 2r + 3 (mod 4)` (`C-0055`).
- **Dishing** is the peak absolute departure from the area-weighted best-fit **plane**, on the same
  **81 × 81** grid as every flatness claim upstream, normalised by the free-tile stroke. **Flat**
  means below **0.10** of it — `T-5b`'s **convention**, not a physical threshold.
- **The operating state is named**: `C-0022`'s **solved** edge profile at **2 mM, a 10 nm gap and
  0.192 V**, carried unchanged to 38.08 nm exactly as `C-0090` carries it — which `C-0100` has
  since measured to be worth **0.0712 %** of the flatness, three decades below the effect here.
- **End-of-row admitted** means a crossover plane lying *on* the row end is kept, inset by
  `CrossoverLayout.EDGE_MARGIN = 0.05 nm`. That is the reading the programme carries (`C-0095`,
  `C-0099`), and both readings are censused.

## Plan

### The cheap bound runs first, and it may be the whole answer

The phase is **a single integer with 32 values**, and each of the three demands is a **census** over
it: `C-0015`'s count rule is a truncation of a lattice, `C-0063`'s symmetry condition is the
congruence `2c ≡ 0 (mod 10.88 nm)`, and the upward inventory is a count of azimuths. **None of the
three needs a solve.** The table is built first, at both widths, and what it settles is stated
before anything else runs.

`C-0090` has already narrowed this and the census must be run at its width before the question is
treated as open: at the buildable 38.08 nm, `C-0015`'s ten eight-column phases **collapse to two**,
and those two are `C-0063`'s. Whether the *richest* set also collapses, and to what, is the first
thing the census answers.

### The sheet-side price, which is four closed forms and one solve set

Nobody has priced a seven-column host on the sheet side. Four channels, all cheap:

1. **`D_⊥` read two ways.** `C-0054` gives the **smeared** (uniform-curvature) reading, linear in
   the retained count, and the **series** (uniform-moment) reading, a harmonic mean over the
   fourteen interfaces. A seven-column sheet splits its columns 4/3 between the two parities, so
   the two readings must disagree — and by how much is a rational number, not a measurement.
2. **The connectivity budget.** `C-0054`'s theorem: a connected sheet needs one retained crossover
   on each of its `D − 1` interfaces, so the spendable budget is `crossovers − 14`.
3. **A severance probability under `C-0087`'s measured incorporation.** An interface carrying three
   crossovers empties with probability `(1 − p)³` against `(1 − p)⁴` — a closed form, swept over
   `C-0087`'s measured 0.48 / 0.84 / 0.95.
4. **The per-load-path force and the thermal dishing**, measured on `C-0009`'s grillage at each
   candidate phase — `C-0016`'s and `C-0010`'s channels, re-read at a phase for the first time.

### The solves, and why they are the ones chosen

The flatness comparison runs the **descent at identical effort** at every candidate phase, because
that is the only tool available at the phases with no centro-symmetric family and a comparison
between an exhaustive enumeration and a descent is not a comparison. `C-0063`'s and `C-0090`'s
**published optima are reproduced by evaluating their own published placement keys** through this
pipeline — one solve each, and a sharper gate than a re-enumeration, since it asserts the pipeline
and not the search. Re-running 560 000 exhaustive placements to re-derive two published numbers is
refused as the expensive route to a gate the cheap route already discharges.

### What would falsify this approach

Declared **before** execution.

| # | falsifier | what it would mean |
|---|---|---|
| **`F1`** | at the buildable width a phase exists that is **simultaneously** richest, eight-column and centro-symmetric | there is no conflict to price and the task is a one-line census |
| **`F2`** | the two `D_⊥` readings of a seven-column sheet agree | the series correction is empty and `C-0054`'s harmonic-mean discipline says nothing here |
| **`F3`** | at **matched search effort** the richest phase is flatter than the eight-column one at the buildable width | the recommendation reverses, and `C-0098`'s 40 nm measurement does not transfer |
| **`F4`** | the pipeline fails to reproduce `C-0063`'s 0.0706145537 or `C-0090`'s 0.0621469105 | the sweep is on the wrong object |
| **`F5`** | a uniform load on a uniform Winkler foundation dishes non-zero on any free host swept | the solver is wrong, not the physics |
| **`F6`** | `C-0090`'s *"the row-end crossover can never be an upward site, at any phase"* holds — admitting the row end changes no upward count at any phase | `CH-0118` does not exist and the richest set at 38.08 nm is a convention artefact of the other sign |

### The five gates

1. **Dimensional** — a census is a set of counts; every station lies inside its own footprint; every
   entry point refuses unphysical arguments.
2. **Limiting cases** — at 40.00 nm with the row end refused the census reproduces `C-0098`'s table
   exactly (52/53/60, the ten eight-column phases, `{8, 24}`); `uniformMomentRigidity` on a uniform
   lattice is exactly `(D/(D−1))²` times `uniformCurvatureRigidity`; an empty interface gives
   `D_⊥ = 0`; a severance probability at `p = 1` is zero and at `p = 0` is one.
3. **Symmetry and conservation** — the census is invariant under `φ → φ + 32` and **not** under
   `φ → φ + 16`; the per-interface counts sum to the interface total; the centro-symmetric set is
   computed two independent ways; a uniform load dishes exactly zero on every host swept.
4. **Numerical convergence** — the dishing sample grid 41/81/161 and nested subdivisions 1 ⊂ 2 ⊂ 4
   on the recommended placement; the descent repeated returns an identical placement key.
5. **Literature and upstream cross-check** — `C-0063`'s 0.0706145537, `C-0090`'s 0.0621469105,
   `C-0015`'s 56 and 49, `C-0098`'s 60 and its ten richest phases, `C-0055`'s 10.88 nm and 34,
   `C-0054`'s `D_⊥` identity.

## Verify — what actually happened

Recorded in [`C-0102`](../claims/C-0102-crossover-phase-selection.md), from
`gpd/results/T-171-crossover-phase-selection.json`.

**All five predicates met. The three demands are irreconcilable at both widths and the one to drop
is the inventory; phase 8 at 38.08 nm is recommended.**

| # | falsifier | fired? | outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| **`F1`** | a phase satisfies all three demands | **NO** | richest `[0, 16]`, eight-column and centro-symmetric `[8, 24]` at 38.08 nm — **0** phases satisfy all three at any of the three readings |
| **`F2`** | the two `D_⊥` readings agree | **NO** | smeared **0.875** against series **0.857142857**, exactly `48/49` apart |
| **`F3`** | at matched effort the richest phase is flatter | **NO** | phase 8 **0.0658484805** against phase 16's **0.125068659** |
| **`F4`** | a published optimum fails to reproduce | **NO** | `C-0063`'s 0.0706145537 at `2.9e−10` and `C-0090`'s 0.0621469105 at `5.5e−10`, each from its own placement key |
| **`F5`** | a uniform load dishes non-zero on a free host | **NO** | worst **`2.3e−7`** of the free stroke over the four hosts; **exactly `0.0`** at phases 0 and 16 |
| **`F6`** | `C-0090`'s *"never an upward site, at any phase"* holds | **NO — and that is the finding** | row-end upward stations `{0 = 15, 8 = 0, 16 = 15, 24 = 0}`, raising [`CH-0118`](../challenges/CH-0118-the-row-end-crossover-is-a-station-at-two-phases.md) |

**Cost**: 39 s of compute, 87 719 descent evaluations, 96 census rows and 9 closed forms. The
expensive route the plan refused — re-enumerating 560 000 exhaustive placements to re-derive two
published numbers — was replaced by two solves on the claims' own placement keys.
