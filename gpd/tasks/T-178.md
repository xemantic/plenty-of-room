## Formulate

### The question

[`C-0103`](../claims/C-0103-path-count-at-fixed-geometry.md) settled
[`CH-0103`](../challenges/CH-0103-the-path-count-recommendation-runs-against-fabrication.md)
at **one** crossover phase.
At fixed station geometry on `C-0063`'s own phase-24 upward lattice
the 34 → 30 reduction costs **+12.8596328 %** of the 90th-percentile dishing under `C-0087`'s
measured dropout (**0.638498565 → 0.720607136** of the free-tile stroke),
against a plan margin improving **68.8998088×**.

It then defended the standing recommendation on a **decomposition**:
the move the programme actually takes is `C-0063`'s 34 at phase **24** → `C-0074`'s 30 at phase **8**,
which reads **8.67824131 % better** (0.639129638 → 0.583664426),
so *"the count term is +12.86 % and the phase term is −19.0 %"*.

**That decomposition is a subtraction, and nothing bounds the interaction it assumes away.**
The count term was measured at one phase and the phase term at one pair of phases.
If the count term depends on the phase, then
*"count + phase"* is not a decomposition at all —
it is one of two paths across a 2 × 2, and the two paths split the same total differently.

Three warnings from the immediately preceding work bear on exactly this:

- **`C-0098`**: the cheap ranking instrument transfers across *designs* (ρ = 0.9729)
  and **does not** transfer across **phases** (ρ = **0.468487481**).
  `C-0103` then found it *does* transfer across **counts** (ρ = 0.942857143–1.000000000).
  The phase is the axis the shortcut fails on, and it is this task's axis.
- **`CH-0119`**: a redundancy slope fitted through **placement-searched** subsets has the **wrong sign**
  against a **nested** chain — **+0.0610348337** against **−0.740086889**, on one lattice,
  differing only in whether the placement was allowed to move.
  This task must say which kind of chain it fits, and it fits **nested** ones.
- **`C-0102`**: the phases are **not interchangeable hosts**.
  At §3's 40.00 nm the richest upward inventory (60 sites) belongs to ten **seven**-column phases,
  `C-0015`'s eight-column ten are disjoint from them,
  and only **8** and **24** are centro-symmetric.
  A seven-column host's series `D_⊥` loses `6/7` where the smeared reading loses `7/8`,
  its spendable crossover budget falls 42 → 35,
  and under `C-0087`'s measured incorporation it is **3.58698588×** likelier to lose
  every crossover on some interface.
  A phase sweep that treats the host as fixed is measuring the wrong thing,
  so **every phase here carries its own host**, exactly as `C-0063` and `C-0102` build theirs.

> **So the question is: is the count term a property of the coupling, or of the phase it was measured at —
> and if it is of the phase, what is left of `C-0103`'s defence of the 34 → 30 move?**

### The numeric target and the acceptance predicates

> **`P1`** — the **90th-percentile** peak dishing, as a ratio of the free-tile stroke, of an
> equal-spring array at `C-0017`'s unchanged total, under `C-0087`'s measured Bernoulli dropout
> at 10 000 seeded realisations, is emitted on a **nested, search-free** family of station sets at
> path counts **22, 25, 28, 30, 34 and 45** at **every one of the 32 crossover phases**,
> each on **its own** host.

- **`P2`** — the **cheap bound runs first and is reported whatever it says.** `C-0089`'s
  single-removal instrument (`n` solves, no sampling) is evaluated over the same 32 × 6 grid, and
  its rank agreement with the graded percentile is measured **across phases at fixed count** —
  the scope `C-0098` found it failing on and `C-0103` found it surviving on the other axis.
- **`P3`** — the **decomposition is tested as a decomposition.** The count term
  `p90(30, φ)/p90(34, φ)` is emitted at every phase, the phase term `p90(c, φ)/p90(c, 24)` at
  every count, and the **interaction** is measured two independent ways: the residual of a
  balanced two-way additive fit in `log p90` over the whole 32 × 6 grid, and the difference
  between the two orderings of the 2 × 2 on the recommendation's own move
  (count-then-phase against phase-then-count). The two orderings share their endpoints, so their
  **totals must agree exactly** and their **splits need not**.
- **`P4`** — the phases are **stratified by `C-0102`'s census** — richest inventory, eight-column,
  centro-symmetric — and the count term is reported per stratum, so a *"the count effect holds"*
  verdict is not an average over hosts that differ structurally.
- **`P5`** — every headline is reproduced rather than cited: `C-0103`'s **0.638498565** and
  **0.720607136** at phase 24, `C-0063`'s **0.0706145537**, the uncoupled tile's
  **0.307902368**, `C-0089`'s **0.583664426**, `C-0026`'s **4.90731102 nm** and `C-0066`'s **53**
  sites at phase 24.

### Locked units, geometry and sign conventions

Lengths **nm**, forces **pN**, stiffness **pN/nm**, pressure **pN/nm² = 1 MPa** exactly;
`k_BT = 4.141947 pN·nm` at **300 K** in aqueous **2 mM MgCl₂**.
`x` runs **along** the helices, `y` **across** them, `z` normal and positive **upward** — away from
the grafted layer, which is **below** the tile; `w` is positive **downward**; the origin is the
tile centre.
**Dishing** is the peak absolute departure from the area-weighted least-squares best-fit **plane**
on an **81 × 81** grid, over the free-tile stroke; **flat** means below **0.10** of it, which is
`T-5b`'s **convention** and not a physical threshold.
A **dropout is a REMOVAL, not a perturbation** (`C-0087`).
**The operating state is named**: `C-0022`'s **solved 2 mM / 10 nm / 0.192 V** profile — the
twelfth time this programme has had to say which state a flatness verdict is read at.
The verdict statistic is the **90th percentile**, a nearest-rank order statistic, which is
`C-0087`'s, `C-0089`'s, `C-0098`'s and `C-0103`'s.
The distribution is **EQUAL springs at `C-0017`'s unchanged SUM** at every count and every phase,
which is the convention that isolates the count (`C-0089`'s Deliverable 1).

## Plan

### The method, and why this one

**The expensive object is a graded cell** — 10 000 Woodbury solves and an 81 × 81 dishing
reconstruction each — and the grid is 32 phases × 6 counts. Three things are done before it, in
this order, and each of them could have made the sweep unnecessary.

1. **The census, which costs no solve.** The upward `EAST` lattice at 40.00 nm is enumerated at
   all 32 phases: site count, per-row shape, sheet column count and centro-symmetry. It says
   whether the swept counts are even *reachable* at every phase (they are: every phase carries
   ≥ 52 sites in rows of 3 or 4, so 15 ≤ count ≤ 45 everywhere) and it supplies `C-0102`'s three
   strata. **Nothing is excluded**: the phase is one integer with 32 values, a complete census
   over it is the project's own idiom, and excluding phases on a buildable-width argument would
   change the host the reproduction gates are written on.
2. **The path identity, which costs no solve either.** In `log p90` the 2 × 2 total is the sum of
   either path's two terms, so the two orderings differ by **exactly one number**, the
   interaction — and that number is a function of **four** cells. So the question
   *"is `C-0103`'s decomposition well posed for the move it defends?"* costs four graded cells,
   not 192. The 32-phase grid answers the wider question (*is the count term a constant of the
   coupling?*) and is run second.
3. **`C-0089`'s single-removal instrument over the whole 32 × 6 grid** — `n` solves per cell and
   no sampling, i.e. about `1/300` of a graded cell. If it ranks phases the way the percentile
   does, the 32-phase grid can be screened cheaply in every future task; `C-0098` measured
   ρ = 0.468487481 across phases on the shared body and `C-0103` measured ρ = 0.94–1.00 across
   counts on the array, so **whichever way this comes out it is a result**, and it is the
   cheapest one in the task.

**And one published cross-check runs before any of it.** `C-0098`'s Deliverable 1 grades the
**array** at full inventory at six phases, and its own table reads **0.612366061** at phase 24
against **0.65233453** at phase 8 — the phase running the *opposite* way to the −19.0 % that
`C-0103`'s subtraction attributes to it. That number is read at run time from
`gpd/results/T-165-shared-body-placement.json`, not retyped, and it is the first evidence that
the decomposition may not be one.

**The family is nested and search-free.** `CH-0119` is this programme's own warning that a
placement-searched family measures the search; and `C-0102` warns that a comparison between an
exhaustive enumeration and a descent is not a comparison. Both are avoided by using **one
deterministic construction at every phase**: the one-root-per-row placement taking each row's site
nearest the tile centre, grown by `C-0103`'s own addition rule. It is nested by construction at
every pair of counts (asserted as a gate), it is identical in kind at all 32 phases, and it
contains **no search at all** — so a difference between two of its cells is a difference of
phase or of count and of nothing else. `C-0063`'s own 34-root anchor is carried **beside** it at
phase 24, as the reproduction gate and as a measure of how much the anchor rule is worth.

**The sampling.** One Bernoulli stream **per phase** over that phase's whole site inventory, at
`C-0087`'s seed **20260817** and 10 000 realisations, restricted per subset — `C-0103`'s common
random numbers, which is what makes a within-phase count difference paired and therefore
measurable at 32× its own sampling floor. Across phases the inventories differ, so the streams
cannot be shared; the quantities differenced across phases are **ratios already formed within a
phase**, which is where the pairing is needed.

### The cost, justified

A graded cell is ~2 s; 192 of them plus 32 banks is a few minutes, against ~40 s for the whole
cheap layer. The expensive part is therefore affordable **and** it is not what settles the
headline: the 2 × 2 does, on four of the 192 cells. The 32-phase grid buys the generality —
whether the count term is a constant of the coupling — which no smaller experiment can supply.

### What would falsify this approach

> **`F1` — the declared falsifier.** **The count term is not one-signed across the phase family**:
> at one or more of the 32 crossover phases the 34 → 30 reduction at fixed station geometry
> **lowers** the 90th-percentile dishing under the measured dropout. Then *"the count effect holds
> at the other 31 phases"* is false as stated, `C-0103`'s +12.86 % is a phase-24 property rather
> than a property of the count, and its decomposition must be restated — which is `CH-0123`.

- **`F2`** — the two axes are **not separable**: the largest residual of the balanced two-way
  additive fit in `log p90` is at least as large as the count term it splits, or the 2 × 2
  interaction on the recommendation's own move exceeds half of `C-0103`'s +12.86 %.
- **`F3`** — the cheap single-removal instrument fails to transfer **across counts** here as well
  as across phases (ρ < 0.8 within a phase), in which case it screens nothing and the whole cheap
  layer is worthless on this lattice.
- **`F4`** — a standing figure fails to reproduce: `C-0103`'s 0.638498565 or 0.720607136,
  `C-0063`'s 0.0706145537, the uncoupled tile's 0.307902368, or `C-0089`'s 0.583664426.
- **`F5`** — the uncoupled tile under a **uniform** load dishes non-zero on any of the 32 hosts.

### What this cannot settle

- **The distribution stays EQUAL at every cell**, deliberately, and that is `T-179`'s axis. The
  distribution is worth 1.30–1.61× on an array (`C-0089`), so it could in principle absorb a
  1.13× count term; nothing here bounds the three-way interaction of count, phase and
  distribution.
- **One deterministic anchor rule.** `C-0103` measured up to 6 % of rule dependence between its
  two chains below 30 paths. The canonical family here is a third rule; the *values* carry that
  dependence and the *signs* and the *interaction* are what is read.
- **One width and one load state.** 40.00 nm, because the reproduction gates are written there;
  `C-0086`/`C-0090`/`C-0102` have moved the buildable width to 38.08 nm and the recommended phase
  to 8, and the census at that width is `C-0102`'s. And `C-0022`'s solved 2 mM / 10 nm / 0.192 V
  only — `C-0068` has already shown a placement can reverse between layer heights.
- **The incorporation field is `C-0087`'s**, a plain Rothemund rectangle's staple map transferred
  to a coupling path nobody has measured, and the whole branch is most sensitive to that.
