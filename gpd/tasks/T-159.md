## Formulate

### The question, stated exactly

`C-0092` established that `C-0084`'s *"the branch ends by folding at 7.9197 nm"* is a property of
`C-0039`'s **doubling** force ladder and not of the elastica:
marched in the near-end rotation, the same arm answers to **8.1610821 nm**
with `max_s|φ| = 1.5707924` rad, 0.999997 of `π/2` and still below it,
out of a contour of **8.16439083 nm**.

`C-0092` did that in a **separate class** (`stability/LargeRotationArmBranch.kt`)
and re-read only the **12** states of the recommended device.
Three things are therefore outstanding:

- **`P1`** — `TwoSpringElastica.forceForDisplacement` still doubles blind,
  so every future consumer of the shared element inherits the artefact.
- **`P2`** — **96 of `C-0084`'s 108 fold rows have not been re-read** at the corrected element domain.
- **`P3`** — `CH-0099`'s inflation table is repriced only for those 12.

### Acceptance predicates

- **`A1`** — a **cheap classification of all 108 rows** into those a corrected element domain
  *can* move and those it *cannot*, with the argument stated as a containment rather than a scan,
  and the movable set re-read.
- **`A2`** — `TwoSpringElastica.forceForDisplacement` **continuing rather than doubling**:
  a branch continuation whose shooting root at each force is anchored on the previous accepted
  root, which **refuses** rather than returning a root off the small-rotation branch,
  and which reaches a supremum strictly greater than the ladder's 7.9196867 nm
  and strictly below the contour.
- **`A3`** — the repair's cost and its **evaluation count** asserted, not just its answer
  (`C-0031`: *a defect invisible in the answer is invisible to every check written on the answer*).
- **`A4`** — **every downstream study re-run and diffed**, with every moved field classified as
  one ulp / inside a declared tolerance / a quantity that is identically zero / a real change.
- **`A5`** — `CH-0099` repriced over all 108 rows, and a verdict on whether
  *"only the recommended device's 12 matter"*.
- **`A6`** — `C-0033`'s collar composition at the extended ceilings **filed as an argument**
  rather than assumed: where there is no fold, a positive increment to the coupled tangent
  cannot create one.

### Units, conditions and conventions — restated, not inherited

- Lengths **nm**, forces **pN**, stiffness **pN/nm**, rotational stiffness **pN·nm/rad**,
  rotation **rad**, potential **V**, concentration **mM**;
  `k_BT = 4.141947 pN·nm` at **300 K** in aqueous MgCl₂.
- **`C-0039`'s geometry, unchanged.** Arc length `s ∈ [0, L]` from the hinge;
  `φ(s)` the tangent angle from the undeformed axis toward the stroke;
  `x(s) = ∫cos φ`, `z(s) = ∫sin φ`;
  `EI φ″ = −F cos φ + H sin φ`, `EI φ′(0) = k_n φ(0)`, `EI φ′(L) = M₀ − k_f φ(L)`.
  The conserved first integral at `H = M₀ = 0` is `½ EI φ′² + F sin φ`.
- One arm's stroke is `δ = z(L)`; the array's reaction is `n` times one arm's tip force,
  because `C-0017`'s mandate is a **sum**.
- The device's stroke `s = L₀ − h` is positive downward; `L₀` is a force-onset height
  at a defining load of 1.0 pN over the tile (`C-0011`, `CH-0010`).
- **A fold is `max_s V_eq(s)`**, and at it `k_c(s) + k_eff(s) = 0` exactly.
  **A boundary maximum is not a stationary point** and no tangency residual is reported at one.
- The **recommended device** is the 10 nm layer at `σ` = 0.024 nm⁻² in 0.5 and 2.0 mM
  (`C-0071`, `C-0068`); the 5 nm and 7 nm rows of `C-0084` are coverage parity and are
  **not** intersected with it (`C-0064`).

## Plan

### The cheap bound, and it runs on a file that already exists

A path is searched over `[0, strokeCeiling]` with
`strokeCeiling = min(layer stroke ceiling, element domain ceiling)`.
**Correcting the element domain can only raise the second argument.**
So a row can move **only if** its stroke ceiling is owned by the element —
and where the layer owns it, `min` is unchanged **identically**, not approximately.

`gpd/results/T-149-recommended-element-fold.json` carries `strokeCeilingOwner` per row, so the
classification is one pass over 108 records and costs nothing:

- **54 rows** are `C-0018`'s affine mandate `R = 33.3333 s`, which contains no elastica at all;
- **36** of the 54 `LQ5` rows have a **layer-owned** ceiling (4.33–6.50 nm), far below both the
  ladder's 7.9197 nm and any corrected domain;
- **18** have an **element-owned** ceiling, all at the 10 nm layer, of which **12** are the
  recommended device `C-0092` already re-read.

**So at most 6 of the 96 can move**, and the whole of `P2` reduces to six rows at 10 nm / 10 mM.
This is the argument the acceptance's second branch — *"only the recommended device's 12 matter"* —
would have to rest on, and it is worth stating before anything is compiled.

**Cost justification.** A blind re-read of 96 states is 96 fold searches at ~42 Poisson-Boltzmann
solves per path point (`C-0084` measured 7.6–8.1 ms each) — tens of minutes — and 90 of them are
provably unchanged. One pass over an existing JSON is milliseconds and it also says *why*.
The expensive half is then spent where it can move something: the six rows, and the downstream diff.

### The repair

The defect is in the **selection of the shooting root**, not in the integrator.
`stateAtForce` brackets the near-end rotation by doubling from a seed; `forceForDisplacement`
brackets the tip force by doubling from `0.5 δ k_small`. Both are exact while the far-end moment
residual has one root, and `C-0092` measured a second appearing at ~100 pN — three decades of force
below the right angle `C-0039`'s own KDoc warns about.

The repair is a **continuation**, and it is confined to the stroke-driven entry points:

1. a private branch solver that takes a **rotation floor** — the previous accepted root — and scans
   **geometrically upward from it** for the first sign change, bisecting on that bracket only.
   Anchoring is legitimate and is checked at run time: at fixed `φ0` the residual is decreasing in
   the tip force, so the previous root is a valid negative endpoint at any larger force, and the
   branch root is above it because `φ0` increases along the branch;
2. it **refuses** — returns `null`, and the caller throws — where the scan reaches
   `max_s|φ| ≥ π/2` without a sign change, rather than returning a root off the small-rotation
   branch (`CLAUDE.md`);
3. `forceForDisplacement` marches the tip force with a **step that shrinks** when a step fails a
   branch test (the solver refusing, the stroke failing to increase, the rotation floor failing to
   be a negative endpoint) instead of doubling unconditionally, and bisects for the target inside
   the last accepted cell;
4. `stateAtDisplacement` returns the **continuation's own** state, so a caller cannot get a state
   from a different branch than the force it was given.

**`stateAtForce` itself is left unchanged.** Its direct callers (`T-79`'s study and its tests) work
at forces where the residual has one root, and a minimal repair is one that does not move them.

### Downstream

The changed file is a shared main source that produces published results, so `C-0031`/`P-15`'s
discipline applies in full: every study that consumes `TwoSpringElastica` is re-run in a snapshot
and diffed against the committed result file, and every moved field is classified. Nothing is
copied back — a moved result belongs to the claim that owns it, and a moved verdict is a challenge.

### What would falsify this approach

- **`F1` — the containment being wrong**: a row whose stroke ceiling is *layer*-owned moving by more
  than solver noise when the whole 108-row study is re-run. That would withdraw the cheap bound and
  with it the *"only 12 matter"* answer.
- **`F2` — the repair not being one**: the continuation reaching a supremum **at or below** the
  ladder's 7.9196867 nm, or above the contour 8.16439083 nm. The second cannot happen (`C-0092`'s
  theorem) and is the stronger of the pair for exactly that reason.
- **`F3` — a headline number moving**: any published figure of `C-0039`, `C-0069`, `C-0071`,
  `C-0084` or `C-0092` moving by more than its own emission precision. That would make this a
  correction to those claims rather than a repair beneath them.
- **`F4` — a fold in the extension at the six states**: which would make `C-0084`'s *"no fold"*
  wrong somewhere outside the recommended device.
- **`F5` — the collar creating a fold**: if `C-0033`'s collar increment to the coupled tangent can
  be negative at any of the extended ceilings, the *"a positive increment cannot make a tangent
  vanish"* argument `C-0084` and `C-0092` both leaned on is unavailable.
