## 3. `P0`, and the drift class it establishes

`C-0154` was filed in commit `49b1a01`, iteration 38, **in the same commit as `C-0155`**, the tenth
synthesis. `grep` for `C-0154`, `24/7`, `dimer`, `acrossHelixRigidity` and `0.0449400126` over both
deliverables at `9e35853` returns **0 hits in 2 files**; neither `C-0155` nor `C-0165` consumes it.

Two of the sentences it alone already falsified needed **no re-grade at all**: `10 × 6`'s
*"no coupling-fraction threshold at all, flat even at `f = 0`"* and the conclusion drawn from it,
that the cross-section **removes** the last unmeasured dependency in the flatness verdict rather
than bounding it. `C-0154`'s own published table gives 0.127358454 at enhancement 1.0 — outside
`T-5b`. Both are withdrawn here, and the honest statement is the weaker and more useful one: `10 × 6`
**clears** the interlayer-coupling calibration across the whole measured band where `15 × 4` fails
inside it, which is a **bound** and not a removal.

The cause is structural, and it is `CH-0221`: **a claim whose subject is a MODEL has no verdict
sentence to attach itself to.** There is no token to `grep`, no status to compare and no passage it
contradicts — until a later claim converts it into a verdict, at which point every carrier becomes
visible at once. The detector is one question per newly filed claim — *what sentence in the
deliverables would be different if this were true?* — with *"none, yet"* recorded as a **pending
premise** rather than as nothing. It is deliberately **not** mechanised, on this repository's own
standard that an unmeasured false-positive rate is what makes a checker stop being believed.
