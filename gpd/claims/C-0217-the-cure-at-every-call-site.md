## 2. The repair is one rule, and that is the point rather than a tidiness

`CLAUDE.md`: *a cure is a property of a CALL SITE, not of a repository — grep for the call sites,
not for the fix*, and *a duplicated rule is invisible to a mutation test of either copy*. The
repair puts the rule in `tile/JointPlacementDistribution.kt` once, with four public entry points:

| | what it is | why it exists separately |
|---|---|---|
| `searchDecisionKey(value)` | the quantisation, at `SEARCH_DECISION_DIGITS = 6` | one place for the mutation `M13` to reach all nineteen sites from |
| `decidesBetter(candidate, incumbent)` | the comparison | the two **ranks** are this decision read as a count, and they had no other home |
| `byDecisionThenLabel(label, key)` | the comparator | the **four** sites that select by sorting |
| `decisionArgmin(candidates, label, key)` | the argmin, evaluating `key` **exactly once** per candidate | the **twelve** sites that select by argmin, three of which have a whole dropout ensemble behind that key |

`jointPlacementBetter` is rewritten on `decidesBetter`, so the tie-break and the comparison are each
written once. The tie-break is the candidate's **own label**, which is stronger than *the earlier
candidate wins*: it makes the answer a property of the family rather than of a traversal, and it
agrees with the earlier-candidate rule wherever the labels are in enumeration order, as every label
in this study is.

**Eight named tests, written first and watched fail** — they do not compile against the model at
`HEAD`, `Unresolved reference 'decidesBetter'` and fifteen more. The discriminating case is the one
`CLAUDE.md` names: a candidate better by **less** than six significant digits carrying a **larger**
key. Rounded it ties and the label refuses it; unrounded it wins. A test that perturbs the objective
only upward holds nothing open, because a raw comparison refuses an upward perturbation too, and
both directions are asserted.

### It is the residue `C-0216` §14(c) named, and the repair makes that measurable rather than asserted

`spearmanAgainstSearched` is `spearmanRankCorrelation(screenValues, tierTwoSearched)`, and
`tierTwoSearched` is the **thirteen** tier-2 `trainingObjective`s — thirteen outputs of
`C-0135`'s composed search, each a descent over 50 stiffnesses on a 60-realisation training
percentile. `C-0135` is explicit that where the active constraints are fewer than the free
directions the optimal set is a **manifold**, that rounding fixes which **branch** is taken and not
the **point**, and that the right response is *report the residual rather than asserting
byte-identity*.

**What makes this a measurement rather than a restatement is that everything else built on those
same thirteen numbers is now bit-identical.** `bestTierTwo` is their **minimum**;
`regretOfSelectingOnThisScreen` is a **ratio** of one of them to it (`1.2208`, unchanged);
`screenArgminIsSearchedArgmin` is their **argmin**; `jointWinnerRankInThisScreen` and
`screenIsBinding` are a **rank** in the *screen*; the finalists and the joint corner are selected
from them. Every one of those is stable across C and D. Only the **rank correlation** — the one
emitted statistic that reads the *order* of the thirteen rather than their value, their extremum or
their argmin — moves.

**And the mechanism is a TIE.** With no ties Spearman is `1 − Σd²/364` at `n = 13`, so a tie-free
reading is an exact multiple of `1/364`: `−0.0824175824` gives `Σd² = 394` exactly and
`0.494505495` gives `184` exactly, while `−0.0935351641` and `0.492435129` give no integer at all.
`spearmanRankCorrelation` assigns **average ranks** to ties, so the two readings differ by whether
two of the thirteen `searchDecision`-rounded objectives **tie at six significant digits** — which
is the decision precision doing exactly what it is for, one level below the statistic that reports
it.

## 10. What this does NOT establish

- **No physics.** No model, no constant, no design variable and no operating state moves. A reader
  looking for a result about the actuator will not find one here.
- **It does not make `T-323`'s search a better search.** A decision precision fixes which **branch**
  a descent takes; `C-0135` is explicit that it does not fix the **point** on a manifold, and the
  90th percentile of a 60-realisation ensemble is an order statistic that selects a realisation.
- **It does not establish that the corpus's other studies are reproducible.** The repair is one call
  graph and the census is one call graph. `CLAUDE.md` records the class three times and has never
  counted it corpus-wide; `T-326` is that sweep and it is not run here.
- **`C-0216`'s determination statement stands**: its `(P₁, D₀)` corner at `f = 0.26` and the three
  Spearman readings were *"determined to about three significant digits, not nine"* when they were
  read at a raw comparison. What this claim can say is that they are now determined **by the
  emitter** — that two runs of it agree — and not that the underlying percentile is determined to
  nine digits by the physics.
