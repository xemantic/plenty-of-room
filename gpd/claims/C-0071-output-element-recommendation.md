## The claim, in one line

**Two iterations ago there were four output branches and no way to choose between them; `C-0069` reduced the element space by a topology argument and `CH-0081` removed the truss from the output role, and what is left is decidable by three integer comparisons — so the programme recommends the element it already had, and the whole content of this claim is the price: three undemonstrated premises, one 0.0256 nm arithmetic that three separate margins reduce to, four unanswered specification questions, and five ways for a single result to remove it, two of which are not hypotheses but published numbers.**

## The conventions, restated rather than inherited

- Lengths **nm**, forces **pN**, stiffness **pN/nm**, rotational stiffness **pN·nm/rad**;
  `k_BT = 4.141947 pN·nm` at **300 K** in aqueous MgCl₂ at the stated concentration.
- `x` along the host sheet's helices, `y` across them, `z` **normal and positive upward**, away from the grafted layer.
- **A margin carries a SENSE**: `limit/value` against a **ceiling**, `value/limit` against a **floor**.
  Both are ≥ 1 when the requirement clears, both are dimensionless, and recording the sense is what stops the two being divided the wrong way round.
- **A margin below 1.05 is `NONE`.** The threshold is a **declared convention**, chosen at the coarsest quantum this programme can build — one base-pair rise, 0.34 nm, on the recommended 8.16 nm member, i.e. 4.2 %. Exactly at the limit is `NONE`, never `VIOLATED`.
- **A premise is flagged by HOW IT WAS OBTAINED**, never by how confident anyone is: `DERIVED`, `CITED_MEASURED`, `CITED_MODEL_INPUT`, `CITED_FITTED`, `SPECIFICATION`, or **`UNDEMONSTRATED`** — a motif this programme constructed and a recorded literature search did not find in print.
- **A failure route is a SINGLE standing result, reversed**, and it carries both what it does (`REMOVES_THE_ELEMENT` / `REMOVES_A_PREMISE` / `CHANGES_UNIQUENESS`) and what would settle it (`CALCULATION` / `MEASUREMENT` / `SPECIFICATION` / `EXPERIMENT`).
- **Every number is read at §3's ACCEPTABLE clause**, 100 pN over 3 nm, because `C-0050` establishes that the desired ~10 nm clause is unreachable on §3's own stack.
- `C-0017`'s mandate is a **sum**, so the per-path secant is `33.3333/34 = 0.980392 pN/nm`.

### The finding that a synthesis was needed to see

**The three `NONE` bands that bind are ONE number.** `8.19 − 8.16439` and `(10.88 − 8.16439) − 2.69` are the same arithmetic, `pitch − d − L`, asserted equal to `1e−12` as a gate-3 test; and the two joint ceilings are the two coordinates of the **same** `c ≤ 2.3417` inequality read one at a time — move either joint and the arm's length moves with it, into or out of the same 0.0256 nm. **So the design does not have three thin margins. ## Validity range

- **TRL 1–3. Nothing here is measured and the motif is not demonstrated.** A recommendation is a reading of the clauses, not a demonstration of the device.
- **The recommendation is for §3's ACCEPTABLE clause only.** At the desired clause no rooted element exists on this lattice at all.
- **Every clause the funnel applies is inherited.** This claim adds no clause; it ranks what survives them. If `C-0069`'s catalogue is incomplete (route `R9`) the ranking is over the wrong set.
- **The three tie-break axes are declared here and are a convention.** They were chosen because each is an integer count a standing claim already carries; a different set of axes could, in principle, rank differently — but `Q5` **dominates** `Q7` on all three, so no reordering of these three changes the winner, and only a **fourth** axis favouring `Q7` could.

- **The flatness numbers are `C-0063`'s and `C-0068`'s and are CITED**, not recomputed here; both are grillage solves at phase 24 under `C-0022`'s solved load, and both belong to the **10 nm** layer.
- **A "places" verdict is the weak direction** (`C-0069`): the plan model is hard-body at nominal positions, so a real array is **less** likely to place.
- **`T-95` and `T-102` are discharged FOR THIS ELEMENT and are not closed as programme questions.** They re-bind the moment the flexure-and-tie branch or §3's 45 paths return.
