## 3. Decision 9, and why it is posed

`C-0201` §7's finding — *"the tile is not too floppy, it is too small"* — had been in
`DECISIONS-FOR-NDI.md` since iteration 47 **inside section 6**, which asks whether §3 admits a
**two-layer** tile. That is a question about **thickness**; this is a question about **width**, and a
reader looking for one would not have found it there. It is not decision 8 either: decision 8 asks
*which of two readings of one tile's width §3 names*, a **convention**, where this asks whether the
width that is buildable at all is acceptable.

`F7` was declared open — *decision 9 should NOT be posed, because the question is already asked
somewhere a reader would find it* — and it **did not fire**.

The question, at the only demonstrated turn topology (`28 nt` of unpaired scaffold at every turn,
`C-0193`, `C-0200`):

| scaffold | nt | uniform row | width | against §3's 40 nm | uncoupled, `f = 0.30` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| M13mp18, NDI's own default | 7 249 | 92 bp | `31.28 nm` | `−21.8 %` | `0.0425678289`, flat |
| p7560 | 7 560 | 98 bp | `33.32 nm` | `−16.7 %` | `0.0422200543`, flat |
| p8064 | 8 064 | 106 bp | `36.04 nm` | `−9.9 %` | `0.0451172785`, flat |

**Three things make it well posed rather than a complaint.** §3's row is *"Tile footprint — 40 × 40 nm
(test tiles up to ~70 × 100 nm)"* and the tilde is on the **test tiles**, so `40 nm` carries no stated
tolerance. NDI's own answer to decision 5 — *"M13, circular ~7-8K nucleotides"* — already puts **p8064
inside the stated band**, so the `−9.9 %` row needs nothing new and the `−21.8 %` row is what the
default delivers. And the two levers that reach §3's `40 nm` are named with their costs: a two-length
raster (**116 bp = `39.44 nm`** box, `109 bp = 37.06 nm` row span, needing **8 010 nt** at the built
allowance, i.e. p8064 with 54 spare — **and it re-opens decision 8**), or the **zero-loop turn**
(**6 330 nt**, M13 with 919 spare, same `39.44 nm`, and **never folded**: `0` of `186` unique records
over `30` queries, `7 of 7` built cross-sections doing the other thing).

**The price line is left explicitly open, and the document says so in as many words.** `CLAUDE.md`
records that when the first six decisions were offered as yes/no, **all three with a cost dimension
came back in a shape the document had not offered** — a price, a contingency on another decision, and
an answer volunteered inside a different question. Decision 9 states that a price, a contingency or
*"the 40 is a nominal and here is its tolerance"* are all answers it is asking for.
