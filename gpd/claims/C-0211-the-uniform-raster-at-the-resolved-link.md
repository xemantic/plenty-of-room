## The claim, in four lines

`C-0207` found route B's uniform raster flat at `756 of 756`, and graded it at a numerical
**penalty**, `39.2452209×` above every rung a crossover connector can supply.

`C-0208` resolved the link by the bond's own direction and left route B carried, not resolved,
with `C-0205` §4's **adverse** direction standing over it: `0 of 16` coupled readings flat, the
`p90` rising as the link softens.

**Re-graded, the count does not move.** `756 of 756` at the resolved ceiling, at the resolved
floor, and at a fourth rung softer than either — `3024 of 3024` over the whole ladder.

The link is worth **`1.00100801–1.02460822×`** of the reading, and a solve-free cheap bound said
so before anything was graded.

## 9. The mutation test, and its three survivors were three real gaps — two of them the same one

`C-0161`'s standard on a Kotlin subject: **sixteen mutations, every one of which must fail a
NAMED test**, with the unmutated copy run first and its failures subtracted (`CH-0237`),
`find src -name '<file>.kt'` asserted to return **exactly one** path (`C-0190`), every anchor
asserted to occur exactly once (`C-0185`), and the `-x` flags **derived** from
`build.gradle.kts`'s own `dependsOn` block (`C-0194`).

**First run: 13 killed, 3 SURVIVED.** All three were real, and two of them were one lesson:

- **`M06` and `M07`** widen the rung's own `require`s. Both failed nothing, because
  `resolvedLinkStiffness` carries the **same two requirements** and every evaluation of a rung's
  two readings goes through it — `C-0207` §8's *a guard whose only observable behaviour is
  duplicated downstream is a guard no mutation of it can reach*, met on a second object. The
  repair is in the **fixture**: assert the exception's **message**, which differs by one word
  (`transverseLinkStiffness` against `transverseStiffness`), and add the `null`-radial case,
  which reaches no downstream call at all.
- **`M05`** removes the `null`-default's **identity** branch. It failed nothing because at every
  constant this study uses the arithmetic rounds back: `k·unitZ² + k·unitY²` is `k` **exactly** at
  `10000`, at `254.808095`, at `754.005141` and at `41.4338953`. It is **not** `k` at `k = 1`,
  where `unitY² + unitZ² = 0.9999999999999999` survives the multiplication — so the repair is a
  limiting case at the unit link, which is `C-0208`'s own stated reason for the branch made
  observable.

**After them: 16 mutations, `0` survivors over a subtracted baseline of `0`.** And the harness
reported `M05`'s first form as `SURVIVED (exit 1)` — a mutation that **does not compile** is a
survivor with a non-zero exit and no killer, which the exit code in the row is what distinguishes
from a real one.
