## The claim, in four lines

The caDNAno paper says the `10 × 6` block pairs 98 of every helix's 126 scaffold bases.
The deposited design file draws staples on all 126.

The supplementary staple **order** is `5 880` nucleotides in `144` strands, which is `60 × 98` exactly,
and the `70` strands the file draws and the order omits total `60 × 28` exactly and lie in the helix ends.

**A design file is a drawing and a staple table is a purchase, and where they disagree the purchase is what folded.**

---

## 1. The cheap bound ran first, and it is what made the discriminator binary

Before any PDF was opened, four integers out of the deposited `Nature09/01-monolith/monolith.json`:
**`214`** linear staple strands, **`7 560`** staple nucleotides, staple coverage spanning **`126`** bases
on every one of the 60 helices, and zero insertions and zero deletions.
`60 × 126 = 7 560` exactly, so the file admits the `7 560` reading and nothing else — which is what
makes `CH-0251`'s one-column discriminator a genuine binary rather than a hope.

The second cheap bound is `CLAUDE.md`'s own, and it paid a **sixth** recorded time:
**both decisive artifacts were already on disk.**
`T-296` retained `douglas2009-SI.pdf` and `T-255` the three gallery archives. **Nothing was refetched.**

## 2. The trap the queue row budgeted for does not apply, and one command says so

`CH-0251`, the `T-302` queue row and `T-296`'s own manifest all record this SI as having
*"no usable text layer — `pdftotext` returns 58 MB of `!"#$` glyphs"*, and budget the task as a
26-page raster transcription.

That is true of **pages 1–11**, the strand-diagram figures: their embedded TrueType subsets carry
**no `/ToUnicode` map and no `post` table**, so a reader gets the subset's own code points.
It is **false of pages 12–23**, which carry the twelve staple sequence tables and are typeset normally.

**The check is one command** — `pdftotext -bbox -f 12 -l 26` — and it turned a transcription into a
geometric read whose every row is falsifiable by the table's own redundancy.
**A blanket statement about a PDF's text layer is a statement about the pages somebody looked at.**

The read itself uses no font and no regular expression over content: each table's column abscissae are taken
from its **own header row** (`Start`, `End`, the title, `Length`, `Color`) — the twelve tables sit at five
different `Length` abscissae, so guessing one would have failed — words are grouped by `yMin`, and the
sequence is the line below.

## 3. The column, counted

| | the Methods sentence | the deposited file | **the supplementary ORDER** |
|---|---|---|---|
| scaffold allotted per helix | **126** | **126** | — |
| paired with staples, per helix | **98** | 126 | **`98`, on all 60** |

`sum(Length) = 5 880`. `5 880 = 60 × 98`. `144 + 70 = 214` and `5 880 + 1 680 = 7 560`.

**The residue is located, not merely computed.** The 70 strands the order omits are
`20 × 12 nt`, `20 × 24 nt` and `30 × 32 nt`, they lie **entirely** within the 28-base end regions of
their own helices, and under the order every helix is paired over the **identical** window `28..125`.

## 5. What the built object therefore is, to the base

- The **duplex** is in perfect register: **`98` bp on every one of the 60 helices, over `28..125`**.
  The 4 bp axial stagger the file draws lives **entirely** in the single-stranded ends.
- The 28 unpaired bases per helix split **`12` at one end and `16` at the other**, alternating —
  **not `14 / 14`**.
- Walked, the scaffold path is `7 560` bases of which **`5 880`** lie inside a duplex window; the
  **`60`** single-stranded gaps between consecutive duplex ends are **`24` nt at thirty turns and `32` at
  the other thirty**, because the stagger puts two `12`-base ends together at one rim and two `16`-base
  ends at the other. Their mean is **`28.0` exactly**.
  Contour: **`15.6–16.8 nm`** and **`20.8–22.4 nm`** at `0.65–0.70 nm/nt`.
- Of the file's **`118`** scaffold crossings, **`58`** sit inside a duplex window and **`60`** sit in
  single-stranded scaffold.

## 10. What this does NOT establish

- **Whether the object folded as ordered.** A staple order is a purchase. What is settled is what was
  bought, and it is bought at the resolution of a sequence table.
- **The other six cross-sections**, per §8.
- **Why** the design file carries end staples that were never ordered. The file is the record of a
  drawing and the table the record of a purchase, and nothing here says which came first, or whether
  the 20 twelve-nucleotide strands were drawn, priced and dropped or never intended.
- **Any energy, stiffness or flatness number.** This result is a count. What it does is fix **which
  design** `C-0175` §9, `C-0180` §4 and `C-0190` are about, and the answer is the one `C-0193` gave:
  a drawable design nobody has folded.
- **`C-0193` §4's purpose attribution.** What is confirmed is the mechanical consequence — the residue
  condition cannot bind a turn with no base pairs at it. Why the authors spent the 28 nt is what
  `C-0193` §5 quotes them saying, and it is aggregation.

## 11. Two process findings, named rather than fixed

**(a) `REFUTED` is not in the challenge-status vocabulary, so a challenge closed with it reads
`UNKNOWN`.** `tools/trace-answers.py`'s `_CHALLENGE_CLOSED` knows `upheld`, `withdrawn`, `stands`,
`resolved`, `overturned`, `discharged`, `answered` and `struck`; a `**Status**` row saying only
*refuted* is read as neither open nor closed, and the `T-298` gate then reports the adjudicating
claim as unrecorded. `CH-0251`'s row is worded to carry `OVERTURNED` and `stands` as well, so the
gate passes — **which is a workaround and not a repair**, and it is exactly `CLAUDE.md`'s standing
rule met from the challenge side: *a new status word must be tested in BOTH senses the day it is
coined*. `T-302` did not edit the shared tool, by ownership.

**(b) A challenge file's header is not a fixed shape, and the gate's authority assumes it is.**
`CH-0251` was filed with its fields as bold **lines** rather than as a two-column table, so it had
**no `| **Status** |` row at all** and `challenge_status_of` returned `UNKNOWN` for it — for the
whole iteration between its filing and this claim. It is converted here, unmodified in content.
A gate keyed on a row cannot see a corpus that also writes the same field as a line, and nothing
counted how many challenge files are in which shape. Counted here — importing
`challenge_status_of` and running it over `gpd/challenges/*.md` in this commit's working tree —
the census is **`58` CLOSED, `131` OPEN and `33` UNKNOWN of `222`**, and **`31` of the `33` carry no
`Status` row at all**. `UNKNOWN` is the safe direction for `stale_challenge_statuses`, which must
not report a live challenge closed; what it costs is that the `T-298` arm can never **confirm** an
adjudication on those 31, so a claim that settles one of them is reported as unrecorded for as long
as the file keeps its shape. **Named here and not repaired**: the fix is one alternative in a
shared tool this task does not own, and it wants its own mutation row in both directions.
