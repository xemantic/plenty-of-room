## 2. What moved, and why it is not a value substitution

### (a) The turn prestrain's sign — five passages, and the ground is what moved

`C-0186` carried `C-0180`'s *"the recovery rests on a sign nothing in this repository fixes … exactly
`1` of `64` is flat at each sign"* into both documents, correctly, naming `T-284` as open.
[`C-0187`](C-0187-the-turn-prestrain-sign-is-derived.md) then **derived** the sign: caDNAno's `±5 bp`
is an integer approximation to a `5.25 bp` half turn, so which side of its own staple position a
crossover sits on fixes the departure's sign, and `C-0148`'s closure condition pins **58 of the 59**
binaries — strictly alternating, **30** turns at the high rim and **29** at the low, reproducing
`C-0175` §8's *by the rim* free tile at **`8.9e−10`**.

So the sentence is false in its **ground** and superseded in its **value**: `C-0180`'s two *uniform*
readings are both off-lattice, and what survives is one **global phase** at which **`1` of `64`** is
flat at each phase, **`0` of `64` at both**, and **`2` of `64`** are phase-contingent. A cell escapes
only with a zero-prestrain 90th percentile below **`0.0979624378`**, against a corpus best of
**`0.0995744767`**, at which **0 of 64 qualify**.

And [`CH-0240`](../challenges/CH-0240-the-allowed-departure-is-common-mode.md) disputes the
**coordinate** without disputing a number: a level displacement rotates both backbones the same way,
so the departure has coefficient **exactly zero** on the relative-roll coordinate the load is applied
through. That is carried into both documents as a raised challenge with its replacement named
(`T-291`), not as a withdrawal, because no number of `C-0175` or `C-0180` is disputed.

**Three further passages carried `2 of 64` as a bare count** in a summary cell. The count has not
moved — it is the zero-prestrain reading, which `C-0187` reproduces — so those are annotated as
`SCOPE` rather than struck: what they were missing is the state they are read at.
