**Every reproduction is a tile that was already flat.** Where the uncoupled tile is inside the
tolerance a coupling can only spend flatness; where it is outside, it has room to buy some. That
is not a conjecture — it is `CLAUDE.md`'s own *an attachment coupling can be a NET DISHING SOURCE,
and the sign flips at an attachment pitch of one Winkler bending length*, read on the **tile**
instead of on the pitch.

## 3. And the exception is reported at its true weight, which is thin

One cell of 256 — *untied, `f = 0.26`, determined station lattice, `5 × 15 = 75` paths,
rim-graded 5:1* — reads `0.163948801` against its own uncoupled tile's `0.164599297`. That is
**0.395 %**, on a 90th percentile of 4 000 draws, and this challenge does **not** rest on it: the
study's own convergence departure at the deciding cell is `2.2e−4`, but a `p90`'s **sampling**
scatter at that sample size is larger than four parts in a thousand. It is a **near** miss, and
its interest is that the regularity is at the edge of resolvability on the first tile that is not
flat, not that it is broken.

**And it changes nothing.** The exception is not flat — `0.163948801` against a tolerance of
`0.10` — so no verdict anywhere moves.

## 4. What is NOT challenged

- **`C-0109`, and every reproduction of it.** All four are correct on the object they were taken
  on, and `C-0109`'s own sentence carries the clause.
- Any flatness verdict, at either cross-section, in either dishing convention.
- `C-0017`'s mandate. The coupling is a **placement and stability** requirement and an uncoupled
  tile delivers no output at all (`CH-0272`), so *"the uncoupled tile is flatter"* was never an
  argument for removing it and is not one here.

## 5. What would settle it

The **clause**, restored wherever the sentence is quoted without it — three words in four places.
And, if anyone wants the exception resolved rather than reported, the ensemble at the one cell run
at a sample size that resolves `0.395 %` of a `p90`, which is a statistical question and not a
lattice one. The queue row is `T-331`.
