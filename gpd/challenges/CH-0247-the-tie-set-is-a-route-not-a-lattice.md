## The ground

`C-0175` §9 adds 59 covalent ties to `C-0154`'s honeycomb grillage on the stated ground that a
raster turn *"is a covalent crossover like any other"*, and `CH-0227` files that as a defect **of
the lattice** — *"the honeycomb lattice omits the raster's own turn ties"*. A lattice is a
property of the object; a routing is a choice.

`C-0193` reads the built `10 × 6` block's own strand diagram (*Nature* **459**:414, Supplementary
Figure S4 — the monolith, whose cross-section inset is ten rows of six helices, and which the
caDNAno paper names as *"`10 × 6` (analyzed independently in ref. 14)"*). Off its own base-pair
axis:

- the **scaffold** occupies bases **14 → 140** of every helix — **126**, the paper's own allotment;
- the **staples** occupy **28 → 126** — **98**, the paper's own paired count;
- the scaffold **turns at the rim with no topological loopout**, at base 14 and base 140;
- therefore the **14 bases nearest each turn carry no staple**, and the two **DUPLEX** ends a turn
  joins are **28** unpaired nucleotides apart, `14` on each side.

`98 + 28 = 126`, and `60 × 126 = 7 560` — the scaffold the paper names, exactly.

## Item 1 — the element between two rim nodes is ssDNA, not a bond

`C-0175`'s tie is a covalent element between two **duplex** ends at `s = ±L/2`. In the only folded
instance of this cross-section the covalent link sits **`14 bp = 4.76 nm` outboard** of the duplex
end on **each** of the two helices it joins, and what stands between the two rim nodes is
**28 nucleotides of single-stranded scaffold** — `C-0147`'s tether, whose position this reading
supplies.

`C-0175` **declared** its modelling choice (*"a turn carrying **zero** unpaired nucleotides"*), so
this is a scope defect and not an error. `CH-0227`'s title and Status sentence do not carry the
qualifier, and neither does `C-0175`'s headline `1.12×`.

## Item 2 — and it is a purchase, which is what makes it a fork rather than a tolerance

**An unpaired base has no azimuth**, so caDNAno's `±5 bp` residue condition cannot bind a turn
flanked by 14 unpaired nucleotides at all. That is how a lattice carrying **both** turn senses is
folded at **one** uniform row length — and it is exactly why `C-0151`'s zero-loop raster has to be
**two-length**. The built design **buys** freedom from the residue condition and **pays** 28 nt of
scaffold; route A pays no scaffold and closes the condition instead. Two currencies, one choice.

## Item 3 — the two designs are separated by the scaffold, and the corpus has graded one

On `C-0151`'s drawable `102 / 109` raster the paired total is **6 330 nt**. At the built allowance
the block needs **8 010**: M13mp18 is short by **761**, p7560 by **450**, and p8064 has **54**
spare. Inverted, M13 affords **15 nt** per turn, p7560 **20**, p8064 **exactly the built 28**.

So both routes exist on real scaffolds, and every mechanical number the corpus carries for this
block is taken on **one** of them.

## What this does NOT claim

- **No number is disputed.** `C-0175` §9's `0.0501417316 / 0.0446459684 / 0.890395426`, `C-0180`
  §4's `2 of 64`, `CH-0228`'s `0.0764244991` and `C-0190`'s `0.296735462` all reproduce and are
  all correct about route A.
- **It does not say route A is unbuildable.** `C-0193` establishes the opposite: the turn is
  reachable at `+1.50 σ` of the measured phosphodiester step, the lattice rule closes with zero
  forced crossovers, and this repository's own committed `.sc` draws it with **zero** loopouts.
- **It does not say the tether is cheaper.** At M13's 15 nt affordance route B's 59 turns store
  `59.5057061–87.5402845 k_BT`, `7.15756436–10.5296662×` `C-0190`'s whole route-A ceiling.

## What would settle it

**Either** one published honeycomb-lattice origami whose raster turns carry no unpaired scaffold —
which makes route A the built precedent and closes this outright —
**or** the re-grade `T-299` opens: the same 64 coupled cells on a lattice carrying a 28 nt
entropic tether at each of the 59 rim turns instead of a covalent tie, which no lattice in this
repository has.

Until one of those, the honest statement is `C-0193`'s: **the corpus has graded one of two
designs, and it is the one nobody has folded.**
