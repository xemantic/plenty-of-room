## The ground, and where it went

`C-0126` classifies `T-142`, `T-143`, `T-174`, `T-176`, `T-177`, `T-179`, `T-180` and `T-185` as
CONTINGENT — well posed only if the tile stays single-layer — and the reason it gives is that a
four-layer tile **needs no coupling**, so optimising one is work on a body §3's own row contradicts.

[`C-0141`](../claims/C-0141-honeycomb-station-lattice-and-placement.md) re-solves the four-layer tile
at a cross-section that is actually a honeycomb:

| | `C-0126`'s ground | corrected | `T-5b`'s 0.10 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `15 × 4` free tile | 0.0577199433 | **0.0978155002** | inside, barely |
| `15 × 4` at the measured band's low end, `f` = 0.26 | 0.0612595739 | **0.101759944** | **OUTSIDE — FAILS** |
| `10 × 6` free tile | 0.00874363524 | **0.0240648102** | inside |
| `10 × 6` at `f` = 0.26 | 0.00927188486 | **0.0255589305** | inside |

**So *"the thicker tile does not need a coupling"* is a statement about `10 × 6`.** On `15 × 4` — the
cross-section every four-layer claim in this repository is written on, and the one `DECISIONS-FOR-NDI.md`
§7 offers NDI as the default — the uncoupled tile is **not** flat across the measured interlayer-coupling
band, so a coupling placement is back on the table and the eight tasks are back with it.

## Why this is a REPRICE and not a reclassification

The eight are still CONTINGENT, because the condition `C-0126` names — §3's thickness row, decision 7
unanswered — is unchanged and still unruled. What moved is the **value** of the contingency:

- if the tile stays **single-layer**, all eight are live exactly as `C-0126` says;
- if the tile becomes four-layer **`10 × 6`**, `C-0126`'s ground holds and they stay contingent;
- if the tile becomes four-layer **`15 × 4`**, `C-0126`'s ground **fails at the band's adverse end**
  and the eight acquire a second reason to be run — on a lattice none of them is written for.

**A classification is a quantity like any other and must be quoted with the state it is read at.**
`C-0126` reads its ground at one cross-section and states it of *"the four-layer tile"*.

## What would settle it

- **`T-232`** — re-grading `C-0118`'s sixteen coupled cells at the corrected cross-section. If a
  coupled `15 × 4` is flat under the measured dropout, the ground is repaired by a coupling rather
  than by a cross-section, and the eight become *more* relevant, not less.
- **NDI's answer to decision 7**, which selects the cross-section and therefore selects which of the
  three readings above applies.
