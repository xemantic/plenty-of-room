**Two claims of one iteration, and the synthesis quoted the challenge rather than the claim that
had answered it.** Neither author is at fault in the way an error is: `C-0148` was filed by a
different agent, and a synthesis reads the corpus for the tokens its task names. What the corpus
lacked is a sweep of *challenges whose status changed inside the same iteration as the pass*.

## 2. Why a question is worse than a number

`C-0067`'s finding is that a deliverable that under-claims is as wrong as one that over-claims.
This is the third variant: a deliverable that **over-prices a question**. §7's own history is the
precedent — a specification *trade* that turned out not to exist — and `C-0144` had to withdraw the
question as posed. Here the question does exist and its **cost dimension** does not.

That matters because of what `CLAUDE.md` records about how NDI answers: *"a question whose
admissible answers you enumerate cannot return the one you did not enumerate, and the missing shape
is a PRICE"*, and *"all three with a cost dimension came back in a shape the document had not
offered"*. **Decision 8 was asked with an explicit price line** — deliberately, and correctly. A
respondent weighing *"is it worth six flat cells of eight against three"* is weighing a quantity
that no longer exists, and the answer that comes back is priced against it.

## 3. What the question is worth now, which is nothing measurable

At the drawable `102 / 109` raster the interface window is **102 bp** and the crossover-column count
is **10** at all three `EDGE_MARGIN` conventions — 0.05, 0.17 and 0.34 nm, slack
**2.45 / 2.21 / 1.87 nm** — so the numerical guard decides nothing and **no flatness cell turns on
the width convention at all** (`C-0151` §4). The two readings still differ, by the raster's
**7 bp = 2.38 nm** stagger rather than 4 bp = 1.36 nm, and no plate or grillage model here can
represent either. **Decision 8 is therefore a drafting question with no margin on either side of
it**, which is a simplification of it and not a withdrawal.

## 4. What is upheld

- **The question.** §3 states one footprint without a tolerance — the tilde in that row is on the
  test tiles — and a two-length raster has two true widths. `C-0149`'s `F1`, `F2` and `F3` all still
  read the way they were recorded.
- **`C-0149`'s framing**, including its decision to leave the price line open and its withdrawal of
  the *"a one-sentence answer is enough"* sentence.
- **`CH-0185` itself**, as a correct statement about the bounding box of `112 / 108`. It is
  answered, not wrong.

## 5. What would settle it, and it is a standing check rather than an edit

The edit is made. What is not made is the check: **a synthesis pass should re-read the STATUS of
every challenge it cites, in the range since the last pass**, exactly as `C-0080` established for
task status and `C-0088` mechanised. `tools/trace-answers.py` already parses challenge statuses —
it reports *"172 challenge(s), 137 with a declared status, 0 open assertion(s) contradicted"* — so
what is missing is the arm that fires when a deliverable **rests a number on** a challenge the
corpus has since answered, rather than merely asserting the challenge is open. Queued as `T-261`.
