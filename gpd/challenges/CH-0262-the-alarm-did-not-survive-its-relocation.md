## What is wrong with it

**The span reading is right.** `C-0207` re-derives the whole census turn for turn and reproduces
`T-304`'s committed `uniformRasters` at all **63** `(width, phase)` rows — `closes`,
`distinctSpanCount`, `minimumSpan`, `maximumSpan`, `meanSpan` and `turnsInsideTheAlignedHalf` —
at a worst departure of `4.8e−9` nm. The worst turn does reach `4.35327572 nm`, which is
`C-0147`'s `d + 2r_P` exactly.

**The inference does not follow, and the study that could have said so was never run.** Graded
with the tethers that span distribution implies, over all 21 phases, three widths and twelve
chain corners:

| | |
|---|---|
| free tile, with the preload | **`0.0483790868–0.0946863482`** of the stroke |
| flat against `T-5b`'s `0.10` | **`756 of 756`** cells |
| `C-0201` §7's untied reading of the same three widths | `0.0425678289 / 0.0422200543 / 0.0451172785`, reproduced at `8.6e−10` |
| the preload's own worth | `0.00361034942–0.0502869583` |

**Three things separate the span from the response, and each is measurable.**

1. **The tile is smaller.** `C-0201`'s `0.11296458` is read on the **116 bp** block extent; these
   rows are `92 / 98 / 106 bp`, whose untied dishing is already `0.0422200543–0.0451172785`
   against the 116 bp tile's `0.0501417316`.
2. **The distribution is not the worst member.** `C-0201`'s bracket end is the worst span at
   **all 59** turns; a uniform raster reaches it at **some**, and the mean chain tension is
   `3.14109619–4.49761906×` the determined raster's where the worst turn is `6.64777142×`.
3. **The sum is signed, and a third of it has no coordinate.** The preload enters as `f·unitZ` on
   the link gradient, so the nine **in-plane** turns of each width contribute **exactly zero** —
   `27 of 177` bank columns are `0.0` — and the through-thickness ones carry two opposite `unitY`
   azimuths. The `C-0104` triangle-inequality ceiling built from the 59 unit-tension influences —
   rigorous on the zero-stiffness lattice the bank spans, and **checked** rather than assumed
   against the reading that carries the tether's own stiffness — is honoured at `756 of 756` cells
   and is **loose by `1.949–5.438×`** for exactly that reason.

## Why it was reasonable, and what the general rule is

`C-0204` did the right thing with the information it had: it found a quantity of its own study at
an adverse bracket end and **named** the follow-up rather than assuming the answer, which is why
`F7` was declared **open** in advance and why `T-307` exists at all. What it could not do without
a solve was cross the step from a **level** — a span, a tension, an alignment count — to a
**field**, and this corpus already records the same step failing twice in the other direction:
*an energy ceiling is not a field* (`C-0187`, two channels agreeing to 2.0 % per site moving a
coupled cell `226.780027×` differently), and *a ratio of a correction to a leading term is an
error bar on the thing that term IS* (`CH-0167`). **A span reaching a bracket end is evidence
about the span.**
