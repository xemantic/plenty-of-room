## The defect

The tile this programme recommends is **route B's** — a uniform honeycomb raster whose turns are
flexible tethers of unpaired scaffold, the only turn topology anybody has folded (`C-0193`,
`C-0200`) — and its buildable paired rows are **`92 / 98 / 106 bp`**.

Every coupled cell in this repository is graded at **`116 bp`**, route A's block extent.
Three things a coupled census stands on are functions of the row length, and all three were
inherited:

- the **bond census**, `358 / 385 / 410` against 435, and its in-plane share moves too (`CH-0270`);
- the **station ladder**, `phase + 21k ≤ L` — route B's rows carry **5** station columns where the
  block extent carries **6**, *at every one of the 21 phases*;
- the **dropout field**, `measuredDepthIncorporation(edgeX, edgeY)`, because `edgeX` is
  `31.28 / 33.32 / 36.04 nm` and not `39.44` — `CLAUDE.md`: *a boundary-layer measurement does not
  transfer between two tile sizes.*

And the **phase** itself was inherited. `C-0141` fixes the ladder phase by caDNAno's `±5 bp` rule
only where the raster **closes**; route B's uniform rows close at **no** phase, so the phase is a
free design variable there — and `T-316`'s `16` is a route-A number that **refuses a 5-column
placement at `92` and `98 bp`**, the column count carrying 12 of `C-0215`'s 27 flat cells.

## What it is worth, measured

`C-0215` grades the same four placements and the same four column counts on route B's own tile and
pairs each cell against `C-0212`'s:

| | |
|---|---|
| paired cells | 48 |
| the flat verdict **moves** | **10** |
| route B's searched `p90` over the block's | **`0.839625798`–`1.34239093×`** |
| flat on the `116 bp` block | 33 of 48 |
| flat on route B | 27 of 48 |

So the substitution is worth up to **`1.34×`** on the deciding statistic, it moves the verdict at a
fifth of the cells, and it moves it in **both** directions.

## What is asked

Nothing is withdrawn. What is asked is that a coupled count in this corpus travel with the **row
length** it was read at, exactly as a stiffness travels with its compression — and that any future
coupled census on route B derive its station set rather than transfer one. `C-0215` does both, and
this challenge is the record of why it had to.
