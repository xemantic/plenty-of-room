## The reading, and the state it is read at

`C-0207`'s conditions name `k_link = 1e4 pN/nm` explicitly, which is
`HoneycombGrillage.RIGID_LINK_STIFFNESS` — a **penalty**, whose whole purpose is that the answer
must not depend on it (`CLAUDE.md`: *scaling a penalty is a step, not a ramp*).

`C-0205` established that it does depend on it, at the coupled level: the largest link stiffness a
crossover connector can supply is `254.808095 pN/nm`, `39.2452209×` (`10000 / 254.808095`) below the penalty, and
`C-0180`'s `2 of 64` becomes `0 of 64` there. `C-0208` resolves the link by the bond's own
direction and the count is still `0 of 64` at every rung of the physical bracket.

`C-0207`'s lattice is the **same lattice**. Its 59 raster turns are tethers rather than ties, and
its **435 staple bonds** are unchanged — they carry `linkStiffness` exactly as route A's do. So
`756 of 756` is a verdict on a lattice `39×` stiffer in its links than anything the corpus can
justify.

## The direction is already measured, and it is adverse

`C-0205` §4, read out of `C-0201`'s own committed `linkStiffness` block and not re-run:
**`0 of 16`** tethered readings are flat at the 90th percentile, over four decades of link
stiffness and four cells, and *"the `p90` **rises** as the link softens at every one — so
extrapolating below the swept range cannot help either"*.

Route B's uniform rasters are a different cell set from `C-0201`'s determined one, so that is a
**direction** and not a verdict. But it is the direction that matters, and `C-0207` states its own
band with no link stiffness attached to it anywhere but the conditions row.

## What this challenge does NOT say

It does not say `C-0207`'s verdict reverses. It is not re-graded here, deliberately: that is a
study with 756 cells and it is `T-315`, not a footnote. `C-0207`'s span census, its `8.6e-10`
reproduction of `C-0201` §7, its phase finding and its `F6` firing are all untouched.

It does not challenge `C-0201`, whose own sweep is what supplies the direction.

## What would falsify this challenge

Re-grading route B's three uniform widths at the resolved per-bond link — `254.808095 pN/nm` in
plane and `629.20588`–`1365.32644` through the thickness — and finding the count still
`756 of 756`. That is `T-315`, and it is the only thing that can settle it.
