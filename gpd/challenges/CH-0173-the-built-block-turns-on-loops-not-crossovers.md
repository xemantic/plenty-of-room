## The ground

The caDNAno paper states its own per-helix scaffold accounting:

> *"Each helix was allotted **126 bases** of scaffold. Of those 126 bases, **98 were paired** with
> complementary staples, and the remaining **28 bases** were divided into **front and rear unpaired
> loop fragments at the ends of each helix**."*

and the TEM criterion names the loops as a visible feature of the folded objects —
*"more than 3 nm away from the **unpaired scaffold loops at the front and rear interfaces**"*.

**So every raster turn of the built blocks passes through `14 + 14 = 28` unpaired nucleotides.** A
turn through a single-stranded loop is not an antiparallel crossover and the `7k ± 5` residue
condition does not bind it at all — which is exactly how a block whose turn sense alternates
(`CH-0172`) can be built with **all sixty helices the same length**. The two findings are one
another's explanation.

## Item 1 — the budget has no line for it

| | `C-0109`/`C-0119` as written | with the built allowance |
|---|---|---|
| paired per helix | 112 bp | 112 bp |
| unpaired per helix | **0** | **28 nt** |
| 60 helices | 6 720 nt | **8 400 nt** |
| M13mp18, 7 249 nt | 529 spare, **92.7 %** | **1 151 nt SHORT** |
| p8064, 8 064 nt | 1 344 spare | **336 nt SHORT** |

At the built allowance the widest four-layer honeycomb tile is **92 bp = 31.28 nm** from M13
(`−21.80 %` of §3's nominal) and **106 bp = 36.04 nm** from p8064 (`−9.90 %`). `C-0119`'s uniform
112 bp × 60 is out of reach of **both** scaffolds once the loops are paid for.

**This is evidence, not a requirement.** 28 nt per helix is the design's own choice and no
measurement here says what the minimum is; `CH-0172`'s two-length raster needs **zero** loop and
fits M13 with 653 nt spare. What the item removes is the reading that a 92.7 % paired occupancy is
a comfortable budget — the one folded instance of this cross-section spends **22.2 %** of its
scaffold on turns.

## Item 2 — design (i) is folded from p7560, not p8064

The paper: *"The shapes were folded either from a **7560**-base scaffold into **60** parallel
helices or from an **8064**-base scaffold into **64** parallel helices"*, and each helix is allotted
126 bases. Then

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;`60 × 126 = 7 560` and `64 × 126 = 8 064`, **both exact**.

`15 × 4` is **60** helices, so design (i) is a **p7560** design. The 64-helix designs are the two
`C-0119` itself identifies as 64 — **(iii) `8 × 8`** and **(v) `4 × 16`**. `C-0119`'s line
*"p8064 — designs i, iii, v"* therefore mis-assigns design (i), and the consequence it draws —
*"the remainder is 1 344 nt rather than 529"* — is wrong twice over: on p7560 the built design's
remainder is **exactly zero**, because the allotment spends the scaffold to the last nucleotide.
